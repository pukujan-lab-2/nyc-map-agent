Real MTA notices from tonight and this weekend. Tap one in the panel: it reads itself out and plays on the map. The voice here is your browser's; the app would use Kokoro.

What's different

  • Four time buttons on one row. The one for the current New York time is already picked. Day and Night are weekdays; weekend nights run Friday to Sunday night.
  • Only what applies then. The Burnside Av skip disappears this weekend because the MTA lifts it; two overlapping downtown 4 notices become one.
  • Tap tells everything. What changed, how to reach a skipped stop, how to leave one, when nothing changes for you, and when it ends. No Full notice button; the MTA's own words sit behind (i).
  • Pick your stop or where you're going. The walk names the exact station to stay on to and how many stops to ride back.
  • The map plays it. The train runs the line, stops where it stops and flies past skipped stations; you change trains, walk, and ride the Bx1 to the right corner.
  • Live problems first, with the MTA's updates merged into one story.
