# Issue #191: Compare mode: 3D street view next to the camera or a Mapillary photo

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-21
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/191

**Problem.** There is no way to check how well our 3D street view matches the real street. Alex asked for "a camera mode to compare live view to the 3D street mode".

**What is wanted.** A labeled "Compare" button in street view opens a slider split at the same position and heading: our 3D street view on one side, the real world on the other. Real world = the rear camera (phone, only after tapping "Use camera") or the nearest Mapillary photo facing that way (desktop / no camera). Turning in the 3D side turns the photo. Camera frames never leave the device; "Stop camera" stops every track, as do tab hide and leaving.

Phase 2 of 3 of Live View (phase 1: #188).
