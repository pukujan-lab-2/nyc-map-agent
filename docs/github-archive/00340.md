# PR #340: fix(map): keep the follow card and Lines clear on a phone

- State: closed (2026-10-25)

- Branch `fix/phone-follow-layout` into `main`, +151 −14 in 6 files

On a phone, following a train put the follow card under Layers & filters, Transit on, and the Layers button. Lines sat alone on the next row, on top of the train, so the card could not be read and Lines was not with the other follow actions.

The follow card now sits below the top bar. On a phone its height is reserved, so the Layers button and the open menu start underneath the card instead of covering it. The menu scrolls if it would otherwise run over the camera row. On a wide screen the card stays below the search bar and clear of the Layers menu.

Chase, Side, Overhead, Cab, X-ray, Stop following, and Lines stay on one row. When that row is wider than the screen it zooms down together, instead of wrapping Lines onto a row of its own.

Checked at a 390px phone width and a 1280px window, following a bus with Layers open. The card text, including the grade, does not meet the top bar, the Layers button, or the menu. Lines shares a row with Chase and Stop following. The same card on the wide window is fully readable. That check followed a bus, so Cab was not on screen; Cab is in that same row and zooms with it.

The words on the card are unchanged. The rest of the phone chrome is unchanged. Underground line paint and the 3D layer are unchanged.

Closes #332.
