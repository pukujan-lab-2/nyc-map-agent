# PR #439: fix(street): restroom kiosks stand on the pavement, not inside the building

In street view, many restroom kiosks were drawn inside a library or park house. The city records the restroom at a point inside the building, and the kiosk was placed exactly there, so people saw it in the facade or not at all.

**What changes**
- `onPavement` in `src/lib/map/signPlacement.ts` finds the nearest street centre line. When the point is more than 9 metres from it, the helper moves the point straight toward that line until it is 7 metres away: the pavement in front, on the same side.
- Points already on the pavement are left alone, and so are points with no street within 45 metres.
- The 3D layer (`threeLayer.ts`) uses the moved spot for both drawing and tapping, so a tap on the kiosk opens it where it now stands.

**How to verify:** `npx vitest run src/lib/map/signPlacement.test.ts` covers a point inside a building, one already on the pavement, one far from any street, and one on the far side of the street. On the live site, walk past a library restroom in street view: the kiosk stands at the kerb.

**What stays the same:** the 2D restroom icons and the restroom list still use the city's point. A kiosk tapped in street view opens its details with directions to the spot where it now stands.
