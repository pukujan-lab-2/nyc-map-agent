# Issue #63: Street level: camera sinks underground (black lower half, pale band) when looking up/down

- State: closed (2026-10-21)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-21
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/63

Bed-Stuy (East) street mode: the bottom half renders black/navy with a pale band; camera seems stuck. Cause: the eye camera aimed at a point above/below ground, but MapLibre clamps the map centre to the ground, so the camera altitude went to −11 m (look up) or +16 m (look down).
