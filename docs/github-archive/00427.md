Tapping a landmark badge on the map did nothing, even though each landmark already had a Wikimedia photo in our data. There was also no way to save Home or Work, so My day kept saying "Save Home and Work to see them here" with no way to do it. This covers the small, finished parts of four open issues.

What changes:
- **Tap a landmark (#325).** A badge or name opens a sheet with the photo, the photographer and licence linked to Commons, and Directions from here. Tapping a hero model at street level gets the same photo when the names match.
- **Save Home and Work (#313).** Place sheets that know their name (landmarks, directories, storefronts, schools) offer Save as Home and Save as Work. Places are kept on this phone in the existing `nma.homeWork` key, and only while Home & Work is on in What we remember. My day's Your rides lists them, and one tap flies the map back. The filter-picking helper that #313 also asks for is not in this pull request.
- **Lines opens at half (#314).** Panels can say how high they open on a phone. Lines asks for half, so the list is readable at once. Every other panel still opens at the peek.
- **One place per switch (#372).** On the home screen, the Layers drawer no longer repeats Stations only, Bus stops, Street names, 3D buildings, Textured city or Eco mode. Those live in the menu row. With the home screen off, the drawer is unchanged.

How to check: on a 375×667 screen, open `/?at=-73.98566,40.74844` and tap the Empire State Building badge. The sheet shows the photo and credit. Save as Home, then My day → Your rides → "Home · Empire State Building" flies back. "My train" on the home screen opens Lines at half height. I checked all of this on a local build with the API proxied to the live site. New unit tests cover saving (including when Home & Work is switched off) and the landmark lookup.

What stays the same: restroom, parking and event sheets don't offer saving. Nothing leaves the phone.

Refs #313. Closes #314, closes #325, closes #372.
