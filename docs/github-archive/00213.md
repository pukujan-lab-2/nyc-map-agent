# Issue #213: Learn subway segment and dwell times from GTFS-rt history

- State: closed (2026-10-22)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-21
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/213

Live train motion only knows the current feed's ETAs, which are often padded schedule times for stops further out. Log observed segment travel times and station dwell times per route, direction and station pair into Octo, roll them up into median and p80 by hour of day and weekday (60 days of raw data kept), serve that through a typed /api route with an OpenAPI spec, and have the interpolator blend the historical time with the live ETA, falling back to schedule when there is no history.

Follows #208.
