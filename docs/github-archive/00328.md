# Issue #328: Trains sit at a station long after the stop should end

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-23
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/328

## Problem

A train that has finished its stop stays on the platform until the live feed removes that station from the trip. The wait is often most of the ride to the next station, so the train looks stuck.

## What is going wrong

The map will not let a train pass the first station the feed still lists, even after that station's departure time. Leaving the station only happens when a later feed snapshot drops the station.

A second copy of the 3D train can also stay where the train was when Follow starts. That copy is tracked on #319.

## Ask

After the normal stop (or the departure time the feed gives), move the train toward the next station. Still do not pass that next station early, and do not pull the train backward when a later arrival slips.

On main the model’s own estimates are 30 seconds at an ordinary stop, 50 seconds at a major complex, and 60 seconds where an express and a local meet or at a terminal. A realtime arrival and departure pair overrides those. The long sit is the hold: the train is pinned at the first stop the feed still lists until that stop drops off the feed, so it can stay for minutes. Pull request #329 lets it leave after the stop. It is not merged. These numbers were not changed.

**Pukujan commented, 2026-10-25**

On main, a train leaves after its dwell (30 s at an ordinary stop, 50 s at a big complex, 60 s at a terminal) even while the feed still lists the stop. It never passes the next listed stop early and never moves backward. See `src/features/transit/model.ts` and `monotonic.ts`, tested in `monotonic.test.ts`. Since #430, a train released after a hold catches up at up to 35 m/s instead of jumping. The one case still held is when the feed itself reports the train stopped at the station; that follows #316 item 5.
