# PR #494: fix(transit): tapping a line notice takes the map to its stations again

- State: merged (2026-10-26)

**Problem.** On a phone, tapping a notice in a subway line's sheet no longer took the map to the stations it is about (owner report). This broke when #484 moved the line panel into a pull-up sheet at the bottom. There were two causes, one per kind of notice:
1. **Elevator and escalator outages** moved the map only when the station was completely off the map. A station that was on the map but behind the sheet or at the screen edge was never brought into view. On the 2 line, the Times Sq-42 St ring stayed at the very top edge, half cut off.
2. **Service alerts** (skipped stops, detours) still framed the stretch for the old top panel. They reserved the top 45% of the screen while the sheet already covered the bottom, which left almost no room.

**What changes.**
- `src/features/transit/LinePanel.tsx`: tapping an outage notice always takes the map to its station(s). On a phone the stations are framed under the quick buttons and above the sheet. Whether the sheet is showing is now passed in, not guessed from the panel's position.
- `src/features/transit/TransitLayer.tsx`: on a phone with a line open, an alert's stretch is framed in the map space left above the sheet. The sheet is already a map cover, so the map's own padding clears it.
- Wide screens (side panel) are unchanged.

**How to verify.** Checked on a local build against live MTA data:
- 2 line, "Elevator out at Times Sq-42 St": at 375×667 with the sheet one step up, and at 320×568 fully up, the map zooms to the station with its "No elevator" ring above the sheet.
- 1 line, "downtown 1 skips 125 St, 116 St…": at 320×568 all four skipped stops show above the sheet.
- `pnpm -s test`, `tsc -b` and lint pass.

**Gap.** No end-to-end test covers line notices. The e2e mocks have no subway data or alerts, and adding those fixtures is the way to keep this from breaking again.
