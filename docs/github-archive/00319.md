# Issue #319: 3D trains flash, stick after Follow, and sit under the elevated track

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-23
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/319

On the tilted map the 3D train misbehaves in four ways.

1. The model flashes when nothing about the train changed.
2. It still shows the generic train model. With 3D buildings off, or with eco on, a route-coloured box (the same idea as the underground subway boxes) is enough. This is not a graphics quality ladder, and Top view stays flat.
3. Starting Follow leaves a 3D model where the train was.
4. An above-ground train is still drawn underground. The 7 at Flushing–Main St and the 3 at Harlem–148 St are on a viaduct, but the car is drawn under the street. A click from right above the car should land on the car. The station dot that currently takes that click is a MapLibre layer and stays where it is.

Top view must not gain 3D buildings or the train model as part of this fix.
