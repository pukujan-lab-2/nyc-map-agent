# PR #262: feat(transit): clickable alerts, direction-aware skips and a Lines drawer

- State: merged (2026-10-22)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-22
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/pull/262
- Branch `feat/alert-focus-lines` into `main`, +909 −72 in 21 files, merge commit 404d95488dc9c32b527fbc7cc097d18fb45cb56a

Riders could read an alert like "[F] is rerouted via Court Sq [E]…" but not see where it happens, and a station skipped in one direction looked the same as any other station. Closes #253.

**What changes**
- **Clickable alerts.** Every alert row (train sheet "Alerts and detours", Service status, Lines drawer) has a "Show on map" action. The map fits to the affected stretch, dims everything else, thickens the stretch, crosses out the skipped stations and draws the reroute over the via lines as a dashed line (the F over the E, then the A to Jay St). A slim card shows the text (full details fold out), active period, direction, via lines, affected stops and any platform change. Back (card button, panel arrow, Esc) restores the camera and reopens what was there before.
- **Direction-aware skips.** `src/lib/transit/serviceChanges.ts` reads "Flushing-bound [7] skips 52 St and 69 St" and "All trains at Woodside-61 St board from the Manhattan-bound platform". A one-direction skip gets a half-strike marker and "Skipped Flushing-bound · Manhattan-bound stops" in the station sheet; a both-ways skip gets the full cross. Platform changes show as a notice on the station sheet ("Flushing-bound trains board from the Manhattan-bound platform").
- **Trip and Ask.** The trip planner skips boarding or exiting at a station only in the skipped direction, and the enter and transfer steps carry the platform notice. The map's own trains need no change: the live GTFS-realtime trips already omit the skipped stops (Flushing-bound 7 trips list 712N but not 711N or 713N, while Manhattan-bound trips list all three), so the animation does not dwell there.
- **Lines drawer.** A labelled "Lines" button on the right edge opens the drawer in the panel slot from #225 (the Layers quick drawer from #251 isn't on main yet, so no entry there). It lists every subway line, the shuttles and SIR with a status badge (Good service, Delays, Detour, Planned work). Tap rows to show only those lines, their stations and their live trains. Each row expands to its alerts. "Show all" resets.
- **Server.** `/api/transit/alerts` adds the description, current active window, Mercury's human-readable period, direction, via lines, skipped names and the station changes. Existing fields are unchanged.

**Budget.** All JS is 943.97 kB of 945 kB. To make room I replaced date-fns' `format`/`subDays`/`subMonths` in `src/lib/windows.ts` with ten lines of plain Date code, which saved about 3 kB. The transit-layers chunk limit goes from 12 kB to 13.5 kB because the alert layers live there (main grew it a little too). date-fns is removed from package.json since nothing imports it now.

**How to verify**
- `pnpm test`: new tests cover both directions on the 7 (Flushing-bound skip vs Manhattan-bound skip), the Woodside platform notice, the F reroute path ending at Jay St, and the planner avoiding a station only in the skipped direction.
- Open Lines, expand F, tap the reroute alert, then Back. Search "69 St" to see the skip badge, and "61 St-Woodside" to see the platform notice.

**What stays the same.** I didn't touch the Layers drawer, Ask panel, view switcher, X-ray, live vehicle models, street-view signs or shadows. three.js and deck.gl aren't involved.
