## Problem

In street view, shop signboards vanish as you walk toward them. From a distance a shop's sign shows on its building; within roughly the last 50–100 m it disappears, so the shops right in front of you are the ones you can't see. Entering street view next to a shop showed none of its neighbours either.

## Cause

The shop signs are drawn by our three.js layer, which reads them from the city tiles with `querySourceFeatures` (a MapLibre call that reads raw tile data). The city tiles stop at zoom 16. At eye level, MapLibre loads the ground close to the camera at zoom 17–23. It builds those tiles by slicing the zoom-16 tile, and it keeps only the data layers (vector tile "source layers") that some map style layer uses (`familiesBySource` in MapLibre 6's `_getOverzoomTile`). No style layer used `storefronts`, so the near-camera tiles had none: 0 shop features in every zoom-17+ tile, measured in the running app at 7th Avenue and 34th Street. Farther ground still comes from zoom-16 tiles, which is why distant shops showed and near ones didn't.

The corner street-name `signs` layer is read the same way and had the same gap.

## What changes

`installCity3d` (`src/lib/map/city3d.ts`) adds one invisible circle layer (radius 0, opacity 0) for each of `storefronts` and `signs`. Nothing draws, but MapLibre now keeps those layers when it slices tiles.

## How to verify

Measured in a dev build at 1280×800 by placing the eye in front of TD Bank (7th Ave and W 34th St) and facing it:

| Distance | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 60 m | sign data present (zoom-16 tile) | present |
| 25 m | no sign, no shop front | TD Bank sign and shop front |
| 8 m | no sign, no shop front | TD Bank sign and shop front, fills the view |

Zoom-17 tiles now carry 54 shop features where they had 0. Checks: types, lint, unit tests (1,328 passed), size budgets (the 3D-layer budget is unchanged at 148.7 of 150 kB) and the street-view Playwright spec (`e2e/ask-street.spec.ts`) all pass. After deploy I'll recheck this on the live site at desktop and 375-wide phone sizes.

## What stays the same

How the signs look, their 200 m radius and 64-sign cap, the zoom-17 start, and every visible layer. No tile rebuild is needed.
