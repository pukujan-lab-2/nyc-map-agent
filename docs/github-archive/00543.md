**Problem.** With live buses on (for example `busLines=B62` around McCarren Park), the B62 route line and bus chips stayed on the map. The map itself had no way to turn them off; the switch was hidden in the Layers menu. In 3D views each bus was a blank white tapered box: the layer passed a fixed near-white colour (`#f8fafc`) for every bus, or used a white glTF model when 3D buildings were on.

**What changes.**
1. **Hide pill.** While live buses are on, a pill near the top of the map shows "Buses · B62" (up to three routes, then "+N") with **Hide ×**. Tapping Hide turns the bus layer off. That removes the route line, the chips and a followed bus, and clears `busLines`, `busNear` and `bus` from the URL. The pill uses the same placement rules as "Back to street view" (centred; it moves down below that button and below a docked line panel). It is hidden in street view, where the street controls sit at the top; the Layers menu still works there.
2. **Bus colours, same box.** The owner asked for "just the colors in the box". The bus keeps the tapered box outline the other vehicles use, now painted in MTA livery by route type:
   - Local: the 2016 navy scheme with a light-blue and yellow wave.
   - Select Bus Service (routes ending in "+"): white with the light-blue wrap below the windows.
   - Express (QM, BM, BxM, X, SIM): white with the straight blue stripe.

   Every bus has dark windows and a dark windshield, and the front strip of the roof is dark, so you can see from above which way it faces. Height and length segments exist only so the colour bands have edges to sit on; the outline is unchanged. Each livery is one instanced mesh. The white glTF bus is dropped, so its 68 KB download and Sketchfab credit go too. The flat top-down map still shows only the route chip.

   Colours follow Wikipedia's description of the liveries ([MTA Regional Bus Operations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTA_Regional_Bus_Operations), [Select Bus Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Select_Bus_Service)). Caveat: whether express coaches have all moved to the 2016 scheme was not confirmed, so express uses the older white-and-blue-stripe livery.

**How to verify.** Turn on Live buses in Greenpoint, tap **Hide**: the line, the chips and `busLines` in the URL go away. Tilt to 3D or follow a bus: B62 is navy, an M15+ is white with light blue, and an X27 is white with a blue stripe. Check at 375 px too.

**Unchanged.** Trains, the bus focus rules (#538) and the stop watch (#542).

Local checks: types, lint on the changed files, dependency boundaries, and the map, transit and component tests (new: the outline is unchanged, roof colour per livery, and route to livery).
