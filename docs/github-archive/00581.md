**Problem:** in street view, asking any question threw the camera to an overhead view while the street bar and mini map stayed up, and the station-entrance labels piled on top of each other. Reproduced on the live site. A stack trace showed the cause: each new question stops the trip preview, and the preview's `stop()` always eased the map to zoom 14 overhead, even when no preview was playing. The street labels are allowed to overlap while in street view (so the nearest stairs always show), so overhead they stacked.

**What changes**
- `stop()` in `usePlayer.ts` moves the camera only when a preview was actually active.
- Street-level entrance and station labels allow overlap only while the camera is at eye level (tilt ≥ 60°). Raised overhead in street view, they give way to each other and use the overhead entrance labels.

**How to verify:** the new browser test in `e2e/task-screens.spec.ts` enters street view, asks a question and checks that the camera stays tilted at eye level, the mini map stays, and the layout check passes. Runs in CI only (8 GB machine).

**Unchanged:** closing a playing trip preview still eases the map home.
