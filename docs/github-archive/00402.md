## What changes

2. **The restroom answer shows you and the restroom above the sheet.** On phones, the framing leaves room for the answer sheet and the right-hand quick bar.
3. **The trip header:**
   - shows the time as "4:05 PM";
   - shows weather in °C / °F, plus the rain chance when it is 40% or more;
   - shows the notice as plain words with a warning icon, without the "[N][R]" brackets;
   - opens the line the notice is about when tapped, preferring the line you ride.
4. **The walk card stops repeating itself.** It reads "Turn right" over "In 40 m · Goldsmith St · 6 min left", instead of "Turn right · Turn right".
5. **A new question clears the old answer's marks.** The restroom pin no longer stays on the map after you ask for a trip.
