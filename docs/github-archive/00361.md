# Issue #361: Agent-first and fast: track the realigned direction

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-24
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/361

On 2026-10-23 the owner realigned the project. The main goal is now an **accessible chat and voice agent that shows its answers on a clean, fast map**, for regular people getting around New York. Earlier work leaned toward visual detail and technical features; the map is also slow on phones and ordinary computers.

This issue tracks the work for that direction. The reasoning is written up in `PROJECT.md`, `docs/ROADMAP.md` and `docs/PERFORMANCE.md` on branch `docs/project-realignment` (not yet merged to `main`).

## Primary uses (owner's order)
Trip planning and simulation, restrooms nearby, landmarks to guide you, help underground and at exits, readable MTA disruptions, weather and traffic shown on the map. Secondary: questions about a place from the data (noise, crashes, a child's school route). Third: finding businesses and food.
