## What and why

On a phone the view controls wrapped into the compass and the layers list, and tapping a subway line opened a 92px peek instead of a drawer. Filter cards were translucent, and the search list could sit on top of the layers drawer. Bus stop dots had no off switch, and the Schools chip did not draw the school points already in the city tiles.

This keeps one Layers control. Phones use Layers & filters only. The view row sits inset from the compass and wraps upward instead of covering it. Opening search, the layers drawer, or the lines list closes the others. Lines opens at half height. Cards, the follow card, the walk controls, the status chip, and the ask panel are solid. Bus stops hides the selected-route stop dots and leaves the buses drawn. Schools turns on the existing city-tile school icons from zoom 14.
