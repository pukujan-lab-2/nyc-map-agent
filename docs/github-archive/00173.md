The default screen had too many controls at once (a left dashboard, a right rail of buttons and a chip row), which made the map hard to read on a phone. Alex picked Layout A from the mockups.

**What changes**
- Default screen: a 'Where to?' search with Ask, one labelled **Layers & filters** button and a small **Transit on** pill. The left dashboard and the right rail are gone.
- One drawer (a vaul bottom sheet on phones, a floating card on the left on desktop) headed 'Exploring <Neighborhood>', with a search box over every option, **Clear all** and **Show map**:
  - **Getting around:** Directions (the accent button), Live trains, Live buses, Transit Focus, Street view, My location / Stop sharing location.
  - **Nearby stations** with live arrivals.
  - **Layers:** Transit, Restrooms, Weather, Traffic, Parking, Schools and the other feature layers.
  - **Places** (hidden until chosen): Food, Coffee, Things to do, Shopping, Parks and Restrooms, each with subcategories, an All/None switch and Hide all places. Cuisine, price and opening hours are not in the open map data, so the drawer says so instead of faking them.
  - **Transit lines:** one chip per subway service (picked lines full strength, the rest faint), Stations only, and Show all lines.
  - **Map detail:** simple or detailed streets, street names, buildings, and a theme picker.
  - **Advanced:** filters and settings, data layers, textured 3D city, eco mode, face north, about the data.
- Themes are CSS variables: Ivory (the default; F2F1ED / 111111 / C7F602), Ink (dark), MTA muted and the optional Neon theme (deep indigo with violet and pink). Each theme also recolours the basemap's land, water and parks. Selected items are filled ink, there is one accent, and subway lines stay muted until you pick a line or turn on Transit Focus.
- Every choice is saved in the browser. There are no tooltips and no icon-only controls.

**How to verify**
Open the app at 390 and 1440 wide: only the search, Layers & filters and Transit on are visible. Open the drawer, pick Food > Restaurants and the restaurant icons appear; Clear all hides them again; Neon in Map detail switches the whole interface and basemap. Before and after screenshots at both widths, plus Neon and Ink, were taken over the browser debugging protocol and reviewed against the mockups.

**What stays the same**
No new dependency (vaul and Radix were already in use). Place cards, station cards, routes and the assistant still open in the same panel. Locally, all the checks CI runs pass: typecheck, 159 unit tests (8 new), lint, boundaries, knip, size, OpenAPI, licences and both end-to-end tests. Full project-continuity and content-generation checks pass too. The all-JS size budget goes from 927 kB to 932 kB: the drawer adds about 3 kB and replaces the rail. This branch builds on the readable transit map change in #172.
