When a shuttle bus replaces a suspended train line, the map's "Take the T426 bus here" label had no station marker and no station name, so it looked like it floated in the middle of nowhere.

## Human outcome

The map says where to change from the train to the bus: a ring marks the end station and the label reads "Transfer to the T426 bus at Broadway Junction".

## Before and after

**Before:** a text-only label at the end of the suspended stretch: "Take the T426 bus here", with nothing under it.

**After:** a ring marker on the station, and the label "Transfer to the T426 bus at <station>". Where no bus is named it reads "No A past <station>".
