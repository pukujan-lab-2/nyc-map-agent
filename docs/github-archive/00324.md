Closes #323

Restrooms are turned on, and they are still easy to miss. The icons were small, drawn under bus stops, and only a few appeared near the centre of a zoomed-out map. Street view's corner map was blank, so there was no way to see where you stand, which way you face, or to open a restroom from it. Tapping a place asked for a second point on the map instead of walking from where you already are.

Restroom icons are larger chartreuse WC discs from the opening zoom, spread across the view, and kept above bus badges. The 3D vehicles stay on top of them. Nearest restroom, in Layers & filters, flies to the closest one and says how far it is from you, or from the map centre when location is not shared. Directions from here starts a walking route at the street-view position, or at My location, on restrooms and on landmark, school, storefront, directory, event, parking, and place-toilet sheets. If location is off, the button asks for it. If that is denied, the sheet says so in a sentence. It does not open a popup or drop into map-pick mode.

The street inset keeps flat building footprints, streets, a You dot, a heading cone, and a facing label. A restroom on it opens the same sheet. Hide map and Leave street view are separate labelled buttons. Entering street view re-applies the flat building footprints the main map already uses when that option is on. A saved choice of 3D buildings is left alone. This does not add new 3D.

Station, bus, and train sheets do not get Directions from here. That UI lives in the transit layer, which this change does not edit. Bus badges are moved under the restroom icons from the restroom module.

## How to verify

Chartreuse WC icons show on the open map, including before you zoom in. Layers & filters → Nearest restroom opens the closest restroom with Directions from here. Street shows a corner map with building footprints, You · Facing …, and a restroom that opens a sheet without leaving the street. Hide map and Leave street view are labelled buttons.
