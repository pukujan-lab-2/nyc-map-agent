Street view had visible glitches and dead taps: road names on the asphalt read backwards ("vA 19x99M"), the BQE overhead drew as floating grey slabs, bus stop signs only answered taps on the pole, shop signs didn't open at all, and you couldn't tell which subway line was passing.

## What changes
- **Road names**: the painted street name no longer flips its texture; it reads left to right like the corner blades.
- **Highway slabs**: decks no higher than 8 m (overpasses, viaducts like the BQE) have no piers in the city tiles and end square, so they are not drawn; tall bridge spans and piers stay. A proper fix (piers and ramps for low viaducts) needs a city-tile rebuild.
- **Taps**: a bus stop's tap target now reaches the top of its plate; shop taps reach the sign band (up to 7 m). The shop card links to Google street photos of the place ("See a real photo").
- **Which train is passing**: subway trains within 300 m keep their route bullet in street view; the street minimap draws trains as bullets with their letter.
- **Entrance labels**: name the station's trains ("47-50 Sts-Rockefeller Ctr · B D F M"), sit higher above near stairs, and stop at 180 m (4 labels on desktop, 2 on a phone).

## How to verify
Street view at Meeker Av & Lorimer St, and at Union Av & N 12 St; tap a bus stop sign's plate, a shop sign, and watch the minimap as a G train passes. Repeat at 375 px wide.

## Unchanged
Overhead and 3D views, bus behaviour, the stair kit.
