# PR #329: fix(transit): let a train leave the station after its stop

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-23
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/pull/329
- Branch `fix/train-leave-station` into `main`, +15 −10 in 3 files

A train that has finished its stop stays on the platform until the live feed removes that station. The wait is often most of the ride to the next station, so the train looks stuck.

The position cap now follows the station the train has not reached yet. During the stop it stays on the platform. After the dwell, or after the departure time the feed gives, it rolls toward the next station and still may not pass that station early. A later arrival still slows the train down instead of pulling it backward.

## How to verify

The motion tests cover a train that is still at the platform 10 seconds into a 30 second stop, has left by 50 seconds later while that station is still listed, and has not passed the next station. A slipped arrival still ends at the station on the new time, without a backward jump.

## Unchanged

Terminals still hold, then leave the map. The camera still follows the same estimated position as the train on the map. The frozen extra model when Follow starts shipped separately.

Closes #328
