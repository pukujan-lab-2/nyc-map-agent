## Problem
The underground helper's "Are you in the station? (14 St-Union Sq)" stayed on screen all the way to Grand Central on the `?sim=walk` pretend trip. Once asked, nothing took it down, and the station it named came from the last good street fix, which can be far behind you after a ride.

## What changes
- An unanswered question clears once you are more than 250 m from that station (the same distance that already ends a "visit").
- The question is never asked about a station you are far from, so clearing it does not bring the same question straight back.

## How to verify
- Unit tests in `underground.test.ts` cover both rules.
- `e2e/you-marker.spec.ts`: on `?sim=walk` the question appears at 14 St and goes away during the ride.
- Live: open https://nyc.design-bakery.com/?sim=walk on a phone and watch it go once the train leaves.

## What stays unchanged
When and how the question is first asked, the Yes/No answers, and the "never again after No" rule.
