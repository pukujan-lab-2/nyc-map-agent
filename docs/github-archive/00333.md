# Issue #333: Show on map and a map click leave Follow running

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-23
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/333

## Summary

While the map is following a subway train or a bus, "Show on map", "Show map", and a click on the map itself leave Follow running. The camera stays glued to that vehicle. Those actions should drop Follow immediately.

## Symptoms

1. **Show on map keeps Follow**: An alert row ends with "Show on map". Pressing it opens the alert and does not clear the followed vehicle.
2. **Show map keeps Follow**: The Layers control has a "Show map" button that only closes the drawer. The street-level corner map has a "Show map" button that only brings that small map back. Neither clears Follow.
3. **A plain map click keeps Follow**: Dragging the map stops Follow. A click on empty map does not. A click on a station does.

## Evidence

The person's words: "clicking show on map or any other thing or show map or somethiing should imemdiately make it stop following a subway or trains? or even clicking on map"

On `main`, Follow clears in these places:

- Dragging the map calls `setFollowed(null)` from a `dragstart` listener in `src/features/transit/TransitLayer.tsx`.
- A click that hits a station or an entrance calls `setFollowed(null)` in that same click handler. The handler returns first when the click hits no transit feature, so an empty-map click does not stop Follow.
- A click on a vehicle, a route bullet, or a bus badge starts Follow on that vehicle.
- Ask the map clears Follow before it moves the camera (`handoffCamera` in `src/features/agent/AgentOverlay.tsx`).

"Show on map" is the last line of an alert row in `src/features/transit/AlertCard.tsx`. Its click opens the alert panel and sets the alert focus. It does not call `setFollowed(null)`. While a vehicle is followed, an effect in `TransitLayer` runs `jumpTo` on every update, and `jumpTo` does not fire `dragstart`, so the camera is pulled back to the train.

The Layers "Show map" button in `src/components/LayersDrawer.tsx` only closes the drawer. The corner-map "Show map" button in `src/components/StreetMiniMap.tsx` only unhides that small map.

## Hypotheses

**Follow owns the camera until something calls `setFollowed(null)`.** Status: supported by the code on `main` cited above. Would confirm: follow a train, tap "Show on map" on an alert, and the camera returns to the train with the follow card still up. Would refute: the follow card is gone and the view stays on the alert. Experiment: follow one train on the live map, tap "Show on map", then tap the empty map. This filing did not run that check.

## Counter-signal

Ask the map, a drag, and a station click already stop Follow. The gap is the named buttons and a click that is not a station and not another vehicle.

## Honest caveat

"Any other thing" is the person's phrase. This log names "Show on map", "Show map", and a map click. It does not invent a full list of every other button. The live site was not exercised in this filing. The code cited is `origin/main`.

## Problems vs gaps

Problem: Follow keeps the camera on the train after "Show on map", "Show map", or a plain map click.

Gap: those actions do not clear Follow. *(proposal)* Clearing Follow from "Show on map", from "Show map", and from a plain map click does not exist. Ask the map, drag, and a station click already do clear it.
