**Problem.** On a bus stop card (for example Driggs Av / Union Av, B62), "See them coming on the map" showed nothing. The button only added B62 to your picked lines. Since #538, picked lines keep the buses nearest *you* (your shared location), not the stop, and the route line is drawn only for a selected bus. So the B62 buses coming to that stop never showed, and neither did the road. The "Next buses" rows could not be tapped either.

**What changes.**
1. The big button (now "See them all coming on the map") puts the stop's routes **in focus around the stop**: up to 5 buses per line nearest the stop, and their route lines. This lasts until you close the stop card (opening a bus's card doesn't count), and then your own lines come back. Nothing is written to the URL.
2. Each "Next buses" row is a button. Tapping it focuses that route and **follows that specific bus** on the map. The bus-arrivals API now returns the live feed's vehicle id (`MTA NYCT_7225`, the same id the bus layer uses). If the MTA leaves the id out, the row shows that route around the stop instead.
3. Turning Live buses off also ends a stop watch.

**How to verify.** Tap a bus stop at Driggs Av / Union Av, then tap "See them all coming on the map": B62 buses and the B62 line appear near the stop. Tap a row such as "B62 DOWNTOWN BROOKLYN 5 min": the map follows that bus. Close the card: the B62 watch ends.

**Unchanged.** Picked lines and nearby lines (#538), trips, shuttles and the route picker.

Local checks: types, lint on the changed files, dependency boundaries, and the transit tests (203 passed, including a new one for the stop watch and the vehicle id in arrivals).
