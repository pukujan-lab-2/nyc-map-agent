# Issue #297: A train press should open its stops and delays

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-22
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/297

Pressing a train follows it and shows a slim card, and that card does not open the line.

The card names the next stop and one delay line. Those words are not buttons, so there is no way to open the stops, the delay, or the alerts for that train. The drawer that already lists them is never shown.

Pressing the same train again should let go of it and ease the camera back. Today that press only starts following once more. Stop following stays a labeled button as well.

The Lines button sits alone at the middle of the right edge. On a desktop that is away from the other controls. Group the camera levels together, and group the train controls together: X-ray, Lines, and Stop following.
