Finding the nearest restroom was unreliable at street level and in the other camera modes, the street-view mini map showed too little, and there was no walking route to a restroom. Fixes for these exist as three separate open PRs that edit the same files, and the first one fails CI. This combines them and fixes what blocked them.

**Supersedes #324, #338 and #339.** This branch contains all three; once it merges, those three can be closed.

## What changes
- From #324: larger restroom icons, a Nearest restroom button, Directions from here ("about N min on foot"), and labelled Hide map and Leave street view buttons.
- From #338: Simulate trip for a restroom under about 20 minutes away.
- From #339: the Layers filters that are on now show on the street mini map.
- New in this PR:
  - The mini map covers only about 360 m across, so the nearest restroom is usually off it. A "Nearest restroom · 480 m N" chip opens its card.
  - Nearest restroom now starts from the street-view position or your location, not the map centre, and no longer flies the camera while you walk.
  - Simulate trip starts from the street-view position.
  - Only one restroom layer is drawn on the mini map (#324 and #339 each added one).
  - The `glyphs` and `sprite` style fields are written only when the main style has them. That missing guard caused #324's e2e failure (`glyphs: string expected, undefined found`).
  - The restrooms chunk limit in `.size-limit.json` goes from 1.5 kB to 2.5 kB. The chunk is 2.42 kB, so it is raised only as far as needed.

## How to verify
- Typecheck, lint, unit tests and the size check pass.
- Checked by hand in a browser against the deployed API at Times Square: the mini map showed streets, buildings, the You dot and facing cone plus the chip; the chip opened the card; Directions showed "about 8 min on foot"; Nearest restroom worked in street view (480 m) and in Top view (470 m, flying to the icon) at phone size.

## What stays unchanged
Layer defaults, the restroom data and API, and the phone controls and top bar (QuickLayers, ViewSwitcher, MapPanel).

## Not checked

- A restroom inside the mini map's own view, the filter toggles on the mini map, and Simulate trip were not tried in a browser.
