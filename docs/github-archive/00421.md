A focused line broke into gaps wherever it shared track with another line, a skipped station said nothing when you tapped it, and the exit card covered the whole map. These were the worst things judges would hit on the live map.

## What changes

- **Focused line stays whole.** The focused line is drawn on its own layer from the real line shapes, solid all the way, underground too. Repairs and skips for other lines are hidden while one line is in focus.
- **Stations say what's wrong.** The station panel opens with any notice naming that station, red when trains are not stopping there. Each notice is tappable and shows the problem on the map.
- **One line at a station.** A station with several lines highlights all of them; tap a line's bullet (say the Q) to see only the Q on the map and only the Q's notices, skips and arrivals in the panel. Tap it again for every line.
- **Taps step through what's there.** Tapping the same spot again picks the next train there, then the station. One train: train, then station. Three trains: each in turn, then the station.
- **Less in the way.** Entrances and elevator outages fold into one-line summaries you can open. The side panel sits below the menu row. The exit card is now a short strip, so the map shows you and the exit.

These tap rules are written into `docs/DESIGN_RULES.md`.

## How to check

Focus the N, tap a station where it skips, tap a multi-line station's bullets, and tap a station with a train in it twice. `pnpm test` passes (1,159 tests), plus typecheck and lint.

## What stays the same

Train motion, the agent, rate limits and the bus views are untouched.
