# Issue #152: Street level: look up at buildings and sky (pitch > 90)

- State: closed (2026-10-21)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-21
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/152

At street level, drag-to-look is capped at 89°, so you can't look up at towers or the sky. MapLibre 5 allows maxPitch up to 180. Raise the street-level ceiling to ~150° while keeping the overhead 85° cap; drag, touch, sky, labels and the shared three.js layer must keep working.
