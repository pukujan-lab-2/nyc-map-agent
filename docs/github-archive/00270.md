# Issue #270: Street view: no corner street-name sign at intersections

- State: closed (2026-10-22)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-22
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/270

Feedback (night, Lower East Side): no street-name sign on a pole at our location, and a small green sign floats on a facade.

Root cause: the published city tiles no longer carry the baked `signs` layer (vector_layers: bridges, buildings, props, schools, skyline, storefronts), so corner signs only rarely appeared; the GLB sign was also placed by a fixed 8 m bisector and picked around the map centre, which at eye level floats far down the look ray.

Fix: derive crossings from the basemap's named OSM centrelines around the eye; instanced pole + blades + atlas text; sidewalk-corner placement at a fixed setback from both centrelines.
