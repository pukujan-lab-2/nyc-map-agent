**Problem.** Someone who comes up from the subway and starts walking the wrong way gets no warning. The guidance just counts the distance up.

**What changes**
- `src/lib/geo/wrongWay.ts` (new, shared core): if the distance to the next turn or place grows more than 20 m past the closest you've been, you're walking the wrong way. Only fixes sharper than 30 m count, so GPS jitter below ground never triggers it. Walking back clears it, and a new turn starts over.
- Walking guidance (`RouteGuide.tsx`): the sub-line becomes one amber line, "Turn around, <street> is behind you", and it is spoken once. This applies to real walking only, not the street-view camera.
- Exit answer (`AgentOverlay.tsx`): the same line, naming the place, replaces the compass hint on the small card.

**How to verify**
- Unit tests: `src/lib/geo/wrongWay.test.ts`.
- e2e: `e2e/exit-answer.spec.ts` shares a location at the exit, walks west away from the place, expects the line, then walks back and expects it gone.

**What stays unchanged.** Routes, the checkpoint card and the compass hint when you are on track.
