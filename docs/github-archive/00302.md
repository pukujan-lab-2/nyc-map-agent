Closes #301

The map was drawing every live bus, and turning Live buses off left them on screen. A train alert that names a shuttle, such as the 6 between Hunts Point Av and 125 St, did not single out that bus.

Buses stay off until one of three things asks for them, and only that set is drawn, at most 20 routes:

- One borough page at a time (Queens, Queens–Manhattan, Manhattan, Manhattan–Staten Island, Brooklyn, Brooklyn–Manhattan, Bronx, Bronx–Manhattan, Staten Island). Choosing another page replaces the previous one. Temporary shuttles stay out of those pages.
- A trip from Ask the map, including a bus named only on an alternative.
- A service alert that names a shuttle bus. The 6 alert frames the closed stretch and draws the T102 shuttle and its buses. Live buses off keeps that shuttle hidden until a different alert, a trip, or a page asks again.

The client never requests the whole fleet. `GET /api/transit/bus?routes=` filters the cached feed. `GET /api/transit/bus-lines` is the page catalog.

The Lines button leaves the middle of the right edge and sits with Street / Mid / Up / Top. On a desktop, those controls sit in the map beside an open sheet or the layers drawer, clear of the rotate buttons. On a phone, a half or full details sheet hides them.

How to verify: open the map with no bus page and confirm the fleet is gone. Open Layers & filters, pick one Queens page, and confirm only those routes appear. Open the 6 shuttle alert and confirm the gap and the T102 buses. Ask for a trip that rides a bus and confirm only that bus (and an alternative the trip names) appears. On a phone, open a details sheet halfway and confirm the view controls are covered. On a desktop, open a sheet and confirm the view controls sit between the sheet and the rotate buttons.

Unchanged: subway, LIRR, and Metro-North still load as before. Walking directions do not turn buses on. The all-JS size cap is unchanged.
