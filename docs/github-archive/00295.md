# Issue #295: Stability pass: one list for shaky map behavior

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-22
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/295

Recent shaky behavior has been filed one bug at a time. There is no single list, and a few things people hit on the live site were not written down until they were named in chat.

This is the stability pass list. Add a new line when something else misbehaves, and give that line its own issue before changing code.

Already filed, still open or waiting on a deploy:

- Street view stays slow with Flat buildings and Simple streets (#292).
- Phone controls overlap. #9 names no controls. The view row (Street / Mid / Up / Top, X-ray, and Stop following) is wider than a phone, and it shares the bottom-right corner with the small street map. The Lines button sits at the vertical middle of the screen, on the edge of the bottom sheet.
- X-ray darkens a multi-kilometre disc instead of the nearby tunnel.
- Restrooms in street view and the per-frame street scan (#290) are merged and not necessarily on the live site yet.
- Trip preview speeds (#288) and Use my location (#287) are merged and waiting on that same deploy.

Still rough, not given their own issue yet:

- Restroom kiosks sit on the city's recorded point, which is often inside the building.
- A station tap still closes the Layers drawer. An empty map tap and a train or bus tap are the ones that were specified.
