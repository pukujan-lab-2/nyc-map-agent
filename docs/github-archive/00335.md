# Issue #335: Simulate the walk to a restroom when it is under about 20 minutes

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-23
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/335

## Summary

From a restroom, the map should simulate the trip. When the walk is under about 20 minutes, that simulation is on foot.

## Symptoms

1. **No simulation from the restroom**: Choosing a restroom does not start a trip playback from where you are.
2. **The 20 minute walk is not chosen**: Nothing compares the on-foot time with about 20 minutes and then walks that trip.

## Evidence

The person's words: "or simulate the trip to the bathroom, im assuming by walking for most part if its closer than 20 min or something?"

On `main`, a walking route between two points draws the line and the card says the distance and "about N min on foot". The button Walk along route then moves street view along that line until the end (`poseAlongRoute` in `src/components/MapView.tsx`). Ask the map has a separate Preview trip for a planned trip. Neither starts from a restroom icon, and neither picks walking because the trip is under about 20 minutes.

Pull request #324, still open, adds Directions from here on a restroom. That button sets the route start to your current location and the end to the restroom. It does not start the walk, and it always asks for a walking route. It is not merged.

## Hypotheses

**The restroom sheet stops at the route pins.** Status: supported for the open pull request, which only calls `setRoutePoint` for the two ends. Would confirm: from a restroom, Directions from here shows the walking line and does not move you along it. Would refute: the street view starts walking toward that restroom on its own. Experiment: open a restroom, press Directions from here, and watch whether the camera walks. This filing did not run that check. The pull request is not on the live map.

## Counter-signal

Walk along route already simulates a walking trip once a route exists. Preview trip already plays a planned trip from Ask the map. The missing piece is starting that kind of playback from the restroom, with walking chosen when the on-foot time is under about 20 minutes.

## Honest caveat

"20 min or something" is the person's estimate, not a measured cutoff. What to do when the walk is longer than that was not said. This filing did not time a real restroom walk. The playback on main is a sped-up camera along the line. The 20 minutes belongs to the router's on-foot duration, which the card already prints.

## Problems vs gaps

Problem: a restroom trip is not simulated from where you are.

Gap: nothing starts the walk from the restroom, and nothing uses about 20 minutes to choose walking. *(proposal)* That simulation does not exist. Directions from here in #324 only pins the two ends of a walking route.
