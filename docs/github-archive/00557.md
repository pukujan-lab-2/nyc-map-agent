**Problem.** Closing a restroom answer left every restroom pin on the map, and closing a trip left its "Buses · M101, Q32" pill and bus line. Found while comparing our app with Apple and Google Maps, where closing a task always clears what it drew.

**What changes**
- A trip that turns live buses on turns them back off when it closes or a new question replaces it. If the person already had buses on, they stay on.
- A restroom answer that turns the restroom layer on turns it back off when the answer closes or a new question is asked, and removes its highlighted pins.
- Map settings in the address bar (restrooms, transit layers) now build on the current URL, not the last render's, so two changes in one tap both stick.

**How to verify**
- Unit: `busReveal.test.ts` "closing a trip turns off the buses it turned on, and leaves buses the person had on".
- e2e: `task-screens.spec.ts` "closing a restroom answer takes its restrooms off the map" (with `expectLayout`).

**Unchanged.** The Layers switches, a restroom layer the person turned on themselves, and "Use this route" (buses stay on while you ride).
