At the demo, someone with an older, very small iPhone had the app's menus and panels covering her whole screen. This makes phones chat-first: the map, the chat bar and voice, with the map always in view. It's a draft for the owner to try before merging.

## What changes (phones narrower than 640px only)
- **No top menu row and no zoom slider.** Pinch zooms. Layers, My day and settings are reached by asking the chat.
- **The four task tiles become one row of small chips** (Restroom, My train, Plan trip, Find exit). The row swipes sideways.
- **A language chip in the right-hand rail.** It opens the phone's own language picker, so someone who doesn't read English can still switch.
- **Answers take less of the screen.** The answer box is capped at 30% of the screen height and opens at the start of the reply. Before, it opened scrolled to the end. Its close button now has its own row instead of covering the text.
- **Map credits stay folded** behind the "i" button on every load. Before, they came back open after the map finished loading.
- **Following a train from the chat.** Train-status answers now carry a "See the 7 trains live" button. It opens the line with its live trains, notices and Follow a train. With the menu row gone, this is how a phone gets there.

Desktop and tablets (640px and wider) keep the full menu row.

## How to verify
On a phone, or with browser emulation at 320×568 (iPhone SE 1st gen) and 375×667 (SE 2/3):
1. Open the app: you see only the map, the rail (view, locate, language, QR), the chips and the chat bar.
2. Ask "How do I get to the Statue of Liberty?": the answer opens at its first line and the top half of the map stays visible.
3. Ask "Is the 7 train running?": tap "See the 7 trains live", then Follow a train.

The end-to-end browser tests that relied on the phone menu row now check the chat-first phone instead. The full menu-row test runs at tablet width. All changed tests pass, and so do unit tests and lint.

## Not done yet
- While following a train on a 320px phone, the "Next stop" card still covers the middle of the screen where the train is.
- Trip answers are still long (the "short answer plus See steps" idea isn't built).
