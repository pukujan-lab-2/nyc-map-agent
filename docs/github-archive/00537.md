**Problem.** After picking a subway line there was no keyboard way to move along it or leave it: Escape did nothing and the arrows only panned the map.

**What changes.**
- With a line picked, the arrow that points along the line on screen steps to whatever comes next on it: a train between two stations, otherwise the next station. A ring and label mark the focus, and the map eases there. Focusing a train also makes it the one "Follow a train" rides.
- The arrow across the line switches to another line serving the focused station (DeKalb Av: N → Q → R …), keeping the focus there.
- Escape closes an open card first (the existing panel stack), then leaves the line.
- Off while typing in chat and in street view, where the arrows walk.

**How to verify.** Pick a line, press → / ← (or ↑ / ↓ on a north–south line) and watch the ring hop train-or-station; at a transfer station press the cross arrow to change line; press Escape.

**Unchanged.** Tapping, the line panel, and street-view keys.

Ordering and stepping are pure helpers in `src/features/transit/lineSteps.ts` with unit tests; the keys live in `useLineKeys.ts`.
