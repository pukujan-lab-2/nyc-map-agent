## What
Floods and hazards feature module (`src/features/hazards`, `server/features/hazards`), basic-tier flag.
- `GET /api/hazards`: NWS active alerts (area=NY, User-Agent) filtered to NYC zones (NYZ072–076, 176, 178) for flood, heat, wind, winter and air-quality events, zone geometry from affectedZones; FloodNet NYC sensors with depth ≥ 25 mm in the last hour. 5 min cache. In the OpenAPI spec. `?mock=1` serves sample data (client: `?hazards=mock`).
- Visuals: hatched tint + edge + badge per alert area, pulsing blue sensor dots (static under eco), thin water planes at street level.
- One-line top-bar notice ("Flash Flood Warning until 6:56 AM") that opens a panel card listing alerts and wet sensors.
