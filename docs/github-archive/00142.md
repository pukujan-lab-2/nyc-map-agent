Closes #141.

- **Data:** MTA Subway Entrances and Exits (data.ny.gov `i9wp-a4ja`, 2,120 points) is baked into the static `subway.json` by `build:transit`. No tile build is needed for 2k points; it can move to Octo later.
- **Asset:** no CC0/CC-BY NYC entrance glTF found, so this is a procedural low-poly kit in the shared `threeLayer.ts`. Stair: green railings, descending steps, two green globe lamps, a sign. Elevator: glass cab with a roof, a sign and a lamp. Two InstancedMeshes, capped at 300 each. Each entrance faces its station.
- **Visibility:** only at street level, only within 300 m of the camera (re-culled on moveend). Easements, station houses and walkways get no kit.
- **Overhead:** a small green dot at z ≥ 16. At street level the dot becomes an invisible tap target. Tapping opens the station panel.
- **Outages:** elevator entrances at stations with an active elevator/escalator outage are tinted gray.
- Darkens the el steel and toughens the scene lighting on both kits.
- A dev-only `window.__walk` hook for headless QA.
