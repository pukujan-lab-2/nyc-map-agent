# Issue #292: Flat buildings and simple streets stay slow in street view

- State: closed (2026-10-22)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-22
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/292

Flat buildings and Simple streets are the light map, and street view is still slow with both of them on.

Street view ignores Flat buildings and turns on extruded buildings plus the textured city: street-name signs, shop signs, and hydrants. Simple streets only pales the road paint, so it leaves that city running. Walking and looking move the camera every frame, and the corner label rereads every loaded street-name tile each time.

With Flat buildings selected, street view should keep flat footprints and leave the textured city off. Corner and street-name reads should wait until you have moved.
