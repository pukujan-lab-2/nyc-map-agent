# Issue #156: Show the Manhattan skyline from zoom 11-12 so waterfront views don't load late

- State: closed (2026-10-21)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-21
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/156

## Summary

Seen from the Brooklyn or Queens waterfront in a tilted view, Manhattan is flat until you zoom in to 14, and then the towers pop in all at once. Every building in the city tiles starts at zoom 14 (tippecanoe `-Z14`), and the basemap's own 3D buildings and the hero landmarks also start at 14. The camera fetches lower-zoom tiles toward the horizon, and those tiles have no buildings in them.

## What I propose
- Bake a light `skyline` layer into the city tiles: towers over 60 m, plus towers over 120 m from zoom 11. Only height and a facade colour are kept, and slender towers aren't dropped.
- Draw it as plain-colour extrusions from zoom 11 and hand over to the existing buildings at 14.
- Rebuild and publish the tiles. Compare before and after screenshots from DUMBO and Long Island City.
