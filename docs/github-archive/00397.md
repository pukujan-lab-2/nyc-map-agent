Three things still got in the way after the clean map came back. Bus-stop icons crowded the transit lines on the overview. Switching on Live buses opened the full filters drawer and asked you to pick a bus line. And street view was a bare grey plane with no buildings, shops or signs, because the "Flat" buildings default was applied at street level too.

## What changes

1. **Bus-stop icons are off on the overview.** The Bus stops switch in Filters turns them back on. Street view always shows the bus-stop poles, because that's where you need them.
2. **Live buses turns on the buses around you.** With your location shared, or otherwise at the map's centre, it switches on the routes that serve the most stops within about 300 m, up to six, using the existing `/api/transit/bus-stops` endpoint. The full line picker stays under More layers.
3. **Street view is the 3D city.** It shows buildings, the textured city, storefronts, shops and corner names, even when the overview is flat. Eco mode still turns all of it off.
4. **Tapping the mini map leaves street view** for the overview. Tapping a restroom on the mini map still opens that restroom.
