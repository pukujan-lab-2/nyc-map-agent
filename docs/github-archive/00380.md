Top view downloaded three.js and its model loader on every first load and could draw 3D buildings, and the detailed 3D city is too heavy for many computers (owner's report; baseline in #362).

## What changes
- **#364 (`587dbc2`)**: removes a storefronts entry that pointed at the three.js file and loaded it for nothing; a new `cityDetail()` helper in `src/lib/map/buildings.ts` gives Top view no extruded buildings, no skyline and no textured city. School icons load only their tiles.
- **#363 (`acb54ff`)**: tilted views use plain theme-coloured extruded buildings from the tiles already loaded (no extra requests). A new flag `detailedCity` (advanced tier, off by default) brings back the skyline, facades, signs, props and landmark models. Empire State, Chrysler, One WTC and the Statue of Liberty stay as name labels. Street level is unchanged.

## How to verify
In the browser: default Top requests no three.js or 3D city chunks and draws no extrusions; tilting brings 3D back and Top removes it; `detailedCity` on restores the detailed city. Map and flag tests pass (95); typecheck and eslint on touched files pass.
