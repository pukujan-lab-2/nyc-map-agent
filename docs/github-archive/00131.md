**What**
- OSM `building:part` massing: DuckDB spatial puts each part (Geofabrik NY extract, ODbL, `osmium tags-filter`) on the footprint holding its centre; footprints with 2+ tagged parts draw as those parts (`h` + `base` from `height`/`min_height`/levels). Citywide: 18,111 parts replace single boxes (setbacks, spires, podiums).
- Facades: `glass` (>100 m, post-1960), `limestone` (prewar >40 m), `castiron` (SoHo/Tribeca, pre-1915, 3–8 floors), `brownstone` (pre-1910 row houses). Textures are drawn in-repo (CC0), see `scripts/make-facade-textures.py`.
- Darker roofs (tar/gravel tones) on the roof cap.
- Rooftop wooden water tanks (legs, barrel, cone) on about one in three prewar mid-rises (6–22 floors, pre-1960), drawn as props (z16+).
- No new renderer: still MapLibre fill-extrusion on the same PMTiles.

**Tiles**: citywide rebuilt (104.9 MB); I'll publish it to Octo/R2 after merge. Pilot tiles are committed.
