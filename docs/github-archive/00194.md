# Issue #194: Live subway trains glide through stations instead of stopping

- State: closed (2026-10-22)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-21
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/194

## Problem
Live subway trains never stop at stations. Their position is estimated from GTFS-realtime (the MTA's live feed) predicted arrival times and interpolated at constant speed, so trains glide straight through stops, start from mid-track at their origin, and slide off after their last stop.

## Symptoms
1. **No dwell**: a train's marker never pauses at a station; it moves continuously between predicted arrivals.
2. **Constant speed**: no slowing into or speeding out of stations.
3. **Origin glide**: trains that have not left their first stop are drawn moving toward it instead of waiting there.
4. **No status**: the train details sheet never says the train is at a station.

## Proposal *(proposal)*
- Use realtime departure times when the feed gives them; otherwise a default dwell: 30 s, about 50 s at major transfer complexes, about 60 s at express/local transfer points (derived from static GTFS stop patterns, with a short curated override list). These hold times are estimates; GTFS has no official hold flag.
- Shorten travel time so the next predicted arrival still holds; ease in and out of stops.
- Wait at the origin until departure; dwell at the terminal, then disappear.
- Sheet shows "At <station>", "Departs <terminal> in X min" or "Arrived at <terminal>". One position function drives 2D bullets, 3D models and the follow camera.
