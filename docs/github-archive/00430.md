Two problems while following a train, both reported from the live site:

1. **Change view did nothing.** You couldn't switch to Overhead, Chase or Street. The menu bar closes any open menu when a line is selected, and following a train always selects that train's line, so the follow view menu shut the moment it opened. It now stays open while following. Picking a newly selected line still closes it.
2. **A train sat still, then jumped.** A train is held at its next stop until the feed says it left. When it runs late (the E had a delay notice), it waits there. Once the feed cleared the stop, the drawn train was set straight to the new estimate, often several hundred metres ahead. Now it catches up at up to 35 m/s, about twice a subway's top speed. A forward gap over 1.5 km is still drawn at once, since that's a new snapshot or a reroute, not a train you were watching move.

**How to verify**
- `pnpm vitest run src/features/transit/monotonic.test.ts`: after a hold, the train never moves more than 35 m in a second and reaches the estimate. A 1.6 km jump is drawn at once. The existing tests (never backward, never past a listed stop) still pass.
- Live: follow any train, tap Change view, and the menu (Map, Chase, Side, Overhead, Street) opens and switches the camera.

**What stays the same**
How trains move between stops, stop holds, and the drawn train sitting just above the station dot while stopped (that offset is deliberate).
