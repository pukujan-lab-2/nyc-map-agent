Closes #149.

- **Data:** `scripts/build-xray.ts` (`OSM_PBF=… pnpm build:xray`) reads a local OSM extract with osmium. It keeps platforms, stairs, indoor/tunnel passages and elevators within reach of the 286 underground subway stations and turns `level`/`layer` tags into depth (5 m per level). Output is `public/transit/xray.json`: 273 stations, 1,752 ways, 556 elevators, 58 kB gzipped, fetched only the first time x-ray is opened. No tile build, no runtime Overpass (public Overpass was at capacity).
- **UI:** one **X-ray** toggle in the subway station panel. Closing the panel turns it off.
- **Render** (shared `threeLayer.ts` renderer, `setXray`): a dimmed ground disc with everything below drawn through it, shallower levels on top. Platforms are colored by level, stairs are yellow sloped ribbons, passages are light ribbons, elevator shafts are green (red when the station has an elevator outage), and street entrances are green posts. The camera tilts in to z17.3 / 55°.
- Also: the vehicle layer no longer disposes the shared renderer on removal (it would break hydrants/signs/storefronts since #143).
