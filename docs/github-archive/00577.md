**Problem:** live after #576, fast trips answered in 1–2 s but wrong. "Plan a trip from Union Square to the Brooklyn Museum" routed to the Spark Brooklyn Children's Museum Annex in Dumbo (the lookup with "the" in front finds it; without, it finds the museum). And every arrival time was 4 hours early ("6:15 pm" for 10:15 pm): the trip's times are New York local without a zone, and the answer converted them as if they were UTC.

**What changes**
- The fast path drops a leading "the" from places, as the model does.
- It answers a trip or exit only when the place found carries the asked name (`foundAsAsked`); otherwise the model takes the question. `tripBrief` now includes `destination` and `station_exit` returns `to`, the found place's label.
- The arrival time is read as written (`clock`: "2026-10-28T22:43" → "10:43 pm").
- "Take the 4 train" rather than "the 4 subway".
