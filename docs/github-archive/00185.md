# PR #185: Each 3D subway train shows its own route bullet on its front and rear signs

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-21
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/pull/185
- Branch `fix/train-route-signs` into `main`, +168 −1 in 4 files

Every 3D subway train showed an **A** on its front and rear signs, whatever line it ran, so a Q train read as an A train in the 3D view.

**Why:** the R142 model from #165 bakes one sign into its texture, and the vehicle layer draws every train with that one shared material. The layer was never told the route, so nothing could change per train.

## What changes
- `VehicleInstance` gains an optional `route`; `TransitLayer` passes each train's route id.
- New `src/lib/map/routeSign.ts` (no three.js): `signFace(route)` picks the bullet (MTA line colour; black letter on yellow N/Q/R/W, white elsewhere; diamond for express ids like 6X; S for shuttles; same rules as the 2D bullets in #178), and `RouteSignCache` keeps one sign per route and disposes it once no train on that route is in the update.
- `threeLayer.ts` draws a small sign quad over each car end (front and rear, not mirrored), one `InstancedMesh` per route sharing one `CanvasTexture`; textures, materials and meshes are disposed on prune, on model refit and on layer removal.

## How to verify
- `pnpm vitest run src/lib/map/routeSign.test.ts`: faces differ by route (A blue/white, Q yellow/black, 6X express), one cached sign per route, unused routes disposed.
- At street level near Times Square, an A and a Q train show a blue A and a yellow Q on their ends.

## What stays unchanged
Bus and rail models, ghost (underground) trains, the model livery itself, and the 2D bullets.
