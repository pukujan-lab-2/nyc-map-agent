# PR #167: Transit Focus: fade the rest of the map so subway and bus stand out

- State: merged (2026-10-21)

When someone plans a trip by subway or bus, streets, labels and data layers compete with the transit lines, so the lines are hard to follow.

**What changes**
- A Transit Focus chip next to Transit. On, it draws one pale veil just under the transit layers, so the street map and every layer below fade while subway and bus lines, stations and vehicles stay at full strength.
- Turning Focus on also switches subway and bus on if transit was off. The choice is saved in the browser and survives a reload.

**How to verify**
Tap Transit Focus at 1440 and 390 wide: the street map fades and the lines stay bright; tap again and the map is exactly as before (the veil layer is removed, nothing else is restyled). Screenshots of off and on at both widths were taken over the browser debugging protocol and reviewed.

**What stays the same**
No new dependency; no layer paint is changed, so turning Focus off cannot leave the map in a half-dimmed state. Typecheck, lint, unit tests (two new) and full project-continuity and content-generation checks pass.
