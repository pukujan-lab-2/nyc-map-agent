## Problem
You could not see where you are on the map. Only the "which exit" answer drew your dot; Center on my location moved the map but marked nothing, and the direction you face was only tracked during that one answer. There was also no way to watch location work without walking outside.

## What changes
- **One "you" marker on every screen** once location is shared: blue dot, a soft cone for the way you face, and a faint circle as wide as the position is uncertain (it grows below ground). It glides between fixes instead of jumping, and stays above other layers. The exit answer no longer draws its own dot; walking guidance keeps its arrow and the dot steps aside while it runs.
- **Center on my location cycles like Google and Apple Maps:** first tap follows you, next tap turns the map with the way you face, next goes back to north up. Dragging the map, or an answer framing a place, stops following.
- **Facing:** the phone's own direction of travel is used when moving; the iOS compass permission is shared across screens.
- **`?sim=walk` pretend trip:** walk through Union Square Park to a stair, down to the platform, ride the uptown 6 along the real track (pausing at each stop) to Grand Central, up to 42 St and west to the Bryant Park restroom. It is just another location feed, so everything that reads your location sees it the same way.

## How to verify
- Open https://nyc.design-bakery.com/?sim=walk on a phone and on desktop: the dot appears, moves, and the map follows. Drag the map: following stops, the dot stays. Tap the locate button: following resumes.
- `e2e/you-marker.spec.ts` checks this at 390 and 1280 px; unit tests cover the pretend trip and the tap cycle.

## What stays unchanged
Location is still shared only after a tap (or with `?sim=walk`), kept in memory only. Walking guidance's arrow is untouched.

## Not in this PR
Smart facing calibration and wrong-way warnings come next.
