## Problem

The map can't show what's happening on the street (street fairs, markets, pop-ups, outdoor shows, NYC Parks events). Trip simulation's inline trip cards will need "street events and closures" near a route, and there was no events data to draw on.

## What changes

**Sources.** All are free NYC Open Data, read through the Socrata API. Each one was checked with a real fetch on 2026-10-21. Terms: [NYC Open Data Terms of Use](https://opendata.cityofnewyork.us/overview/#termsofuse).

| Source | Dataset | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| [NYC Permitted Event Information](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/City-Government/NYC-Permitted-Event-Information/tvpp-9vvx) | `tvpp-9vvx` (historical: `bkfu-528j`) | Street fairs, block parties, farmers markets, sidewalk sales, plaza and open-street events, parades and races (as closures). Sports permits, film shoots and theatre load-ins are dropped. Permits longer than 14 days are dropped too (bump-outs, outreach tables), except markets. |
| [NYC Parks Public Events – Upcoming 14 Days](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/d/w3wp-dpdi) | `w3wp-dpdi` | Parks events with coordinates. Recurring fitness and rec-centre classes are dropped. This is the same feed as nycgovparks.org `/xml/events_*`, but that path is disallowed in their robots.txt, so we read the Open Data copy. |
| [Centerline (CSCL)](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/City-Government/Centerline/inkn-q76z) | `inkn-q76z` | Geocoding. Permitted events only say "X between Y and Z", and GeoSearch can't resolve intersections. CSCL segment endpoints shared by two streets give both ends of the block, which becomes a LineString. |
| NYC Planning GeoSearch (already used by the app) | — | Park-name locations in Parks permits ("Union Square Park: North Plaza"). |

Luma and Eventbrite are left for later. Nothing is scraped. Street closures come from the permits' own `street_closure_type` and from parade and race permits, so no separate closures dataset was needed.

**Server** (`server/features/events/`)
- `schema.ts`: zod schemas for the three payloads, the normalised `Event` `{id, title, kind (fair|market|popup|show|parks|closure|other), start, end, location (Point|LineString), address, source, url}`, and the query and response.
- `GET /api/v1/events?bbox=w,s,e,n&from=&to=&kind=a,b` (also `/api/events`) is a typed zod-openapi route with a response schema. It reads `core.street_events` and returns GeoJSON. The window defaults to now → +7 days, with a 31-day maximum. Bad input gets a 400. It is added to `openapi.json`.
- Migration `20261022000100_street_events.sql` creates `core.street_events`, with generated lon/lat for the bbox filter and a time index. It has a down migration.
- `scripts/ingest.ts` adds an `events` step to the existing nightly ingest on gravebuster. It runs fetch → normalise → geocode → `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE`, then prunes events that ended more than 2 days ago and logs to `core.ingest_runs`. `pnpm ingest events` runs only this step.

**Client** (`src/features/events/`, registered in `src/features/index.ts`, flag `events` in the registry)
- An "Events" chip in Layers & filters, using the existing `chip` mechanism, so the layer is off and not mounted until it's chosen (`on=events`). While it's on, "Events today" and "Events this weekend" chips narrow the window (New York days).
- Small ink pins with a white ring. A chartreuse dot marks events happening now, refreshed every minute. A closed block also gets a thin dashed ink line.
- A tap opens the event sheet through the public `pushPanel` → `showPanel` path: title, kind, time, place, a labelled "Source: …" link and Directions. No tooltips. MapPanel, PanelContext and the drawer internals are unchanged.

## Sample counts, this week (Mon 19 – Sun 25 Oct 2026, live fetch)

- 416 events kept after filtering and geocoding:
  - 207 permitted events: 139 fair, 30 other, 22 closure, 10 show, 6 market
  - 209 Parks events: 175 parks, 22 fair, 12 show
- 40 happening today (Wed 21 Oct); 273 this weekend.
- 77 events are drawn as block lines.
- Geocoding: 357 of 376 public permitted events this week were placed (95%). The rest are skipped. Most are aliases such as "6 Avenue" for Avenue of the Americas, or West Houston.
- Full nightly window (−1 day to +30 days): 3,080 permitted rows and 780 Parks rows give 995 events, with only 35 public permits left unplaced. A full ingest run takes about 55 s.
