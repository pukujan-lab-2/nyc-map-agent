The street-view mini map didn't help you find your way. It drew blocks and a cone but no street names, no station names, no landmarks and no trip, so you couldn't tell where you were or where you were heading. Street view also started with live trains, buses and x-ray switched off. Subway entrances didn't say which station they belong to.

## What changes
- **Mini map content:** street names, subway station names, bus stops, landmark badges and names, and the trip you are on (walk, ride, exit path, destination and answer pins) are always drawn. Shops already showed and still do.
- **Mini map controls:** the map floats. Drag the move handle to place it anywhere. Drag the corner handle to resize it, or tap the handle to step through three sizes. Both choices are remembered. "Hide map" is gone and "Leave street view" now reads "Leave". The restroom chip shows an arrow pointing where to turn, a restroom icon and the distance (for example "30 m").
- **Street view defaults:** live trains, the bus routes near you and x-ray of nearby tunnels come on when you enter street view. Leaving puts back whatever this turned on, unless you changed it meanwhile. Roads stay drawn even when Hide roads is set.
- **Entrance labels:** each subway entrance names its station: "47-50 Sts-Rockefeller Ctr · Stairs" on the first line, the corner on the second. On a phone, entrances that are farther away show only the station name.
- **Location button:** between 640 and 767 px wide, the location button and view switch move up so the map credits' ⓘ no longer covers the location button.

## How to verify
Open street view (desktop, 700 px wide, and 375 px wide).
- The mini map names streets and stations and shows landmarks and bus stops.
- The move and resize handles work, and the map comes back at the same size and place after a reload.
- A nearby entrance names its station.

## What stays the same
- The mini map is still north-up and stays flat (no 3D).
- A tap on the mini map still opens the flat map, and a tap on a restroom still opens its card.
