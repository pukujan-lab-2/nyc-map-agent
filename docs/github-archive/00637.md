# Issue #637: Wide screens: move the menus to a left column like Google Maps

### Summary

On desktop and other wide screens the app's menus, panels and toasts crowd each other: the single swipeable menu row at the top left is clipped at the edge, panels open over the map, and a toast sits against the chat bar. The owner wants wide screens to use a Google Maps style left column of menus, with the panels opening beside it, and phones left as they are.

### Identity and Lineage

Repository Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent. Code: `src/components/MapChrome.tsx`, `src/components/MenuRow.tsx`, `src/lib/map/mapCover.ts`. Rules: `docs/DESIGN_RULES.md` ("Top left is one swipeable menu row") and `docs/PDD.md` ("Screen space"). Related: the layout bar in AGENTS.md (`e2e/layout.ts`).

### Acceptance Criteria

1. At 1024 px wide and up, the six menus (My day, Layers, Filters, Data, City data, Settings) are a vertical column docked to the left edge, in that order, each with its icon and label.
2. Tapping one opens its panel beside the column; only one is open at a time; the map's cover padding keeps the focus (you, the followed train, the asked place) clear.
3. Below 1024 px the current top row and pull sheet are unchanged.
4. The chat bar, the quick buttons, zoom, compass, view switch and locate keep their places, and nothing overlaps or scrolls sideways.
5. `e2e/responsive.spec.ts` calls `expectLayout` for the column at 1280x800 and 1024x768, and the small sizes still pass.
6. `docs/DESIGN_RULES.md` and `docs/PDD.md` describe the wide-screen layout.

### Boundaries and Next Action

In scope: wide-screen placement of the menu row and its panels. Out of scope: phones and small tablets, what is inside each menu, the chat bar. Next action: build the column from the existing menu row, reuse the cover mechanism, add the layout test.

Owner decision, 2026-11-01 (in chat): wide screens get a left column like Google Maps; phones keep what they have.

### Symptoms

1. **Menu row clipped:** at about 2000 px wide the row ends under its own arrow, which sits on top of the lock icon.
2. **Crowded bottom centre:** the quick buttons, an error toast and the chat bar stack in a small area.
3. **Panels cover the map:** the Lines panel and the toast take much of a phone-width view.

### Hypotheses (unverified)

H1. A vertical list needs no swiping, looping or clipped arrow, so the row's problems disappear on wide screens. **Status:** unverified. **Would confirm:** the layout test passes at 1280x800 and 1024x768 with all six menus visible. **Would refute:** a menu or panel still overlaps another control. **Experiment:** build it and run `expectLayout` at all listed sizes.

### Counter-signal

`docs/DESIGN_RULES.md` says the top-left row is the same at every size, so this changes a rule the owner wrote on 2026-10; the owner has now said wide screens differ.

### Honest Caveat

I have seen two screenshots (a wide desktop and a phone), not every size between 600 and 1024 px, so the breakpoint may need tuning.

### Problems vs Gaps

Problem: the wide-screen layout wastes the side and crowds the middle. Gap: no wide-screen layout rule and no layout test for one.
