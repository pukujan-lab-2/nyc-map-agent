Closes #145

- `scripts/bridges.ts`: OSM highway/railway ways with bridge=yes|viaduct (Geofabrik NY extract, ODbL, `osmium tags-filter`) are cut into ≤25 m pieces and baked into a new `bridges` tile layer: deck slabs, railings, and piers every ~50 m under decks higher than 8 m.
- Deck height: 16 named major bridges get a mid-span clearance (Brooklyn/Manhattan/Williamsburg 41 m, Verrazzano 69 m, GWB 65 m…) with a smoothstep ramp from the two shore ends. Overpasses sit at 6 m per OSM `layer`.
- `city3d-bridges` fill-extrusion layer, from z14. No new renderer.
- Not included: suspension towers and cables (a follow-up could use the hero-landmark models).
