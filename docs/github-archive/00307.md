# Issue #307: The underground dash breaks between elevated stretches

- State: closed (2026-10-23)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-23
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/307

On Top view, the underground line between elevated stretches breaks. Some of that stretch looks solid, and some of it looks like a torn dash.

The route shapes keep a separate copy for every branch. Where those branches share a trunk, the map paints the trunk once per branch. An elevated line is already solid, so the extra copies still look like one line. The underground line is a faint dash, so the copies pile up. Where the dashes line up they look solid. Where they slip they look broken. That seam shows between the elevated sections over East New York.

The Fulton Street tunnel through Euclid Avenue, Shepherd Avenue, Van Siclen Avenue, Liberty Avenue, and Grant Avenue is underground and should stay a dash. The Liberty Avenue line east of Grant Avenue is elevated and should stay solid.
