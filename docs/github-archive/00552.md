**Problem.** Tapping "Use this route" on a train trip (for example the M to Elmhurst Av) jumped straight to the M line panel. It gave no directions to the station, and on phones the open sheet hid the "Center on my location" button, so there was no way to see where you were.

**What changes**
- A subway or bus trip with more than 80 m to walk first starts with walking guidance to the station entrance or bus stop.
- Under the walking card, a "Next on this trip" panel says "Then the M train at Elmhurst Av", with an "I'm at the station" button (or "I'm at the stop" for a bus) and an "I'm lost" button. Tapping "I'm at the station" ends the walk and opens that line toward your stop, the same view as before.
- Near the station (under 150 m left), this panel takes priority over a landmark checkpoint.
- On phones, the locate button now stays above a half-open sheet. Only a full-height sheet hides it.

**How to verify**
- New layout case in `e2e/responsive.spec.ts`: a train trip, then Use this route, then walking guidance, then "I'm at the station", then the line panel with locate visible, at every phone and desktop size.
- Unit tests for `walkToBoard`.

**Unchanged**
- Walk-only trips work as before.
- Trips with a short walk still open the line straight away.
