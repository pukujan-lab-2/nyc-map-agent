**Problem.** Taking the train the wrong way and coming up at the wrong exit are the two classic subway mistakes for a newcomer. Until now the app said nothing about either.

**What changes**
- `src/features/underground/wrongTrain.ts` (new, pure) works on the trip from `publishActiveTrip`.
  - **Wrong direction:** after you board, you get more than 400 m farther from the stop you get off at than the closest you've been. Fixes vaguer than 200 m are ignored. Heading back toward the stop clears it.
  - **Next station:** the next station on the line in your direction of travel, for the message.
  - **Wrong exit:** the first sharp fix after vague ones below ground lands more than 150 m from the planned exit.
- The underground helper (`Underground.tsx`) prints one line in the bottom sheet and speaks it, with a Dismiss button:
  - "This train is going downtown. Get off at <next> and cross over."
  - After the wrong exit: "You came up at a different exit. New walk to <place>." The walk is planned again from where you are.
- `?sim=wrongway` (`simWalk.ts`): the same pretend trip, but it boards the downtown 6 by mistake and rides to Astor Pl, with the uptown trip to Grand Central on screen.

**How to verify**
- Unit tests: `src/features/underground/wrongTrain.test.ts`. This includes the whole `?sim=wrongway` ride, which warns south of Union Sq.
- e2e: `e2e/wrong-train.spec.ts` checks the line at 375×812, that the ask box is still there, and that Dismiss works.

**What stays unchanged.** The station question, exit packs and the ride estimate. `?sim=walk` is the same.
