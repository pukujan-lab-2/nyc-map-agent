Picking Top or Aerial while following a walking route could snap back to the tilted street camera. The camera had several owners that didn't know about each other. Street walking, 2D guidance, "keep me centred" and the heading-up tilt were separate on/off flags in three files (`MapStateContext`, `RouteGuide`, `MapView`). Some combinations should never happen, but nothing stopped them. Each fix patched one combination by hand.

## What changes

1. **New `src/lib/map/cameraMode.ts`: one value says who drives the camera.**
   - **map:** you pan.
   - **street:** eye-level walk. It remembers whether guidance was on before.
   - **guide:** 2D guidance from your location, with `centred` and `tilt`.

   Every change goes through one function, `nextCameraMode`, so street and guidance can never both move the camera.
2. **The rules are written down once:**

   | You do | From | You get |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | Pick Top, Aerial or Closer | street walk | map, or guidance without tilt if guidance was on |
   | Pick a view | guidance | guidance keeps you centred, no re-tilt |
   | Leave street | street walk during guidance | guidance resumes |
   | Pan | guidance | stops centring until Recenter |
   | Reset view | guidance | the whole city; guidance stays on but stops pulling back |
3. **The existing code reads from the store.** `walking` and `walkRequest` in `MapStateContext`, and `navigating` and `centered` in `RouteGuide`, are now derived from it, so other components don't change. The Change view menus no longer stop the walk themselves; the map's view command does it.
