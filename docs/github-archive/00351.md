In street mode, subway station names were missing, so a person walking could not tell which station they were looking at.

**Stacked on #346.** This branch includes that PR's commit (`00571cb`), so this PR targets its branch to show only the new commit. After #346 merges it can be re-targeted to `main`.

## Why they were missing
- The walking view hides every map text layer except a short allow-list, and the transit station labels were not on it.
- Once visible, the labels were still dropped by collision: at street level the camera is tilted about 86° at zoom 22.7, which squashes the collision boxes.

## What changes
- Layers whose id starts with `transit-` are exempt from the hiding (`MapView.tsx`).
- While walking, only stations within 220 m of the walker get labels, so far stations do not pile up on the horizon.
- While walking, the label allows overlap and draws larger (name 15 px, route bullets 1.35×), with `text-pitch-alignment` set to `viewport`.

## How to verify
Open the map, enter street mode near a station, and turn the camera until the station is on screen. Checked in a browser at phone size: "Times Sq-42 St" with red 1, 2, 3 bullets (underground), and "Knickerbocker Av" with an orange M bullet beside the elevated columns. Typecheck, lint and the 61 transit and components tests pass.

## What stays unchanged
Station labels outside street mode, and the files used by the phone controls.

## Not checked
Halsey St (L) was not verified: no station was in the loaded map within 400 m of that point. A second `__walk` call while already in street mode does nothing, so the checks reloaded between tests. `MapView.tsx` is also edited by #347 and by the draft #341, so expect small conflicts.
