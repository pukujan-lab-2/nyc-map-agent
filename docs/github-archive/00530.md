**Problem.** In street view you couldn't tap a bus stop or a subway entrance to learn anything, couldn't ride a passing bus or train, phones had no switch for restrooms and buses, the open credits had no way to close, pressing Exit didn't stop the agent from answering and moving the map, and the "Are you at …?" exit question was hidden behind the task buttons.

**What changes.**
- Bus stop signs and entrances are tap targets in street view (bus arrivals with *See them coming on the map*; the entrance opens its station).
- Tapping a bus or train while walking rides it in chase cam; **Get off and walk** resumes the walk where the vehicle is.
- Phones get a layers button with the restroom and bus switches.
- The credits button shows an x when open; any map tap closes it.
- Exit / Close Ask aborts the running answer; nothing after the abort moves the map or speaks.
- The idle task row hides while a pull sheet is up.
