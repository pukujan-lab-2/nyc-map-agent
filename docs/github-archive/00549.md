**Problem.** On the live site, pressing the arrow across a picked line never changed line (checked at Atlantic Av-Barclays Ctr on the N). Three causes: on a diagonal line both arrows counted as \"along\" it (the cut-off was 60° instead of 45°); with a train focused there was no station to switch at; and only the focused platform's own lines were offered, while Atlantic Av-Barclays Ctr is four station ids (2 3 4 5, B Q, D N R W, and the L nearby).

**What changes.**
- An arrow within 45° of the line steps along it; otherwise it switches line.
- Switching uses the station nearest the focus, so it works from a focused train.
- The lines offered are those of every platform within 300 m, so the 2/3/4/5 and B/Q are reachable from the N at Atlantic. The new line starts at its own platform there.

**How to verify.** Pick the N, step to Atlantic Av-Barclays Ctr, press → repeatedly: R, W, then the B/Q and 2/3/4/5 platforms in turn. Unit tests cover the complex case.

**Unchanged.** Stepping along a line and Escape.
