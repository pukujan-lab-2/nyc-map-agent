**What**: at street level (zoom ≥ 17), a green NYC-style street-name sign stands on a corner at every intersection near the camera: a grey pole about 3.5 m tall with two perpendicular green blades naming the cross streets.

- **Data**: `scripts/street-signs.sql` (DuckDB spatial) derives about 50k intersections from DCP LION 25C. An intersection is a LION node shared by street segments with two or more names. Blade A is the busiest street at the node, and blade B is the cross street closest to perpendicular. Bearings come from node-to-node azimuths, and names use DOT abbreviations (ST, AV, BLVD…). `pnpm build:sign-tiles` packs them with tippecanoe and uses tile-join to merge a `signs` point layer (`a`, `b`, `ba`, `bb`) into `public/tiles/nyc-city.pmtiles`. That adds about 1.3 MB. The merged file is already published through Octo/R2 (`/api/city-tiles`, etag `2a95bd29…`).
- **Render**: `src/lib/map/streetSigns.ts` is a MapLibre custom layer that draws with three.js in the same canvas and depth buffer, so there is no second canvas and no deck.gl. It is lazy-loaded with the city layers. It places the CC0 Grab3D "Street Name Sign" GLB 8 m off the centreline crossing along the bisector, and overlays canvas-text decals on both faces of each blade. Signs appear only from zoom 17, at most 60 of them, and only within 300 m of the camera. They toggle with the textured city.
- **Deps**: `three` 0.134.0 (already in the lockfile via mapillary-js) plus `@types/three`, noted in AI_RULES.md. Model license: `public/models/LICENSE.md`. Data credit: `public/tiles/LICENSE.md`.
