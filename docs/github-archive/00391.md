# PR #391: feat(street): show rain and snow in street view, with a heads-up

The owner wants weather shown, not told: rain or snow visible in street view with how much, and a plain "bring a jacket" heads-up.

## What changes (behind the new flag `streetWeather`, off by default)
- A 2D canvas over street view draws rain, snow or sleet from the forecast's weather code and mm/h (at most 260 particles, one batch per frame, paused when the tab is hidden), plus a grey tint for clouds and fog. Reduced motion and eco mode get the tint only.
- A heads-up chip: one recommendation ("Take an umbrella", "Roads may be icy", "Bring a jacket"), feels-like temperature, precipitation chance and amount, strong wind, and active warnings. The same line appears in trip answers; planned trips use their departure hour.
- New `/api/weather/hour`. Weather lookups now round coordinates to about 1 km (a privacy-audit gap from #384).
- Dev override: `?streetWeather=rain:0.8` or `window.__streetWeather("snow", 1)`.

## How to verify
20 new tests pass; typecheck, eslint and depcruise are clean. In the browser at Times Square, rain, snow and sleet at full strength held 60 fps.

## Not checked
The live endpoint in the browser (production doesn't have it yet, so the override was used; called directly it returned real forecasts), the trip-answer line, and the reduced-motion tint.

Based on `main`.
