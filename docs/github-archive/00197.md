# Issue #197: Live trains and stations should react to skipped stops, holds, cancellations and alerts

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-21
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/197

## Problem
Live trains and stations ignore service changes. A train keeps gliding toward a stop it will skip, a held train keeps moving on the map, cancelled trips stay visible, and alerts show only the raw MTA header with no sense of where on the line the problem is. Riders find MTA notices hard to read, so the map should say plainly what is going on.

## What the feeds actually carry (live sample, 2026-10-21 ~17:03 EDT)
1. **Trip updates**: the ACE, BDFM and 1-6 feeds had 674 trip updates and 539 vehicle positions. No trip was CANCELED and no stop was SKIPPED in this sample. Vehicles reported `current_status` (STOPPED_AT 289, IN_TRANSIT_TO 216, INCOMING_AT 34), `stop_id`, `current_stop_sequence` and `timestamp`.
2. **Alerts** (Mercury JSON): 470 entities, 111 active, 10 active for the subway. 7 of the 10 had stop-level `informed_entity` entries (with `direction_id` on skipped-stop work); 3 were route-only delays, and none used "between A and B" wording. Mercury fields present: `alert_type`, `human_readable_active_period`, `affected_stations`, `created_at`, `updated_at`, `display_before_active`, `station_alternative` (key seen, empty), `clone_id`.

## Proposal *(proposal)*
- Honor SKIPPED stops and the feed's stop order; ride another line's track when the trip's stops are on it.
- Hold a train at its last known position when its ETA keeps slipping or it reports the same stop with no progress, with a Holding/Delayed tag; never move past a stop the feed has not cleared.
- Drop CANCELED trips.
- Attach active alerts to stations ('!' dot), the station and train sheets, and draw the affected segment when the data allows (2+ stops on a route, or a "between A and B" phrase where both stations resolve).
- A plain-English summary per alert from fixed rules, with the full MTA notice expandable. No model calls.

Builds on #178 (calm map: bus and train sheets, station sheet with alerts). No train-dwell PR was open at the time.


---

**Pukujan commented, 2026-10-25**

Everything this issue asks for is now on main, so I'm closing it.

- **Cancelled trips and skipped stops:** the server drops trips the feed marks cancelled and stops it marks skipped (`server/features/transit/routes.ts`).
- **Held trains:** a train whose arrival time keeps slipping, or that reports the same stop without moving, stays where it was last seen with a Holding or Delayed tag (`src/lib/transit/holds.ts`).
- **Where the problem is:** opening an alert draws the affected stretch, the stations it skips and any detour on the map (`alertFocus`, the `transit-alert-affected` and `transit-alert-skipped` layers). Lines with an unplanned delay or detour get a '!' badge.
- **Stations:** a station touched by a notice shows it at the top of its panel. A skip reads in red as "Not stopping here".
- **Plain wording:** the line panel's notices (`lineNotices.ts`) and the agent's answers (`trouble.ts`, `troubleSummary`) state what is wrong, which stations are skipped, where the shuttle runs and where to get back on, all from fixed rules with no model call. The MTA's full notice stays behind Full details.
