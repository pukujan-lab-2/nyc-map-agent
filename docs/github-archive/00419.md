# Issue #419: Live map: trains sit at stations instead of moving, the map hangs, exit panels overlap

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-25
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/419

On the live site (nyc.design-bakery.com, 2026-10-25 around 1:24 AM), trains look frozen, the map hangs in many places, and the exit help panel is drawn on top of another panel. A rider following a train sees it sit still, and the exit help covers the map without showing the exit.

## Symptoms
1. **Trains don't glide.** Subway trains sit at stations and jump every 30 seconds. Clicking a line doesn't make them move.
2. **Map hangs.** In many places the map stops responding for a while (owner report; where and for how long isn't measured yet).
3. **Exit panels overlap.** While following an F to Briarwood, the "YOUR EXIT" card (Queens Blvd & Main Street · SE corner, "Follow the station's exit signs", "About 98 m from the station centre", I'm out / Other exits) is drawn on top of the panel with Exit help / Other ways. Together they cover much of the left of the screen, and no exit marker or route is drawn on the map.

## Hypotheses

**H1: trains are pinned at their next station (#415).** Status: confirmed by reading the code and live data; not yet confirmed by a fix.
- In `estimateTrain` (`src/features/transit/model.ts`), a rule from #415 never draws a train past the stop the vehicle reports (`trip.at`). When that stop is the first one left on the train's list (`atI === 0`), the rule draws the train dwelling *at* that station even while the feed says it is still travelling there (`st` 2, IN_TRANSIT_TO) or arriving (`st` 0, INCOMING_AT).
- Would confirm: with the rule relaxed for that case, those trains glide between stations again.
- Would refute: trains still sit still with the rule relaxed.
- Experiment: a unit test with one `st: 2` trip whose `at` is its first stop. It should come out moving, short of that stop, not dwelling at it.

**H2: the hangs come from rendering, not the data.** Status: unverified.
- Would confirm: a performance trace on the live site shows long frames on the main thread during the hang (map redraw, the train layer, or a React re-render).
- Would refute: no long frames, with the hang lining up with a slow network request.
- Experiment: record a performance trace on the live site at a place that hangs; separately, check whether fixing H1 changes it.

**H3: the exit card doesn't replace the panel it opens from.** Status: unverified; seen once in a screenshot.
- Would confirm: opening Exit help mounts the exit card while the trip panel stays mounted underneath.
- Experiment: on the e2e build at 1280×800 and 375×667, open Exit help while following a train and list the mounted panels.

## Evidence
- **For H1 (confirmed):** in the live feed at 05:25:56 UTC, 116 of 252 subway trips report their first remaining stop as the stop they are travelling to (107) or arriving at (9). Those are the trains the rule pins. Another 88 really are stopped at that stop and are drawn correctly.
- **Against a data cause for H1 and H2:** `/api/transit-live` answered in 0.16–0.32 s, and the feed's timestamp matched the time of the request. `/api/transit/alerts` answered in 0.19 s.
- **For H3:** the owner's screenshot from the live site.
- **Counter-signal for H2:** the measuring tab closed in the middle of a 35-second frame-timing check, so there is no frame data yet.

## Caveat
H1 is the only cause found so far. The hangs aren't measured, and the overlap was seen once.

## Problems and gaps
- **Problems:** H1 makes the trains look frozen. H3 covers the map without showing the exit.
- **Gap:** there is no measurement of the hangs yet.

## Proposal *(proposal)*
None of this exists yet.
1. In `estimateTrain`, when `atI === 0` and the vehicle isn't stopped (`st !== 1`), keep the moving position and cap it short of that stop instead of switching to dwell. Add the test from H1.
2. Record a performance trace at a place that hangs, after fix 1 lands.
3. Make Exit help replace the trip panel instead of stacking on it. Draw the exit marker and the route on the map, as in the exit scene of #418.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A train travelling to its next stop is drawn between stations and moves smoothly between updates.
- [ ] A trace of the hang is attached, with its cause or a narrower question.
- [ ] Opening Exit help shows one panel, with the exit marked on the map, at 375×667 and 320 wide.

**Pukujan commented, 2026-10-25**

All three symptoms are gone on the live site. I rebuilt the hang case from the owner's report and it no longer hangs.

1. **Trains don't glide.** Fixed in #420. A train heading to the first stop left on its list now moves toward it instead of sitting there (hypothesis H1, confirmed).
2. **Exit panels overlap.** Fixed in #424. The exit card is now a short strip at the top left. It tells the screen layout it's open, so the line panel moves to the right instead of sitting underneath. Exit markers draw on the map at Jackson Hts-Roosevelt Av.
3. **Map hangs.** Not reproduced after #420, #421, #423 and #424. On nyc.design-bakery.com (2026-10-25, about 3:30 AM), I turned the states from the report on one at a time and counted long tasks, meaning main-thread work over 50 ms:
   - idle map, 15 s: none
   - live buses on, 15 s: none
   - an F trip from 34 St-Herald Sq to Briarwood, after the camera move: none in 10 s
   - following the F, then Exit help on top: one 86 ms task as the exit card opened, then none in 20 s

For H2, this is the refuting result: no long frames with everything on.

Caveat: this ran in a desktop browser on a Mac. A phone has a slower processor, so if the hang comes back on a phone, a trace from the phone at that moment is the next thing to get.

Closing. Reopen if it shows up again.
