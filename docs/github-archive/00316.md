# Issue #316: Following a bus or train hides delays, stops, and the station

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-23
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/316

While you follow a bus or a subway train, the card names the next stop and one delay line. Those words are not buttons. Upcoming stops are not listed. Notices are not listed. You cannot open any of them.

A train drawn on a station covers the station name and the dot you would press, so the station's issues stay out of reach.

The word Skipped on a station is not a button, so you cannot see which stops that trip misses, or the time if the alert has one.

Following a bus drops the bus symbol, so the route is only the 3D model.

Trains also sit at a station much longer than a real 30 to 45 second stop.

What should change:
1. While following, the delay, the upcoming stops, and the notices are visible, and each one is a button that opens the detail.
2. A train at a station does not cover it. The station can be opened.
3. Skipped is a button. It lists the stops that trip skips and the time, or says when there is no time.
4. Following a bus keeps a bus symbol above the bus.
5. If the motion model is what holds a train for minutes, stop doing that. If the live feed reports a long stop, leave that stop as the feed reports it.

Done when those five are true on the map, and the dwell tests show a train leaving after its dwell even when the stop is still listed.

Related: #297.

All five asks are on main.
1. While following, stops, delays and notices are buttons (`FollowCard` in `src/features/transit/TransitCard.tsx`).
2. A stopped train is drawn lifted off its station, so the station stays tappable (`STOP_LIFT_PX`, `src/features/transit/layerOrder.ts`).
3. Skipped opens the list of skipped stops and the time, or says no time is given (`SkipPlaces`, `SkipDetails`).
4. A followed bus keeps its badge (`transit-bus-badge-follow`).
5. A train leaves after its dwell even when the stop is still listed (`monotonic.test.ts`, "sits out the dwell, then leaves"). Since #430 it glides forward after a hold instead of jumping.
