# Issue #278: Sun-driven shadows in the 3D map and street view

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-22
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/278

Real NYC sun (suncalc, ?time=HH:MM / ?date= override) drives one shadow-casting DirectionalLight (PCF soft, 2048 / 1024 low-end, frustum follows the view target). Buildings, trees, trains and buses cast; ground and buildings receive. Warm at golden hour, fades below the horizon, softer under cloud. 'Shadows' switch in the Layers drawer (default on for desktop). Tracked in #274.
