Several things on the live map got in the way. At city zoom every train showed a full letter badge, so the map was cluttered. During a trip, the trains you weren't riding looked just as bright as yours. Picking Top or Aerial while following a walking route snapped straight back to the tilted street camera. "Use my location" turned on location but didn't move the map to you. The "Walking directions" card covered the middle of the screen, and in Lines you had to hit a small pill to see a line's notices.

## What changes

1. **Trains are small coloured dots below zoom 12** and fade into letter badges as you zoom in (`TransitLayer.tsx`, `mapContext/rules.ts`).
2. **During a trip, only your lines stay bright.** Other trains fade to 15%.
3. **The view you pick stays put while guiding.** The guide tilts the camera heading-up once when guidance starts. After that it only keeps you centred, so Top or Aerial sticks (`RouteGuide.tsx`).
4. **"Use my location" flies the map to you**, with the point placed above the phone sheet. On desktop, restroom results are framed clear of the side panel.
5. **No more A/B "Walking directions" card on home.** "Walk me there" and the walk card already cover that.
6. **Tapping anywhere on a Lines row opens that line's notices.**

## How to verify

- Type check and lint pass. The related unit tests pass (219 across transit, map context, live view, agent and components). Size limits pass.
- On a phone:
  - zoom out to see dots;
  - plan a trip to see only your line bright;
  - start "Walk me there", then pick Aerial and check it stays;
  - tap a Lines row.

## What stays the same

There are no server, database or secret changes.
