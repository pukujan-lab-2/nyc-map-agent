Most subway trains on the live map looked frozen. Since #415, a train still travelling to the first stop left on its list was drawn parked at that stop. In the live feed that was 116 of 252 trips. The assistant also sent the owner, at 1:36 am, to a playground restroom listed as open 8am to 4pm.

## What changes
- **Trains** (`src/features/transit/model.ts`): a train the feed reports as on its way to its first remaining stop keeps moving toward that stop. If it runs late against the timetable, it waits 150 m short of the stop until the feed says it has arrived. Stopped trains are drawn as before.
- **Restrooms** (`server/features/agent/hours.ts`): the city list's free-text hours are read in New York time. Places closed right now drop out unless nothing else is within 1.5 km. The model sees `openNow` and is told never to send someone to a closed one.
- **Fair use** (`server/features/agent/fair-use.ts`): every address gets one question per 5 seconds, permanently, against spam. The 20-per-3-hours window is off until 27 Oct 00:00 New York time so the judges can test without hitting it. It comes back on by itself, with no deploy needed.
- **More** in the answer card scrolls what it opens into view.
- **Locate button**: the crosshair from Google Maps instead of the paper-plane arrow.

## How to check
- `pnpm test`: all 1,155 tests pass. The two new train tests fail on main and pass here. New tests cover the hours parser, the closed-restroom case from the owner's screenshot, the cooldown, and the window switch-off.

## What stays the same
The daily token budget and its fallback to a cheaper model, held-train badges from #415, and the restroom layer on the map.

If the server's env file sets `AGENT_RATE_BURST_*` or `AGENT_RATE_WINDOW_*`, those values still win over the new defaults.
