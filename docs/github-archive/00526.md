**Problem:** on desktop, Map/3D/Street and the location button floated in the middle of the map. The line card was three loose pieces. Live buses did nothing over a creek or a park, with no way to choose routes, on desktop or on phones. Street view showed noise and crash dots plus a keyboard hint.

**What changes**
- **Map controls in one corner:** Map/3D/Street is a small vertical switch in the bottom-right corner, right above the location button. From 768 px they sit in the corner itself; narrower screens keep them just above the chat bar.
- **One line card:** the line name, notice count and ✕ head one panel, with the board, notices and actions under it on the same left edge. Two notices show before it scrolls (was about 1.5).
- **Live buses:**
  - If no stop is within about 300 m, it asks a ring of points about 700 m out.
  - If it still finds nothing, it says so ("No buses near here") instead of staying silent.
  - Under the switch: "Showing B24, Q59 · Change" opens nearby routes as on/off buttons, plus a box to add any route (like Q58).
  - On phones the same switches and chooser are at the top of the Settings sheet.
- **Street view:** the noise and crash dots are removed, and so is the "W/S move…" hint. One top bar shows "← Map", the street, what's ahead in smaller text, and Ask. The arrow pad sits just above the chat bar, not mid-screen.

**How to verify:** a new unit test checks that the wider bus search finds the B24 when only the ring point has a stop. Line panel tests are updated for the single card. Typecheck and lint pass.

**What stays unchanged:** the More layers bus picker, the phone cube view menu, and the follow and trip cameras.
