Following a bus or train showed one line, so the stops, the destination, and any delay or notice were not there to open.

The follow card now lists where the trip is going and the next stops, and it lists delays and notices. Each one is a button: a stop or the destination opens that place, and a delay or notice opens the alert. Follow stays on while that sheet is open. A train sitting at a station lifts clear of the station name, so the station can still be tapped. Tapping Skipped lists the skipped stops and the time. The bus you are following keeps its route badge above the bus, including in street view.

A train that sits too long at a station is pull request #329.

Refs #316

## Verify

Follow a subway train and a bus. The card shows "To" the destination, the next stops with a time, and a delay or "No delay reported", plus notices or "No notices". Tap a stop and the destination: the place opens and Follow stays on. Tap a delay when one is listed: the alert opens and Follow stays on. The followed bus still shows its route badge up close.

## Unchanged

Train motion along the track, the 3D vehicle models, which bus routes are on, and the all-JavaScript size cap.
