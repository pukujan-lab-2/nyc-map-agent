In the demo video, after planning tomorrow's trip in Spanish, the agent offers to remind you when to leave. The app couldn't. Anyone who tried it after watching would find that missing.

**What changes**
- A trip planned for later ("tomorrow at six") gets a **Remind me** button, both on the trip card and under the answer. Tapping it hands the phone a calendar event (`.ics`). The event starts at the trip's leave time, alerts 10 minutes before, and lists the steps ("Take the 4 from 14 St-Union Sq to Grand Central-42 St"). The phone's calendar keeps it, so it works with the app closed. Nothing is stored or sent by us.
- A short note confirms when the alert will come: "Saved to your calendar. It will remind you at 17:50, 10 minutes before you leave." The button and note follow the app language in all eight languages.
- For those trips the agent ends with "Want a reminder? Tap Remind me." instead of "Want to start?". It never says it set one itself and never suggests a different time.
- `docs/DESIGN_RULES.md` records the owner's decision as a narrow exception to "never tell people how to manage their time". A trip leaving now never shows the button.

**How to verify**
- `pnpm vitest run src/features/agent/reminder.test.ts server/features/agent/mapActions.test.ts`. The event has the New York start and end times, a 10-minute alert, escaped text, and lines that fit the calendar format's 75-byte limit. The button appears only for trips planned ahead.
- On a phone: ask "How do I get to Flushing tomorrow at 6 from Union Square?", tap Remind me, and the calendar offers to add the event.

**What stays the same**
Trips leaving now, the route on the map, and memory. No notification permission is asked for.
