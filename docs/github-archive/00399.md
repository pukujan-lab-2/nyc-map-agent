Testing the live site turned up a run of problems around answers, trips and following:
- the agent's primary button pointed at a place it had just called unrelated ("Show 3 YACHT CLUB CV, Staten Island");
- an old line panel and trip card stayed up after a new trip or question;
- every stop was read aloud during a ride;
- you couldn't zoom while following or previewing;
- Chase / Side / Overhead did nothing on the new home;
- buses vanished when you zoomed in to tap them;
- the answer sheet sat in the middle of the desktop map;
- trip steps were static text;
- Exit help asked the agent, which has no exit data.

## What changes

1. **Buttons only for places the answer names.** A "Show …" button needs the answer to mention that place (`answerNames` in `server/features/agent/mapActions.ts`, with tests).
2. **A new trip or question starts clean.** The line panel closes, following stops, and the old trip card goes.
3. **Quieter rides.** Only boarding, "your stop is next" and your stop are spoken; the stops in between still show on screen.
4. **One quick bar for views and zoom.** On the right, top to bottom: Change view, a zoom slider (+ / slider / −), the compass, Locate.
   - While following, Change view lists Map, Chase, Side, Overhead and Street (riding along, for buses too), plus Stop following.
   - Your zoom is kept on top of the follow and preview cameras.
   - The floating view row is gone.
5. **Buses stay visible when zoomed in.** Full-name bus badges no longer hide behind other labels from zoom 15.
6. **Answers dock left on tablet and desktop.**
   - Trip steps fold under "Steps".
   - Each step is a button that frames that stretch and marks "Board at …" and "Get off at …".
   - Weather shows °C and °F.
7. **Exit help opens the exit screen.** The underground feature is now on by default: station exits from the MTA entrance list, which covers every station. Its "I'm in the station" chip is hidden on the home, because Find exit and Exit help cover it.
