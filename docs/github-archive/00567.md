**Problem.** In street view, the "Pick a line" pill named stations far from where you were: "14 St-Union Sq station" next to the UN headquarters, and "Times Sq-42 St station" in Queens. Two causes:
1. It picked the station closest *on screen* to the tap, within 24 px. In a tilted street view, 24 px near the horizon covers kilometres, and points behind the camera can project anywhere.
2. It stayed open after switching views or after the app moved the camera, so a chooser opened at Union Square floated over a different street.

**What changes**
- A station counts only if it is within 120 m of the tapped ground and drawn on screen, as well as within 24 px of the tap.
- The chooser closes when you enter or leave street view, and after any camera move that takes the tapped spot off screen.

**How to verify:** tap where lines meet, then switch to street view or ask for somewhere else. The pill closes. In street view, the pill never names a far station.

**Unchanged:** a tap where the J, M and Z meet still asks which line, and the existing tests for that still run.

A new e2e test in `e2e/line-chooser.spec.ts` flies the map away from Myrtle Av and checks that the chooser closes and the layout holds.
