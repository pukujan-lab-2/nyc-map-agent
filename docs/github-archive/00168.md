# PR #168: Restrooms: show only the nearest few, not the whole city

- State: merged (2026-10-21)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-21
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/pull/168
- Branch `feat/restrooms-nearest` into `main`, +91 −4 in 3 files, merge commit 39f814a5d44d4ea0370faf832d17a7b1774a58ec

Turning on Restrooms drew every public restroom in the city, which buried the few a person could actually walk to.

**What changes**
- The city-wide list from the restrooms endpoint is fetched once and kept in the browser.
- Only the five closest to the map centre are drawn, and they are re-picked each time the map stops moving.

**How to verify**
Turn on the Restrooms chip around Union Square: five restroom markers appear near the centre; pan to another neighborhood and the markers follow. Screenshots at 1440 and 390 wide were taken over the browser debugging protocol with the city's open restroom list, and reviewed. A new unit test checks the five closest are kept, nearest first, and that a short list is handled.

**What stays the same**
The server endpoint and the restroom card are unchanged. Typecheck, lint, unit tests and full project-continuity and content-generation checks pass.

Closes #160
