# Issue #159: Transit Focus: dim the rest of the map so subway and bus stand out

- State: closed (2026-10-21)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-21
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/159

When someone is planning a trip by subway or bus, streets, labels and data layers compete with the transit lines, so the lines are hard to follow.

What should change: a Transit Focus chip that fades the street map and other layers and keeps subway and bus lines, stations and vehicles at full strength. Turning it off restores the map exactly.

Done when the chip toggles the effect, it survives a reload, and screenshots at 1440 and 390 wide show the difference.
