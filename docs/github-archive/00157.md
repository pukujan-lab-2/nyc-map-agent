# PR #157: Show the Manhattan skyline from zoom 11 in waterfront views

- State: merged (2026-10-21)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-21
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/pull/157
- Branch `feat/skyline-distance` into `main`, +213 −5 in 8 files, merge commit 18deb70f3f0827851ec1badf0473437447e27b33

From the DUMBO or Long Island City waterfront in a tilted view, Manhattan was flat streets until zoom 14, and then the towers popped in. Every building tile started at z14. The basemap's 3D buildings and the hero landmarks also start at z14, so the lower-zoom tiles the camera loads toward Manhattan had no buildings in them.

## What changes
- `pnpm build:skyline-tiles` (new script, run after `build:city-tiles`) pulls the 2,689 footprints over 60 m from NYC Open Data. Towers over 120 m start at z11 and the rest at z12. They go into a `skyline` layer at tile zooms 11-16, merged into `nyc-city.pmtiles` with tile-join. Tiny-polygon reduction is off for this layer, so slender towers aren't dropped at low zoom. The file grows from 113.9 to 114.3 MB.
- A new `city3d-skyline` layer draws the towers in plain colour (glass, limestone, other) from z11 and fades out at z14, where the existing buildings take over. It's on whether or not the textured city is on, and off in low-power mode.
- The layer deliberately has no `maxzoom`. MapLibre skips a layer in any tile whose own zoom is past the layer's maxzoom, and a pitched camera at z13 loads z14-16 tiles near the eye. My first try used maxzoom 14: distant towers showed, but Midtown right across the river stayed flat.
- The design is recorded in `.coord/proposals/0002-distance-skyline.md`. This PR also claims the skyline seat with `seat_protocol.py join` (`.coord/boss_claim.json`) and adds the agent and `check_in.issue_url` to the assignment.

## How to verify
The rebuilt tiles are already published (etag `7f82106264461f64b8f5e7223e3bad86`, 3 parts), so `/api/city-tiles` serves the skyline layer now. The old code ignores it. To check, tilt the camera from DUMBO toward Lower Manhattan at zoom 13.6, or from Long Island City toward Midtown at zoom 12.2 to 13.6: the towers are there from the first frame.

I took before and after screenshots with the camera on the waterfront (it was centred over Brooklyn before), shot only after the style and tiles finished loading:

| view | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| DUMBO, z13.6, pitch 80 | Lower Manhattan flat | Lower Manhattan towers |
| LIC, z13.6, pitch 80 | Midtown flat | Midtown towers |
| LIC, z12.2 | (no buildings) | Midtown and Hudson Yards visible |

## What stays the same
Textured buildings, props and landmarks at z14 and above, the tile pipeline itself, and every other layer.
