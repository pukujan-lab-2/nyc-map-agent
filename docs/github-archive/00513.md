**Problem:** where several subway lines meet (Myrtle Av on the J, M and Z), tapping the map opened whichever line happened to be drawn on top. There was no way to pick the line you meant, or the station itself.

**What changes**
- **One line opens straight away,** as before.
- **More than one line under the tap gets a chooser:** a row of line bullets, plus "Myrtle Av station" when the tap is on a station (`src/features/transit/lineChoice.ts` decides; `LineChooser.tsx` draws it).
- **Placement:** on a wide screen it sits just above the tapped spot and follows it as the map moves. On a phone it docks above the chat bar, so the spot stays in view.
- **Closing:** Esc, panning the map, or any other tap closes it.
- **Station dots tap more easily:** they now show and take taps from zoom 13 (was 14), slightly larger.

**How to verify:** tap Myrtle Av (J, M and Z) at zoom 13.5 and pick M; the M line opens. On a 375 px phone, the chooser sits above the chat, and "Myrtle Av station" opens the station card. Both cases are in `e2e/line-chooser.spec.ts`.

**What stays unchanged:** taps on a single line, vehicles, notices, and street view.
