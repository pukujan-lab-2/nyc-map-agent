**Problem:** in the trip preview, the marker came up at the exit and then jumped back to the middle of the station before walking on. **What changes:** the preview timeline (`src/features/agent/flythrough.ts`) joins each part to the one before it:
- the walk in stops at the chosen entrance;
- the stairs down meet the train where it starts;

- the walk out starts at the chosen exit;
- any other gap becomes a short straight step.

Turn and stop callouts are placed after the joining, so they still fire at the right spot.
