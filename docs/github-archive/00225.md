Closes #224. Build step 2 of the UI plan, stacked on #178.

The drawer and every details sheet now share one primary slot, owned by `PanelContext`.

**What changed**
- `layersOpen` left MapPanel.tsx. `PanelContext` holds the slot (drawer open, sheet minimized) through a small pure reducer in `src/lib/store/panelSlot.ts`, so the rules are unit-tested without a DOM.
- `showPanel` closes the drawer, so trains, buses, stations and entrances (TransitLayer) and restrooms, directories, schools and storefronts (MapView) all take the slot. A neighborhood or block pick does the same.
- Opening the drawer hides the sheet until the drawer closes, on phone as well as desktop. No more two bottom sheets stacked.
- **Minimize** on a sheet folds it into a labelled peek chip (bottom-left, ink on ivory, accent border). Tap to restore. Vehicle sheets label it with the route, e.g. "Q · live". There's no ETA on the clicked feature yet, so "2 min" waits for the vehicle data to carry it.
- Phone drawer is a plain non-modal bottom panel (60% max height) instead of vaul. vaul 1.1.2 doesn't pass `modal` to Radix, so its Root always made the page behind aria-hidden. The map above it stays tappable.
- Empty-map tap (nothing picked) calls `closeAll()`.
- Esc (capture phase) and browser Back go through `dismissTop()`: drawer, then sheet, then the active mode. Follow registers with `usePanelMode("follow", …)`. Its own Esc listener is gone; drag still stops it. Navigate can register the same way in #189.
- While a mode is active on phone, the sheet drops to peek and the drawer caps at 50%, so neither covers the top HUD.

**Tests**
- `src/lib/store/panelSlot.test.ts`: feature tap closes the drawer, empty tap, peek chip label, Esc/Back order.
- `e2e/panel-slot.spec.ts` at 390px: drawer and sheet never overlap, a vehicle tap closes the drawer (mocked live bus; trains go through the same click path), minimize/restore, empty tap closes both, Esc then Back.

**Screenshots** (box-local QA copies, Greenpoint sheet open, then Layers & filters): `/workspace/nyc-map-qa/slot-{before,after}-{390,1440}.png`. At 390 the drawer now leaves the top of the map free.
