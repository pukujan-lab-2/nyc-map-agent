**Problem.** On a tablet the line panel ran across the whole top of the map, and an open answer didn't tell the map to move. So you, a followed train or the place being talked about could end up hidden under a panel. On small iPhones a tall panel could cover the focus too, because each edge was only capped at 45% of the map. The answer card also showed sources and two follow-up buttons on the first view, which is more than someone just getting around needs.

**What changes**
- The line panel docks to the side from tablet width up (380 px, 400 px on desktop).
- An open answer or the voice orb is now a map cover. The map moves so its focus sits beside the panel.
- Covers always leave a clear strip of map: a quarter of it, and at least 160 px. On a small phone, both panels shrink their share and the focus lands in the gap.
- An opened notice in the line panel is capped at 30% of the screen height on short phones.
- The answer card shows the answer and one action. Sources, "What's nearby" and "Other options" are behind **More**.

**How to verify**
- Tablet (820 × 1180): open the R line. The panel is on the right and the map pads the right side (369 px). Ask a question: the answer docks bottom-left and the map pads the left side (385 px).
- iPhone SE (375 × 667): open the R line and a notice. The map pads 352 px at the top, so the focus is centred in the clear map below the panel.
