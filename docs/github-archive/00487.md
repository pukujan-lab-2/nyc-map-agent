**Problem.** On phones, the map's "i" credits button sat in the bottom-right corner, on top of the Center on my location button. With a pull sheet open, it showed through the sheet over the "Show the app's QR code" button.

**What changes** (CSS only, phones under 640px wide)
- The credits move to the bottom left, with the same 12px edge gap as the other controls.
- They ride above any pull sheet (follow, line, settings) through `--pull-sheet-h`, the way the locate button does, so they never show through a sheet.
- Closed, the credits box is exactly the size of the "i".
- Opened, the credits wrap short of the locate button, so the "i" that closes them stays reachable.

**How to verify:** use a 320×568 or 375×667 viewport. The "i" is at the left, above the chips. Tap it, and the credits open without reaching the locate button. Open Settings, and the "i" rides above the sheet.

**Unchanged:** wider screens, and the credits starting folded on phones (`foldCredits` in `MapView.tsx`).
