**Problem:** while a trip preview walks you to or from the subway, it never says which street you're on or what to look for, and reaching the destination just stops with no sign you've arrived.

**What changes**
- While a walk plays, a line under the step reads like "On 63rd Road · near Citibank, Mobil": the street under the marker, plus up to two named places within about 60 metres, nearest first. With none close by, it names a tall landmark within 400 m.
- The places come from the base map's named shops and places, so they work in the flat Top view too. The 3D storefront signs only load in the Closer and Street views. The exit helper doesn't use this option and behaves as before.
- On arrival the panel says "You've arrived at Aldi", with the street and nearby places at the destination, but not the destination itself. A few balloons drift up once; with reduced motion they're hidden.
- The map is read at most every 0.6 s and its loaded tiles re-read every 2.5 s, never on every frame.

**How to verify**
- New unit tests in `src/features/agent/walkPlace.test.ts`. The full suite passes (1005 tests).
- Local preview, "63 Dr-Rego Park station to Aldi on Queens Blvd" at 30×: "On Queens Boulevard", "On 63rd Road · near Pizza Hut, East Coast Realty", "On 97th Street · near The UPS Store, Citibank", then "You've arrived at Aldi · On 97th Street · near Gong Cha, Petco", with the balloons.

**Unchanged:** ride, drive and underground legs; spoken callouts; the exit helper's landmark lookup.
