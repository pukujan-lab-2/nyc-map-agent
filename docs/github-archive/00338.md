Opening a restroom only shows its details. A short walk from where you are stays a manual directions setup, so the camera never follows the route.

**Simulate trip**, on the restroom sheet, asks for a walking route from the current location to that restroom. The directions card still prints the on-foot time. When that time is under 20 minutes, the existing walk-along moves street view along the line. At 20 minutes or more, the walking route stays on the map and the walk does not start. A longer walk is not switched to transit or another mode.

Clicking the restroom still only opens the sheet. The camera moves only after Simulate trip, and only when the walk is under 20 minutes.

## How to verify

- `pnpm exec vitest run src/lib/map/restroomTrip.test.ts` — under 20 minutes walks; 20 or more does not auto-walk. That comparison uses the same rounded minutes the card prints as "about N min".
- Open a restroom and press Simulate trip. A short route walks street view. A route the card calls about 20 minutes or more draws the line and leaves the camera where it is.

## Unchanged

The walking router, Walk along route on the directions card, and the choice of what a longer trip should do. This does not build on the unmerged directions-from-here pull request.
