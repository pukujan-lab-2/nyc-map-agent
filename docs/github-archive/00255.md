# PR #255: fix(trains): live vehicles visible in every view, street level and X-ray

- State: merged (2026-10-22)

- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/pull/255

Fixes #254.

**Causes**
- Street level culled vehicles to `map.getBounds()`, which at walking zoom (~z23) and pitch (~86°) is a few metres wide, so nearly every train and bus was dropped. Street level now keeps vehicles within 600 m of the walker.
- Underground trains were filtered out at street level. They're still hidden by default, but a new **X-ray** toggle in the street-level header shows them as glowing cars 12 m below a semi-transparent ground. The street-level city layer draws its pavement after the vehicle layer, so in X-ray the vehicle layer moves to the top and clears depth.
- Ghost cars were always white: the instance colour buffer was created only after the shader had compiled. It's now created up front, so ghosts use the line colour.
- Tilted 3D hid every 2D route bullet and left only small 3D bodies, so trains seemed to vanish. Bullets now stay on in every view except street level (symbols draw above buildings); underground ones are drawn at 70% opacity.
- Boarded (followed) train: it always draws, and riding underground at street level turns X-ray on.
- Full bus badges took part in label placement, so moving buses knocked street labels in and out. They now ignore placement.

**Verified locally** (dev build against live APIs):
- Street level at Union Sq and Marcy Av: buses and trains within 600 m are drawn; X-ray glows in route colour.
- 30 s label sample at pitch 0, 50 and 72: 0 street labels toggled; train bullets stayed visible throughout.
