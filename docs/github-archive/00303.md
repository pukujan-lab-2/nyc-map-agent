# Issue #303: Stop following leaves x-ray on, and the M sits on the street at Middle Village

- State: closed (2026-10-23)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-23
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/303

Stop following a subway leaves the x-ray veil on, and the M train's open-air track at Middle Village is drawn on the street.

Riding an underground train turns x-ray on so the train stays visible. Pressing Stop following, or the train coming out into the open, leaves that veil up.

The Myrtle Avenue viaduct from Middle Village–Metropolitan Avenue toward Fresh Pond Road is tagged at street level for about the first 650 m. Trains there sit on the pavement instead of on the elevated track.
