**Problem.** A restroom card said where the restroom was, but not what it looks like. People had no way to recognize the spot from the street, or to check it on Google Maps.

**What changes**
- Tapping a restroom in any view (top, 3D or street, or from a chat answer) opens its card. The card now shows the nearest Mapillary street photo within 40 m, with how far away the photo was taken, the date and a link to the photo on Mapillary.
- An **Open in Google Maps** link opens the restroom's location in Google Maps, which has more photos and reviews.
- The photo loads only when the card opens, so the map itself stays photo-free.
- With no photo within 40 m, there's no picture and the card shows only the link.

**How to verify:** tap a restroom pin, check the photo and link, and confirm the link opens Google Maps at that spot.

**Unchanged:** the restroom pins, directions and Simulate trip.

The end-to-end test `e2e/minimap.spec.ts` now checks the link's address, that no photo shows when Mapillary returns none, and the card's layout.
