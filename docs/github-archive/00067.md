# Issue #67: Storefront signboards at street level (OSM shops, 3D facade signs)

- State: closed (2026-10-21)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-21
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/67

OSM shop=* / amenity=restaurant,cafe,bar,fast_food,pharmacy,bank… with names (Geofabrik NY extract + osmium, ODbL). DuckDB spatial snaps each to its nearest footprint edge (wall point, outward bearing, wall width) and the points are baked into the city PMTiles as a storefronts layer. A three.js custom layer (same pinned three as street signs) draws flat signboards on the facade (~0.8 m tall at ~3.2 m, 5 cm off the wall), canvas-text atlas, instanced quads, colored by category, eye level only within ~200 m. Data shaped for later procedural ground-floor storefronts.
