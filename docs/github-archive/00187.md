Station names were barely readable when zoomed in. Without a hover, the only names on the map were the basemap's faint gray transit labels, and they lost label collisions to street names, places and live buses. This adds a Station names switch and makes the labels bold and placed first.

Closes #186. Stacked on #178 (base `feat/calm-map`, itself on #173). It **builds on #178's station-label layer** and its shared bullets in `src/lib/transit/bullets.ts`. It does not add a second layer or a second bullet drawer. Do not merge before #173 and #178.

## What changes
- **Switch**: Layers & filters › Transit lines › **Station names**. It is on by default and saved in `nma.mapOptions` (`stationNames`). When it is off, labels show only for a hovered or open station, which is how #178 worked before.
- **Zoom tiers**: major complexes (4+ routes, or several platforms) show from z14 and every station from z15.
- **One label per complex**: platforms that share a name within 300 m are merged, so Atlantic Av–Barclays Ctr gets one label with 2 3 4 5 B D N Q R W instead of three labels.
- **Readable**: the name is bold ink at 12–15 px, to the right of the dot, with the bullet row under it. The halo uses the palette's land colour, so it works for Ivory, Ink, MTA muted, Neon and Auto's day, dusk and night. Ink is near-black on light palettes and near-white on dark ones.
- **Priority**: the label layer is drawn last, so it is placed first. Street names, places and moving bus badges give way to it. The wrench (planned work) and accessibility markers no longer take label space (`icon-ignore-placement`).
- **Duplicates hidden**: while the switch is on, the basemap's `poi_transit` layer drops rail and subway stations. Airports and bus stops are unchanged, and the ranked `poi_r*` layers stay with Places.
- **CI**: `.github/` is copied from main (#176) so this stacked PR runs on the self-hosted gravebuster runner. The files are identical to main.
