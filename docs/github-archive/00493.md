**Problem.** After #491, the live site drew the exit and the walk, but a which-exit answer still opened the full trip sheet. The sheet covers the lower half of a phone and hides the "Show my direction" compass hint. On a 320-wide phone, the place's label also ran under the north button. The agent instructions from #422 told it to call showRoute and openPanel right after `station_exit`.

**What changes.**
- **Agent instructions** (`server/features/agent/prompt.ts`): after `station_exit` the agent no longer opens the trip. It answers in one or two lines (the exit's corner and street, the minutes on foot), then asks "Want to start?". It opens the trip only on yes.
- **Labels** (`src/features/agent/exitLayer.ts`): the exit's and the place's labels now grow toward each other, so neither runs off the screen edge or under the round map buttons.
- **Framing:** because labels grow inward, the phone framing goes back to narrower sides (48 px left, 84 px right), so the walk gets more of the screen.
