# Issue #256: Buses skip and jump between 30 s GPS reports

- State: closed (2026-10-22)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-22
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/256

Problem: live buses only move when a new GPS report arrives (about every 30 s), then glide or teleport to the new spot, so they look like they skip and jump.

Proposal: estimate motion client-side: snap each bus to its route shape, dead-reckon along the shape at an estimated speed, ease into stops for a typical dwell, correct smoothly (never backward unless >150 m behind, then fade), and fade buses whose report is over 2 minutes old. Follow-up: learn per-segment speeds and dwell from history (like #214).
