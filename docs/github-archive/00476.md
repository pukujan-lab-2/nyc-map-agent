Ask for a walk, tap Use this route, then tap Stop: the walking cards go away, but the trip's dotted blue line stays on the map. It also stayed after closing the trip card or asking a new question, until another trip replaced it. During the walk it ran underneath the guide's own route, so two lines were drawn for one path.

## What changes

- `src/features/agent/tripLayer.ts` gets `clearTrip`, which empties the trip's map source.
- `src/features/agent/AgentOverlay.tsx` calls it whenever there is no trip on screen any more, which covers closing the card, a new question, and Stop. Use this route on a walk now hands over to walking guidance and drops the trip, because the guide draws its own route.

## How to check

1. At 320×568, ask "Walk to Times Square from Grand Central" and tap Use this route. Only the guide's route shows, not a second dotted line.
2. Tap the square Stop button. No route is left on the map.
3. Ask for a subway trip, then close its card with the X. The trip line goes too.

## What stays the same

Trips that ride a train or bus keep their line on the map while you use them. The trip preview, its Play button and walking guidance itself are unchanged.
