Elevated station dots sit on the street under the viaduct, and turning off Live buses leaves the buses on the map.

On a tilted map the station name lifts above the track, but the white dot stays on the pavement. That dot is the click target, so a tap on the station name misses and a tap on the dot hits the street underneath. Turning Live buses off stops the markers where they are. A selected route and its stops stay too.

In a tilted view, a station on raised track draws its dot with the name, and tapping the name opens the station. The flat map and the street mini map keep the dot on the station. Turning Live buses off removes the markers, the selected route, and a bus the camera was following. Trains stay.

To verify: tilt the map over the Astoria or Myrtle Avenue el and tap an elevated station on its dot and on its name. Turn Live buses on for one page, then turn it off. The badges, the route, and the stops leave the map.
