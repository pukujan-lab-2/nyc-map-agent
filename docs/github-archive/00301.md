# Issue #301: Live buses cover the map, and a replacement shuttle is not singled out

- State: closed (2026-10-23)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-23
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/301

The 6 train is not running between 125 St and Hunts Point Av. Free T102 shuttle buses cover that gap. Opening that alert should frame the stretch where the 6 is missing, draw the T102 route, and show the T102 buses. The rest of the bus fleet stays hidden.

Turning Live buses off leaves the buses on the map, and a silent visit loads the whole fleet. Buses should stay hidden until a person picks one corridor page: Queens, Queens–Manhattan, Manhattan, Manhattan–Staten Island, Brooklyn, Brooklyn–Manhattan, Bronx, Bronx–Manhattan, or Staten Island. Each page is 20 routes. Picking another page replaces the one that was on.

Asking for directions, and a trip from Ask the map, should show the bus lines that trip uses, including a bus line named only on an alternative.

The Lines button, the view controls, and the details sheet sit on top of each other on a laptop and on a phone.
