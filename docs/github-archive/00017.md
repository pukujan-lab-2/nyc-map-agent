This adds an opt-in textured 3D city for a pilot area (Lower Manhattan, DUMBO, Brooklyn Heights, Downtown Brooklyn, Park Slope). Turn it on from the existing map view (Layers) dropdown under **Textured 3D city**. There is no new button and no tooltip. Stacked on #16.

## Approach
- **Streets GL check**: Streets GL (MIT) is a full WebGL2 app with its own renderer and its own OSM tile pipeline. It isn't published as a library, so there's nothing to depend on, and forking it would bring in a second renderer, which AI_RULES rules out. I borrowed its ideas instead: a facade picked by building type, plain roofs, and low-poly instanced props.
- **Buildings**: NYC Building Footprints (`5zhs-2jue`, roof heights) joined to PLUTO (`64uk-42ks`: bldgclass, numfloors, yearbuilt). `scripts/build-city-tiles.ts` sorts each building into rowhouse/brownstone, walk-up, prewar mid-rise, modern, or glass tower, and flags storefronts (S/K classes, C7, D6, D7...). tippecanoe 2.53 packs it into one static `public/tiles/nyc-city-pilot.pmtiles`, read through the `pmtiles` protocol. No live queries.
- **Rendering**: MapLibre `fill-extrusion` throughout. Walls use `fill-extrusion-pattern` with the facade picked by the `facade` property. Storefronts get a separate 4.5 m ground band. A thin solid roof cap stops windows showing on roofs.
- **Props**, placed from real data and drawn as tiny extruded polygons (no glTF, no deck.gl):
  - Street trees (`uvpi-gqnh`): trunk plus a hexagonal canopy sized by trunk diameter
  - Hydrants (`5bgh-vtsn`)
  - Benches (`kuxa-tauh`)
  - Street-light poles (`tbgj-tdd6`). NYC doesn't publish a streetlight inventory. These are the poles reserved for small cells, so they're a real but partial set.
- **Level of detail**: textures and props draw only from zoom 16. Tiles stop at z16, so a view never holds more than the props in a few z16 tiles. While the 3D city is on, the basemap's own buildings stop at zoom 16 so the two don't z-fight. The module and pmtiles load only on first use.

## Numbers
- **Tiles**: 3.32 MB total for the pilot, holding 32,489 buildings (10,306 rowhouse, 17,025 walk-up, 1,822 prewar, 2,834 modern, 502 tower), 29,630 trees, 6,751 hydrants, 233 benches and 1,431 poles. The build takes about 55 s.
- **Bundle**: main chunk +0.4 kB. The lazy chunks are `city3d` (2.8 kB) and `pmtiles` (18.6 kB). The six 32×32 facade PNGs add up to about 2 kB.
- **Frame rate**: measured in headless Chrome on the SwiftShader software renderer, which has no GPU, so treat these as relative only:

  | View | 3D city on | 3D city off |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | 1440 px overhead, z16.6 | 8.9 fps | 11.9 fps |
  | 1440 px street level | 7.2 fps | – |
  | 390 px overhead | 22.5 fps | 21.5 fps |
  | 390 px street level | 17.6 fps | – |

  It needs a check on a real phone.

## Limits
- Pilot area only. Covering the whole city means rerunning the script with a bigger bbox. Expect roughly 100 MB or more, which would need hosting outside the repo, such as R2 or S3 plus a CDN.
- Outside the pilot area, the map shows no 3D buildings above zoom 16 while this is on.
- The dev build exposes `window.__map` for headless QA. Production builds don't.
