**Problem:** line sheets looked like they showed every notice twice. The MTA lists each elevator and escalator on its own, often one per direction, and the sheet made a card per unit with the same title, so the F line showed "Escalator out at Smith-9 Sts" four times. The Lines list also disagreed with the sheet: it counted alerts only, so the F said 3 notices while its sheet listed 19.

**What changes:**
- Outages group by station into one card: "2 escalators out at 34 St-Penn Station", "Elevator and escalator out at Bleecker St". Opened, it lists each unit as a row, an up/down pair as one "both ways" row, then the reason. A station with a single outage keeps its old card.
- The ring on the map says "No elevator" whenever an elevator is among them.
- The Lines list counts the same notices the sheet shows, from one shared builder (`allLineNotices`).
- The transit layers size budget goes from 23.2 kB to 23.8 kB (separate commit) for that shared count.

**Verify:** `pnpm exec vitest run src/features/transit/lineNotices.test.ts` and `pnpm build:e2e && pnpm exec playwright test e2e/line-notices.spec.ts`. The new browser test adds an up/down escalator pair, checks the 2 line says "2 notices" in the Lines list and in its sheet, and that the card opens to the "both ways" row.

**Unchanged:** MTA alerts, held-train notices, and tapping a notice to frame its station.
