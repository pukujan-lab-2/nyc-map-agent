There is no way to take the roads off the map, and a bus badge is about half the size of a subway bullet.

The street grid stays up when the map is being used for the subway and the buses. Simple streets only pales that grid. With one borough of buses on, the badges are still specks.

The Layers list has a Roads switch, and Map detail has Hide roads. Turning roads off removes the road paint and the road names. Water, parks, and the rail stay, and turning roads back on restores the simple or detailed streets you had. Bus badges grow to about the size of a subway bullet. A picked route is a little larger.

To verify: open Layers and turn Roads off over a street grid. The blocks go quiet and the colored lines stay. Turn Roads back on and the streets return. Turn on one borough of buses. The square badges read at about the same size as a train bullet.

Closes #309
