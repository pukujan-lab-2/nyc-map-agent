# PR #554: fix: service changes reach the rider in trip plans and line answers

**Problem.** The owner asked about the 7 to Flushing this weekend. The app answered in chat only: it didn't open the 7, didn't show the weekend work, and the trip planner said nothing about the 7's changes on that trip. Line questions only ever looked at right now, and the alert parser missed about 20 live MTA wordings across other lines (local skips, "[1] and [2]", "No [W] between …", platform changes worded the other way round). So the main thing the app exists to do, warn riders when service changes hit their trip, wasn't happening.

**What changes**
- **Trip planner.** Each subway ride's boarding, transfer and get-off stations, and any suspended stretch it rides, are checked against the changes in effect *at departure*. A trip that can't be made is ranked last and marked blocked. When a line has changes, a bus option is added. The plan says why it moved off a line.
- **Trip card.** The first impact shows in full and opens the line. A trip for another day shows the day. Blocked options are marked.
- **Line answers.** `line_status` takes a time ("this weekend" → Saturday 10:00). The answer opens the line on its trouble notice; on desktop this happens automatically. The map button now stays beside "Use my location" instead of being replaced by it.
- **Alert parser** (`src/lib/transit/serviceChanges.ts`) now reads:
  - skips worded "local skips", and skips naming two lines ("[1] and [2]");
  - a station closed outright ("No [7] at Flushing-Main St") and a closed direction ("No Jamaica-bound [F] service at …");
  - a suspension between two stations ("No [W] between …");
  - platform changes that name the station last ("board from the … platform at Burnside Av"), including the Staten Island Railway;
  - uptown/downtown, borough names ("Queens") and numbered labels ("8 Av") as directions.
- **Bus pole signs.** A new cache key, so stops cached before #553 also get destinations. "St-Broadway" is now capitalised correctly.

**How to verify.** Ask "any problem with the 7 this weekend? I need to go to Flushing". The 7 should open on its weekend notice. A trip for Nov 14 should say the 7 doesn't stop at Flushing-Main St and offer the bus.

**Unchanged.** Live delays, the step-by-step walking guide, and how routes are drawn.

Unit tests: 1,417 pass. The new cases use real feed wording.
