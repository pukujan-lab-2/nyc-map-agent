# PR #196: Follow train: ride the track, deck height on the el, x-ray underground

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-21
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/pull/196
- Branch `feat/follow-train` into `main`, +930 −9 in 20 files

**Problem.** Following a train kept the camera at one height: on the 7 in Queens or the J/M/Z over Broadway you looked up at the el, and underground the camera sat in the ground. The old Follow also re-centred on a straight-line tween, so it cut curves and jumped when the feed updated.

**What changes**
- `FollowTrain.tsx` (new): once you tap a train and press **Follow**, the camera rides the route shape. It sits just behind and above the train at deck height on elevated and at-grade track. Underground it rises into an x-ray view: the ground is veiled in the theme ink, buildings fade out over 0.9 s, and the line's tunnel and its stations are highlighted. Across a portal the camera and the fade blend together. A status line shows the bullet, the next station and minutes to it, with a labelled **Stop following** button (top centre on desktop, above the sheet on phones).
- `followCam.ts` (new, unit-tested): the eye and look-at point on the track, the underground share within 160 m of the train, and frame-rate-independent easing.
- `model.ts`: `trainProgress` / `trackPoint` split out of `estimateTrain` (same behaviour), so the camera can sample the track by distance.
- `TransitLayer.tsx`: 3 lines. The old straight-line follow now applies only to buses, and the layer mounts `FollowTrain` for trains.

**How elevated or underground is decided.** No new data. Each route-shape point in `public/transit/subway.json` already has a grade from OpenStreetMap `tunnel` / `bridge` / `layer` tags (`scripts/build-transit.ts`). `trackHeightProfile` turns that into deck heights. See `docs/follow-train.md`.

**How to verify.** Tap a 7 train in Queens and press Follow. Then try one between Court Sq and Hunters Point Av (the portal) and one in Midtown. The screenshots used a test-only 7 train injected by the QA script (a fetch wrapper; nothing in the app), so the train sits at the exact spot every time: elevated, portal and underground, at 1440 and 390 px wide.

**Unchanged.** Bus follow, Esc / drag to stop, the vehicle panel's Follow button, the station x-ray.

## Added in this update (Refs #201)

**Problem.** There was no camera between the flat map and eye level, X-ray only appeared inside a station card, buildings vanished when you looked up at street level, and at street level nothing showed where you were or which way you faced.

**What changes**
- **View chips** `ViewSwitcher.tsx` (new): labelled chips **Map · Elevated · Street** and an **X-ray** toggle next to them. The toggle reads "X-ray · auto" while a followed train is underground and the camera has turned X-ray on by itself.
- **Elevated** (`viewMode.ts`, new, unit-tested): the camera sits 60 m up, tilted 55°, and looks at the current centre.
- **X-ray toggle** (`xrayDressing.ts`): the follow camera's x-ray dressing has moved into a shared hook. With no train followed, the toggle veils the ground, fades the buildings and lights every tunnel and underground station.
- **Look-up fix** (`tallTileCover.ts`, unit-tested): MapLibre culled tiles using flat, zero-height boxes. Looking more than about 25° above the horizon therefore dropped every tile, and every building with it. At street level the tile boxes now take the height of the tallest building. **See through buildings** (`seeThrough.ts`, in the street strip) is an opt-in toggle that brings back the old faded look.
- **Mini map** (`StreetMiniMap.tsx` and `miniMap.ts`, unit-tested): a circular, heading-up disc at street level. It shows your position and a heading chevron, streets, nearby subway lines (dashed underground), stations and live trains, the walking route glowing, and an N marker on the rim, all in theme colours. Tap a point to stand there.
- **Size budget:** the all-JS budget goes from 932 kB to 937 kB (it was 4.84 kB over). The app entry chunk is still under its budget (525 of 535 kB).

**How to verify.** Press **Elevated**, then **Street**, and drag to look up at the Empire State Building. Turn on **X-ray**, and follow a 7 train under Midtown.

**Unchanged.** The follow camera's behaviour, the station x-ray card, and the walk controls.
