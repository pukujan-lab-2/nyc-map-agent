Closes #316

While following a bus or train, the card lists the delay, the upcoming stops, and the notices. Each one is a labeled button. A stop opens that station or bus stop without ending follow. Skipped, on the map and in the station sheet, lists the stops that trip misses and the alert time, or says there is no time when the feed has none.

A dwelling train's bullet and its tap target lift clear of the station. Station names paint above the 3D vehicles, so the name stays readable and the station still opens. The followed bus keeps a route badge above the bus, including when the 3D model is on top.

The motion model was pinning a train to the first stop still listed. The server keeps a passed stop for up to 10 minutes, so trains sat for minutes after a 30 second dwell. Progress is now capped at the stop not yet left: this platform during its dwell, then the next stop. A departure the feed itself reports still holds for that whole gap. Terminal and origin waits are unchanged.

Checked against the live subway feed (262 trips, 369 stops with a separate departure): median departure gap 30s, 90th percentile 240s, longest 420s. Two trains were inside a feed-reported hold (a 1 with a 240s gap, a 2 with a 360s gap). Those stays match the feed and are not shortened. Three other trips were stuck on a head stop already minutes past its time; that pin is what this removes.

Related: #297.
