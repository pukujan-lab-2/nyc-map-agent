## Problem

In street view the mini map and its pill said "Nearest restroom · 20 m NE", but no restroom sign stood in the 3D scene. The owner saw this at Trump Tower on the live site. The 3D restroom kiosks only read the overhead **Restrooms** layer, which is off by default, while the mini map reads the city-wide restroom list. So the two disagreed.

## What changes

- The 3D kiosks now read the same city-wide restroom list as the mini map (`loadRestrooms()`, fetched once per page). Public restrooms always get a kiosk in street view.
- Shop and café restrooms still follow the "Include shops & cafés" chip, as before.
- `kioskSources` drops its `restrooms` switch; `MapView` no longer sets it.

This is a different cause from the shop-signs bug fixed in #502 (map tiles dropping shop data up close). Restroom points never came from those tiles.

## How to verify

1. Open street view at Trump Tower (ask "street view at Trump Tower").
2. Face the Fifth Avenue atrium about 20–40 m away: a "Restroom · Anyone" kiosk stands there, at 1280 wide and at 375 wide.

Checked in the dev build at both sizes. Type check, lint, 1,328 unit tests, the size budgets (no limit raised) and `e2e/ask-street.spec.ts` all pass.

## What stays the same

The overhead map's Restrooms switch, the mini map, and the nearest-restroom pill are unchanged.
