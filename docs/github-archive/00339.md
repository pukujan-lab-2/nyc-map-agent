# PR #339: fix(map): show Layers filters on the street-view mini map

- State: closed (2026-10-25)

The street-view corner map ignored Layers. A filter that was on drew its places on the big map and left the inset as streets, parks, water, subway lines, stations, and live vehicle dots. Turning a filter on or off while street view was open did not change the inset, because the inset copied its style once when it opened.

The inset now draws the point layers those same switches already use, for places inside the corner map, and removes them when the switch is turned off. Changing a filter while street view is open updates the inset in place. The inset stays a flat north-up map.

Switches mirrored:

- Places, including Businesses, Health, Parks & places, and Citi Bike
- Bus stops
- Restrooms
- Include shops & cafés, only while Restrooms is on
- Parking icons (not the street blade signs)
- Events
- Ferry terminals and vessels
- Schools

How to verify: turn Restrooms on, enter street view, and confirm the corner map shows the restroom icons in its view. Turn Restrooms off and those icons leave. Toggle another point layer above while street view stays open. Subway lines, stations, and the live vehicle dots stay. `src/lib/map/miniStyle.test.ts` checks which switch keeps which layer.

Street art, x-ray, roads, traffic lines, and weather are not flat point layers those switches draw, so they are unchanged. The Show map button is unchanged.

Closes #334
