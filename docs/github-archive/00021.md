**Approach: MapLibre 5 camera, not deck.gl.** maplibre-gl 4.7.1 → 5.24.0 (BSD-3-Clause), react-map-gl 7.1.9 → 8.1.3 (MIT, `react-map-gl/maplibre` unchanged). MapLibre 5's `calculateCameraOptionsFromTo(from, alt, to, alt)` places the camera 1.7 m above the pavement aiming at a point 30 m down the street (pitch 86° default, z≈21.3). That is ~15 lines of glue in `eyeCamera()` and keeps one canvas/one render pass; deck.gl FirstPersonView would mean a second renderer and re-rendering the same extrusions, which AI_RULES forbids. (Aiming via `calculateCameraOptionsFromCameraLngLatAltRotation` was rejected: it puts the map centre km away below ground, so tiles load at z13.)

- Walk loop drives an eye pose {lon, lat, bearing, pitch}; W/S move, A/D turn, drag (mouse/one finger) looks around, pitch 55–115°. Map drag-pan and scroll-zoom are off while walking; maxPitch 120 / maxZoom 24 only while walking, overhead unchanged (85).
- Walk-along-route follows at eye level with smoothed bearing at corners.
- Basemap labels hidden at eye level; sky/horizon from existing `applyAtmosphere`.
- Textured 3D city: tile URL `VITE_CITY_TILES_URL` (default `/tiles/nyc-city.pmtiles`), `pnpm build:city-tiles` builds all five boroughs (~82 MB, gitignored; streamed write fixes a RangeError). Street level turns the textured city on by default (explicit Layers choice wins). Basemap extrusions now only step aside where textured buildings are loaded near the camera, so missing tiles never blank the street.
