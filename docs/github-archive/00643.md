### Summary

On the live map, subway trains look parked in stations far longer than a real train stops. A train is drawn standing at a station for as long as the vehicle feed keeps saying "stopped at" it, with no time limit, so about 34% of trains are drawn parked when the timetable alone gives about 12%. Real subway stops last 30 seconds to a minute.

### Identity and Lineage

Repository Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent. Code: `estimateTrain` in `src/features/transit/model.ts` (the block that never draws a train past the stop the vehicle reports) and the call in `src/features/transit/TransitLayer.tsx`. Related: `src/lib/transit/holds.ts` (held-train detector), `src/features/transit/dwell.ts`, #594 (estimates never scored against outcomes).

### Acceptance Criteria

1. A train is held at a station by the vehicle report for at most 60 seconds after the report's own timestamp, unless its line is in trouble.
2. Trouble means the feed-based held-train detector flags the train, or the route has an active delay or detour alert; the report is trusted without a limit there.
3. A unit test covers a fresh report, a stale report, a troubled line, and a train the timetable already has at the station.
4. The share of trains drawn parked drops from about 34% toward the timetable's about 12%, measured on the live feed.

### Boundaries and Next Action

In scope: the pin rule in `estimateTrain` and its caller. Out of scope: the default dwell lengths, the held-train detector, buses. Next action: open the pull request, then re-measure the parked share on the live feed after it deploys.

Owner decision, 2026-11-01 (in chat): a max timer on stops unless there is trouble, because subways stop 30 seconds to a minute and are mostly moving.

### Symptoms

1. **Mostly parked:** most subway bullets sit at stations on the live map.
2. **Measured:** on a live feed sample of 480 trips, 12% are parked by the timetable model; 22% more are pinned by the vehicle report; 34% in all.
3. **No limit:** the pin lasts until the next vehicle report; the median report is 29 s old, the 90th percentile 71 s.

### Hypotheses (unverified)

H1. The pin rule causes the extra 22%. **Status:** confirmed on one sample. **Would confirm:** capping it drops the parked share toward 12%. **Would refute:** it stays near 34%. **Experiment:** rerun the measurement script on the live feed after deploy.

### Counter-signal

Some pinned trains really are stopped (a train waiting at a signal), and the feed's report is the only evidence; the cap lets them look like they are moving for a short time. The exception for trouble keeps the genuinely held ones.

### Honest Caveat

One feed sample at one moment, using the default dwell times and no history blend, so the exact shares will differ.

### Problems vs Gaps

Problem: pin without a limit. Gap: no measure of how far drawn positions are from real ones (#594).
