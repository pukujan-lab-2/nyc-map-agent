Street view was missing the bathrooms on the block you are standing on, and turning your head or walking made the view stall.

The WC icons stay hidden while you walk, and the 3D kiosks are what you see instead. Those kiosks draw from the restroom source, which held only the five closest public restrooms in the city and rewrote them on every camera step. At street zoom (17 and closer) the source now keeps public restrooms within 300 m, at most 24. Neighbourhood zoom still shows the closest five. The source is left alone when that set has not changed.

MapLibre fires moveend on every jumpTo, and a walk or a look calls jumpTo every frame. Street-name signs, storefronts, hydrants, and the restroom list were rescanning loaded tiles on that event. They now rescan after the camera has moved about 15 m, when the zoom crosses their cutoff, or when the tiles reload.

## Verify
