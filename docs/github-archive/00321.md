# PR #321: fix(map): stop 3D trains flashing, sticking after Follow, and riding under the el

## Problem
A tilted train flashes, a second 3D model stays put after Follow starts, and the subway model still reads as a generic train. At Flushing–Main St and Harlem–148 St an above-ground train is drawn as an underground ghost, so it does not sit under the raised station marker.

## What changes
- Vehicle meshes load once. Follow drops street level and the chase pitch removes and re-adds the layer; the same meshes are reused, and every update hides the inactive set, so no frozen copy remains.
- Solid stand-in boxes are unlit. The subway glTF emissive atlas is cleared, so a turning car does not strobe.
- The glTF is drawn only when 3D buildings are on and eco is off. Otherwise the train is a route-colored box, the same idea as the underground boxes. Top view does not turn the model on.
- Flushing–Main St (7 and 7X) and Harlem–148 St (3) are hand-placed viaducts at 8 m. A short underground pocket at a shape end that opens onto open track (St George, 374 m) takes that neighbor's grade. The 7's East River and Manhattan tunnel stays underground.

## Click target
A click from right above an elevated train hits MapLibre `transit-station-raised` (also `transit-station` and `transit-station-label`). Those layers live in TransitLayer and are unchanged. This PR only places the three.js body on the viaduct. A click on the white station dot still opens the station.

## How to verify
- Tilt the map with 3D buildings off, or open with `eco=1`: trains are route-colored boxes.
- Turn 3D buildings on, tilt, and follow a train from street level: one model moves with the train and does not flash as it turns.
- At Flushing–Main St or Harlem–148 St the train sits on the el.
- Top view stays flat.

## Unchanged
- TransitLayer, including the station click layers.
- No GPU quality ladder, and Top view does not enable the 3D vehicle layer.
- Street and x-ray radii. Bundle caps.

Closes #319
