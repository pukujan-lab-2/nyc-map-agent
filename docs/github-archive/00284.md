# Issue #284: Stop following leaves the camera stuck in the chase view

- State: closed (2026-10-22)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-22
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/284

Stop following leaves the camera stuck in the chase view.

Tapping a train or bus drops the camera onto that vehicle, low and tilted. Stop following only lets go of the vehicle, so the map stays nose-down on the tracks. That feels like the wrong place to land.

Stop following should ease back to the view from just before the follow started. Dragging the map, or Esc, still just lets go wherever the camera is, so you can look around the train and stay there.
