In the demo video, the exit scene shows the exit on the map and the walk to the entrance. The trip scene marks the change at Grand Central and a skipped station. Ours didn't. Exits sometimes never drew, because the code waited for a map "load" event that had already fired. There was no walk from the exit, and the route didn't say where to change trains.

## What changes

- **Exits always show.** They draw straight away and are put back once the map settles if a style change removed them.
- **Walk from your exit.** A dotted blue line runs from the chosen exit to where you're going, along the trip's own street route. It appears only at the final station, not at a transfer.
- **The exit card makes room.** It tells the screen manager it's open, so the answer card and the follow card step aside.
- **The route says where to change.** A label such as "Change to the 7 · Grand Central-42 St" sits at the transfer, and a red dot with "Skipped" sits on each station the trip's lines skip. Both labels follow the app's language (8 languages).
