# Issue #418: Make the app do what the demo video shows

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-25
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/418

The demo video shows the app as we promise it. The live app doesn't do all of it yet, so anyone who tries the app after watching will see a gap. This issue lists what is left to make the app match the video, one scene at a time.

The video is generated, not a screen recording. It is drawn on real map images from the app, with generated voices and AI-generated people in the opening and ending. The hackathon writeup now says so (`docs/HACKATHON_WRITEUP.md`, "About the demo video").

## What the video shows, and what is left

### 1. Event: "anything fun at Bryant Park tomorrow?"
Video: one web search, a pin on the exact place, and an answer that ends "Want me to plan how to get there?"
- [x] Use Tavily as the main web search and TinyFish as the fallback. Both keys are in the owner's env file, and production keys are approved.
- [x] Pin the place the answer names, not just the area around it.
- [x] End event answers by offering the way there.

### 2. Exit: "which way out for the Empire State?"
Video: the right exit on the south side of 34th St, an arrow for where the phone is facing (with a compass-calibration step), a walking route to the 34th St entrance, and a small answer card that leaves the map visible.
- [x] Show the exit at its real place on the map. An earlier run showed it in the wrong place.
- [x] Add a facing arrow from the phone's compass, with a "calibrate" prompt when its readings are poor. This was started and stopped partway.
- [x] Draw the walking route from the exit to the entrance.
- [x] Keep the answer card small on phones (375×667 and 320 wide), so the person, the exit and the route stay visible. Today the exit answer covers the whole phone screen.

### 3. Trip, in Spanish: "¿Cómo llego mañana, como a las seis?"
Video: the 4 from Union Sq, a change to the 7 at Grand Central, a delay warning at Grand Central with a backup train on the same platform, a skipped station marked, rain advice (umbrella and jacket), an offer to remind tomorrow, and Spanish labels.
- [x] Show the transfer as its own step on the route.
- [x] Put the delay on the station where it happens, with what to take instead.
- [x] Mark skipped or closed stations on the route.
- [x] Turn the weather at the trip time into what to bring.
- [x] Offer a reminder before leaving.
- [x] Translate the route chips and labels. Translating the menu row is PR #417.

### 4. Restroom, in Chinese, hands-free
Video: the "hey map" wake phrase, the question in Chinese, then the way there by landmarks ("along 42nd St, on your left, next to the library") ending "want to start?", with Chinese labels.
- [x] Give the way there by landmarks, not just the restroom's name or park.
- [x] End with "want to start?" and start the walk on yes.

### 5. Every answer
- [x] Change the agent prompt so every answer says how to get there, using landmarks, and ends by asking to start. Never just name where a place is.

### 6. Map camera
- [x] Use a fixed top-down view instead of the shaky camera that follows behind the person. The fix is committed locally as `b3a67a8` on branch `fix/overhead-camera`; it is not pushed and has no pull request yet.

### 7. The video itself
- [x] Re-cut the video if a scene changes after these fixes. The scripts in `.demo/gen/` (`timeline.py`, `page.html`, `render.mjs`, `assemble.py`) rebuild it in about 5 minutes.

- [x] On 375×667 and 320-wide screens, the person and the answer's place stay visible beneath the answer card.

**Pukujan commented, 2026-10-25**

We went through the demo video scene by scene and compared it with the live app. Most of the gap came from what the agent said, not the map. The video answers with the route first and then asks "Want to start?". Ours answered with facts about the place and stopped there. It also didn't know you were underground, couldn't find events at a venue, and gave restroom answers without directions.

Three merged pull requests close most of that:

1. Answers now read like directions and end with "Want to start?" ([#422](https://github.com/Pukujan/nyc-map-agent/pull/422)). Every place answer says how to get there. A restroom answer comes with the walking route and its turns. "What's on at Brooklyn Bridge Park" searches event listings, using Tavily first and TinyFish as the fallback. The weather turns into what to bring.
2. The agent knows when you're underground ([#422](https://github.com/Pukujan/nyc-map-agent/pull/422)). The app tells it whether you're riding or at a station and which exit you picked, so "where do I go now" gets an answer about your exit.
3. The exit and trip scenes look like the video ([#424](https://github.com/Pukujan/nyc-map-agent/pull/424)). The exit card is a small strip instead of a full-screen sheet. Exits redraw if the map drops them. A blue dotted line runs from the exit to where you're going. The route marks where you change trains ("Change to the 7 · Grand Central-42 St") and any skipped stops. A delay is mentioned at the station where it happens. The camera no longer jumps while you follow a trip ([#423](https://github.com/Pukujan/nyc-map-agent/pull/423)).

Four things are still open, and each one needs a decision from the owner:

- The reminder offer in the video ("I'll remind you when to leave") goes against the rule that the app never tells you when to leave. Which one wins?
- Some route chips are still in English only.
- The agent runs on `cb/deepseek-v4.1-flash` with thinking off. A stronger model would follow the route-first rules more reliably, but it costs more.
- The video could be re-recorded against the live app once the points above are settled.

For production, `TAVILY_API_KEY` has to be added to the server's `.env`. Until then, event search falls back to TinyFish.
