**Problem.** At street level, the subway entrance at Nassau Av looked like a grey box with railings on top, and its name ("Nassau Av · G / Stairs · Manhattan Av…") was cut off behind the sign and globe lamps. Bus stop flags listed routes (B43, B62) but not which way those buses go, so you couldn't tell if you were on the right side of the street.

**Causes**
- The stair kit drew its stairwell walls and steps below street level. The map's ground doesn't hide anything, so the whole stairwell showed through as a box.
- On every map move, the 3D vehicle layer (which also draws the entrances) is moved above the layers it doesn't list as "keep above". The stair name wasn't on that list, so the kit painted over it.

**What changes**
- The stair and elevator kits now stand on the 0.5 m street-level pavement. Nothing is drawn below it: the opening is dark, with step edges that fade into it.
- `transit-entrance-label` joins `ABOVE_VEHICLE_LAYERS`.
- `/api/transit/bus-stops` now returns `heads` per stop, for example `{ B43: "Greenpoint Manhattan Av" }`. It comes from Bus Time's `stops-for-route` stop groupings, cached for 24 hours per route. Flags with up to three routes print "to …" under each route.

**How to verify**
- Unit tests cover the destination wording and the `heads` mapping.
- After deploy: street view at Nassau Av and Manhattan Av, and the bus stop flags there.

**Unchanged:** overhead entrance badges and the bus stop tap card.
