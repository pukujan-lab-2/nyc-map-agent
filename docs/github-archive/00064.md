Closes #63

Root cause: `eyeCamera` aimed at a point 30 m ahead *raised or lowered* to match the look angle, but MapLibre keeps the map centre clamped to the ground, so the camera altitude drifted: −11.5 m after looking up (black lower half, ground seen edge-on as a pale band), +16.6 m after looking down. Fix: aim at the ground point the look ray hits (`1.7 m × tan(pitch)` ahead), and clamp look pitch to 70°–89° (above 90° no ground point exists; below 70° the needed zoom exceeds MapLibre's z24). Measured headless at Bed-Stuy (-73.9235, 40.6845): altitude stays 1.70 m at 70°, 86° and 89°. Walking forward 6 s moves ~65 m along the street on desktop and mobile; there is no movement clamp in the walk loop (the snap only runs on entry), so the 'stuck' feel was the underground camera.
