**Problem.** On a small iPhone, picking a line (for example from My train) opened a panel across the top half of the map. It covered the line you had just asked to see, and it hid the quick buttons.

**What changes**
- **On phones, a picked line uses the same pull-up sheet as following a train.** The sheet opens one step up with Other ways and Follow a train first, then the next trains and the line's notices. Swiping down, or tapping the grip, shrinks it to one line above the chat: bullet, line name, notice count, X.
- **The quick buttons stay at the top right** (Change view, zoom, north) while a line is open on a phone.
- **The sheet is now shared.** It moved into one component, `src/components/PullSheet.tsx`, used by both the follow sheet and the line sheet. That includes the gestures, the grip, the height published to `--pull-sheet-h` so the locate button rides above the sheet, and hiding locate while the sheet is open.
- **Phone or wide now comes from one place.** The line panel and the quick buttons read the app's existing screen-size state (`useScreenSize`), so they always agree on which layout to show.

**How to verify**
- `pnpm exec vitest run src/features/transit/linePanel.test.tsx` checks that the phone gets the sheet, the panel folds to a pill when an answer is open, and wide screens keep the side panel.
- By hand on a phone: My train → a line → swipe the sheet down and up → Follow a train.

**Stays unchanged**
- Wide screens (640 px and up) keep the side-docked line panel.
- On a phone, when an answer is open the line still folds to its pill at the top.

**Evidence.** Headless iPhone screenshots at 320×568 and 375×667 show the line visible above the sheet at each step, and Follow a train hands over to the follow sheet. Unit tests all pass: 1212 passed, 8 skipped. Typecheck and lint are clean.
