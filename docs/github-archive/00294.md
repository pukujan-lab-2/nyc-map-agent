# Issue #294: X-ray darkens far more than the nearby tunnel

- State: closed (2026-10-23)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-22
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/294

The X-ray patch is far too big.

On the tilted map it grows with zoom, from 600 m at street level up to 6 km overhead, so a dark oval covers neighborhoods you are not looking at. On the flat map the same switch darkens the whole city. Underground trains are only useful for the tunnel right beside you.

The see-through ground should stay about 250 m across every view, centered on where you are looking.
