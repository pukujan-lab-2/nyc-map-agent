# PR #327: feat(map): save home and work and let Ask set filters

- State: closed (2026-10-25)

There is no saved home or work place, and Ask the map cannot change the filters a person names. A trip that starts at home, or a request to hide the roads, has nowhere to land.

Layers and filters gains a Home and work section. Save here stores the map center with the neighborhood name. The saved pill flies back to that spot. A Home or Work pin appears on the map, and a tap opens its sheet. The spots stay on this phone.

Ask the map receives those spots with the question. The words home and work work in the existing tools, including a trip plan. A new filter tool changes only the layers the person named: roads, bus stops, street names, buildings, streets, subway lines, restrooms, x-ray, transit focus, eco, the textured city, events, parking, traffic, weather, ferries, live trains, the theme, and place icons such as restaurants or parks. Asking to show live buses opens the line picker and leaves the rest of the fleet off.

## How to verify

1. Open Layers and filters, tap Save home here, then tap the Home pill. The map flies to the saved spot.
2. Zoom out until the Home pin shows, tap it away from a subway line, and confirm the sheet names Home.
3. Ask the map to hide roads, or to plan a trip from home to work after both are saved.

## Unchanged

Live buses stay off until one page is picked. A tap on a train or bus still opens that vehicle. Landmarks, the follow sheet, the colored train box, and the phone chrome stay in their own pull requests.
