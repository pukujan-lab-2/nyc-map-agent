On phones, people could not close the exit card or the trip card. While following a train, changing the view often stopped the follow instead. The close X on those cards was a faint, see-through circle about 10 px from the view and zoom buttons. On iPhones the exit card also covered the lower part of its own X, and the trip card stacked under it with a second X in the same spot. So a tap could land on the wrong control.

## What changes
- **Close buttons:** solid, plainly visible, and drawn above the card they close (exit card, trip card, follow card).
- **A wide gap on the right:** cards and the top menu bar now end 104 px from the right edge (was 80 px), so they no longer crowd the view and zoom buttons.
- **One card at a time:** the exit card starts below its header. The trip card waits while station help is open, so the two never stack.
- **Top menu bar:** one boxed strip with every icon at full strength (they used to fade out and looked broken). Swipe it, or tap the new › button to slide to the rest.
- **QR code:** a small QR button under "Center on my location" opens a scannable code for https://nyc.design-bakery.com. Tap outside it or press Esc to close.

## How to verify
- At 390 px or 414 px wide, open Find exit or a trip preview: the X is a solid dark circle and one tap closes the card.
- Follow a train, tap Change view, and pick Chase, Side or Street: the follow stays on.
- Swipe the top bar, or tap ›, to reach Settings. Tap the QR button to see the code.
