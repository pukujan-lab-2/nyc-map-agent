### Non-Binding Proposed Next Actions

*(proposal)*

**Fair-run rules:** one phone, one starting spot, same day and time for every app; each task run twice and screen-recorded. Competitor apps are tested by hand. If possible, 3–5 people from the target group each try 5 tasks, and their scores count more than ours.

**Scored per task:**

| Measure | How |
|---|---|
| Right answer | 0 wrong, 1 partly, 2 right |
| Effort | Taps and typed words |
| Time | Seconds to the answer |
| No signal | Works in airplane mode, yes or no |
| Language | Works fully in Spanish, Chinese or Bengali |
| Plain words | A newcomer rates 1–5: "I understood what to do" |
| Ads | Paid pins or offers seen |

**Tasks (17):**

- *Trips:* (1) now: Jackson Heights–74 St to Bryant Park, weekday 8am; (2) later: Flushing–Main St to Coney Island, Saturday 10am, with that weekend's service changes; (3) no stairs: Grand Central to Barclays Center, with today's elevator outages.
- *Disruptions:* (4) "Is the M running this weekend?", answered plainly; (5) explain a real MTA notice from the wall, in Spanish; (6) which elevators and escalators are out on the 7.
- *Underground, airplane mode:* (7) at 34 St–Herald Sq, which exit for the Empire State Building; (8) just came up: which corner, which way am I facing; (9) on the wrong-direction train: does the app notice.
- *Places:* (10) nearest open public restroom from Union Square; (11) a landmark by description ("the big library with the lion statues"); (12) when asked, "a pharmacy open now near me" (kept in to be fair; Google will likely win).
- *Language and first use:* (13) task 1 entirely by voice in Bengali; (14) first open: time to the first useful answer, and whether an install or account is needed.
- *Rehearsal:* (15) practise a trip ahead and list what you'll need (payment, weather, a restroom on the way).
- *Trust:* (16) count ads and offers across all tasks; (17) when location is sent, and whether an account is needed.

**Output:** one table of apps against tasks plus a short "where we lose" list, which becomes the next round of fixes. Rerun before the demo and monthly.
