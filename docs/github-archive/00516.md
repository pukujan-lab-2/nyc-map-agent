**Problem.** In street view, the 3D subway entrances (stairs and elevators) carried no words. The owner wanted each entrance to say where it is and what it is, so you can tell which stair to take.

**What changes**
- **A label over each of the nearest entrances, corner first.** Line 1 reads like a street sign: "6 Av & W 47 St · SE corner". Line 2 says what it is: Stairs, Escalator, Elevator, or "Elevator · out of service" when an elevator outage covers that station.
- **The station name stays once per station.** The station's name and line bullets already stand over the station at street level, so they are not repeated on each entrance.
- **Only the nearest entrances get labels:** 6 on wider screens, 3 on a phone. On a phone, an entrance farther than 60 m shows only the corner line until you get close. Two stairs on the same corner share one label.
- **The labels draw above the 3D storefronts and kiosks,** which would otherwise hide them.

**How to verify**
1. Open street view at 47–50 Sts–Rockefeller Center and look south-east down 6 Avenue.
2. Expected: "6 Av & W 47 St · SE corner / Stairs" over the stair at that corner.

I checked this on the dev build in a headless browser at 1280×800 and 375×667, with #514 applied locally. The label above shows over the stair. On the phone, the far entrances keep to one line. All 1,337 unit tests pass, including four new ones for the label text. End-to-end tests run in CI only.

**Depends on #514.** Street names reach street level only after #514 (street-level tile fix) merges. Until then, labels fall back to the kind alone ("Stairs").

**Not in this change**
- "Exit only" and "Uptown only" are not shown yet. The MTA entrance data has `entry_allowed` and `exit_allowed` fields, but the transit build script (`scripts/build-transit.ts`) doesn't fetch them. Adding them means rebuilding `public/transit/subway.json`. That is a separate data change.
- Line bullets drawn as icons on the entrance labels are also not included: the station label above the station already carries them.
