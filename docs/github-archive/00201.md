# Issue #201: Camera views: Elevated view, labelled view chips with X-ray, solid buildings when looking up, street-view mini map

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-21
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/201

**Problem.** Three street-level and camera problems: (1) there is no middle camera between the flat map and eye level, and X-ray only appears inside a station card, so people cannot find it; (2) looking up at street level makes buildings vanish; (3) at street level you lose track of where you are and which way you face.

**Symptoms**
1. **No elevated view.** The only choices are the overhead map and the street camera.
2. **X-ray hidden.** Turning on the see-through view needs a station card.
3. **Buildings vanish when looking up.** More than about 25° above the horizon, every 3D building disappears.
4. **No orientation at street level.** No small map shows position, heading, stations or trains.

**Hypothesis (3)** — Status: confirmed. MapLibre culls vector tiles with flat, zero-height bounding boxes, so once the pavement leaves the view every tile fails the frustum test. Would confirm: tile count drops to zero at pitch > ~115°. Would refute: tiles stay loaded while buildings vanish.

**Proposal** *(proposal)*: labelled view chips "Map · Elevated · Street" (Elevated is ~60 m up, tilted 55°) with an X-ray toggle beside them; tall tile bounding boxes at street level, plus an opt-in "See through buildings" toggle for the old faded look; and a circular, heading-up mini map at street level.


---

**Pukujan commented, 2026-10-25**

All four parts are live on nyc.design-bakery.com.

1. **Elevated view.** The view menu offers Top, Aerial (tilted, from above), Closer (low angle, buildings) and Street.
2. **X-ray without a station card.** An X-ray switch sits in the view menu (`ViewSwitcher`) and in the Layers drawer as "X-ray (trains underground)".
3. **Buildings stay when looking up.** At street level in Times Square, dragging up to the 150° limit, so the view looks straight up between the towers, every building stays drawn against the sky. Street view keeps the camera centre unclamped from the ground (`setCenterClampedToGround(!walking)`), so tiles are no longer culled when the pavement leaves the view.
4. **Orientation at street level.** A mini map in the corner shows your dot with an arrow for the way you face ("You · Facing northwest"), nearby places and lines, with Hide map and Leave street view beside it.
