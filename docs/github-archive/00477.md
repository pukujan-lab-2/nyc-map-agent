Someone who uses the app in Chinese sees 厕所 on the Restroom button, but tapping it sent "Where's the nearest public restroom that's open now?" in English. The English question showed up in their chat, and the assistant answered in English. In Spanish the answer did come back in Spanish, but the question bubble was still English. My train, Find exit and the "Plan a trip to" text that Plan trip types for you had the same problem.

Then, once the answer did come back in Chinese, it had no Use my location button. The app only adds that button when the reply mentions the person's location in English.

## What changes

- **The quick buttons ask in the app's language** (`src/features/agent/AgentOverlay.tsx`, `src/lib/i18n/appLang.ts`). This covers Restroom, My train (both one line and all lines), Find exit, and the Plan trip prefix, in all eight languages.
- **Use my location in every language** (`src/features/agent/needsOrigin.ts`). A reply that names the button in any app language, or says "my location" in one of them, now gets the button.
- **Answers without the model** (`server/features/agent/noModel.ts`). When the daily budget runs out, restroom questions still get a real answer. That check now also knows the restroom words the button uses in French, Haitian Creole, Russian, Chinese, Korean and Bengali. Before, it only knew English and Spanish.

## How to check

1. Set the app to Chinese (ask one question in Chinese, or pick it in the menu), then tap 厕所. The question is in Chinese, the answer is in Chinese, and 使用我的位置 (Use my location) shows under it.
2. Do the same in Spanish: the question reads "¿Dónde está el baño público abierto más cercano?".
3. Tap Plan trip in Spanish: the box reads "Planear un viaje a ".

New tests check that the restroom question in each of the eight languages gets the restroom answer without the model, and that Chinese and Spanish replies get the location button.

## What stays the same

English questions and answers are unchanged. Some questions are still sent in English: Use this route, Other options, and the bus card's "Where to get off". Some text also still shows in English, such as the "Planner: reading your question" progress lines and the "from here" location prompt. Each needs its own strings.
