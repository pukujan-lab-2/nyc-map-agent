## Problem
Live subway trains never stopped at stations: position was interpolated at constant speed between predicted arrival times, so trains glided through stops, drifted toward their origin instead of waiting there, and slid off after their last stop. Refs #194 · Closes #194

## What changes
- **Dwell at every stop.** When the realtime feed gives a stop both an arrival and a later departure time, those win. Otherwise a default hold: 30 s at ordinary stops, 50 s at major transfer complexes (Times Sq, Grand Central, Union Sq, Atlantic Av, Fulton St, Herald Sq, Columbus Circle, Jackson Hts, 74 St), 60 s at express/local transfer points. A default dwell never takes more than half the gap to the next arrival.
- **Timetable kept.** Travel time between stops is shortened by the dwell, so the train still reaches each stop at its predicted arrival.
- **Easing.** Cosine ease-in-out between stops: the train slows into and speeds out of each station.
- **Origin and terminal.** If the first update has only a departure time (the trip's origin), the train waits there until it departs. At the last stop it dwells 60 s (or until the feed's departure time), then leaves the map.
- **Details sheet** shows "At <station>", "Departs <terminal> in X min" or "Arrived at <terminal>".
- One function (`estimateTrain` → `motionAt` in `src/features/transit/dwell.ts`) drives the 2D bullets, the 3D models and the follow camera, so all three change together.
- **Express/local list from data.** `npm run build:transit-holds` reads static GTFS `stop_times.txt` stop patterns (mapped to `stops.txt` parent stations), per route and direction, and marks stations where one pattern runs nonstop between two stops another pattern stops at with stops in between. Output is the small committed `src/features/transit/holds.json` (80 parent stations), plus curated overrides: added DeKalb Av, Queensboro Plaza, Atlantic Av (B/Q); removed J/Z skip-stop, Staten Island Railway and a few line-crossing false positives. It reproduces 96 St and 72 St (1/2/3), 125 St and Union Sq (4/5/6), 125 St and Columbus Circle (A/B/C/D), Forest Hills and Jackson Hts (E/F/M/R), Canal St and Herald Sq (N/Q/R/W). `transfers.txt` was not needed: same-parent-station mapping already merges platforms. **Hold times are estimates**; GTFS has no official hold flag.
- Server now passes `[stop, arrival, departure]` when departure is later, and an `origin` flag.

## How to verify
- `npx vitest run src/features/transit`: new `dwell.test.ts` covers dwell, feed departure times, timetable preserved, easing (slow near stations, monotonic), dwell cap, origin wait, back-extrapolation, terminal dwell then gone, express/local derivation, and the sheet states via `estimateTrain`.
- Open the map, tap a subway train: it pauses at stations and the sheet reads "At …".

## What stays unchanged
Arrivals boards, buses, LIRR/Metro-North logic apart from a flat 45 s default dwell, and the 30-minute "not running yet" cutoff.
