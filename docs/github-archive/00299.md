# Issue #299: Follow and a trip preview fight over the camera

- State: closed (2026-10-22)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-22
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/299

Following a vehicle and then asking how to get somewhere makes the map thrash.

The follow camera starts a new quarter-second glide toward that vehicle about 15 times a second, and the trip preview sets the camera every frame along the new route. Both run together. Overhead is high enough that a heading twitch swings the whole view, and the X-ray disc sweeps with it.

Asking for directions, fitting the route, or previewing the trip should take the camera. Follow should let go without gliding back to the old view. Tapping a vehicle again should stop the preview without that glide. While follow does own the camera, it should take one pose per update, and ignore a heading that flips almost backward until that flip stays.
