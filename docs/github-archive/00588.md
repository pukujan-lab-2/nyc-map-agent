### Summary

Answers about subway trouble drop every affected spot on the map at once. The owner wants the map to travel like a tour, one station or group at a time, while the agent speaks a plain sentence for each. The trip preview player already moves the camera and speaks, so this can reuse it.

### Identity and Lineage

- Leaf issue: this one
- Parent: none
- Task/branch: none yet

### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A problem answer offers "Walk me through it" (proposed)
- [ ] The camera visits each spot in turn and speaks one sentence per spot, in the rider's language (proposed)
- [ ] Pause, Next, Back and Stop work, and speech stops when the answer closes (proposed)
- [ ] On phones, the spot being described stays visible above the sheet (proposed)

### Boundaries and Next Action

Out of scope: street view (it stays at eye level, per #584 and #585).
Next action: the owner confirms the two open decisions under Proposed Next Actions.

### Symptoms

1. **Every problem spot drops at once.** `server/features/agent/mapActions.ts` sends one `showTrouble` action with all points; `showPoints` in `src/features/agent/tripLayer.ts` draws them together and fits the camera around all of them.
2. **Nothing moves or speaks per spot.** The rider zooms around by hand and matches each station to the answer text.
3. **The parts exist for trips.** The trip preview (`src/features/agent/usePlayer.ts`, `flythrough.ts`) moves the camera on a timeline, speaks each step in the rider's language, and has play, pause and speed controls.

### Hypotheses (unverified)

- (hypothesis) Grouping neighbouring stations keeps a tour to about 6 stops for most notices.
  Experiment to confirm: run the grouping over a week of live notices and count stops per tour.

### Counter-signal

Some notices list dozens of stations; if grouping fails, tours get long.

### Honest Caveat

Tour length and speech timing are untested. A tour that doesn't stop cleanly when the rider asks something new would be annoying.

### Problems vs Gaps

- Problem (evidenced): problem spots show all at once, with no narration per spot.
- Gap (untested): whether grouping keeps tours short.

### Non-Binding Proposed Next Actions

*(proposal)*

1. **Start with a button.** The answer stays short ("The M skips 4 stations in Queens this weekend"), and **Walk me through it** starts the tour.
2. **One spot at a time.** The camera flies to a station, zooms in a little, says one plain sentence ("Fresh Pond Road: the M doesn't stop here Saturday and Sunday. Take the shuttle bus from the corner."), pauses, then moves on.
3. **Controls:** Pause, Next, Back and Stop, in the trip preview's bar.
4. **Short tours:** group neighbouring stations, about 6 stops at most; at the end, show every spot again.
5. **All problem answers:** skipped stations, a stuck train, where to switch direction, shuttle buses, elevator outages on a line.
6. **In the rider's language**, so a notice explained in Spanish is toured in Spanish.

Open decisions for the owner: (a) button to start (recommended), or automatic when asked by voice? (b) overhead map only (recommended), or street view too?
