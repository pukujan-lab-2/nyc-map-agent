# PR #650: feat(transit): pool learned times by MTA schedule and time of day

- State: open

- Branch `feat/648-pool-by-schedule` into `main`, +145 −14 in 4 files

The learned train and bus times were kept per day of the week and hour (168 buckets), but the MTA runs three schedules, so most buckets had too few samples to be used at all.

## Human outcome

The map and the agent use learned travel and dwell times in far more places, because each lookup now pools every sample from the same schedule and time of day.

## Before and after

**Before:** a lookup for Wednesday 08:00 matched only Wednesday 08:00 rows, and a segment needed 3 samples. Counted on the stored data: Wednesday 8 am had 0 usable subway segments, weekday 3 am had 0, Saturday 10 pm had 17.

**After:** a lookup pools Monday to Friday over the morning band (7 to 9), or Saturday, or Sunday, and the band's hours. Same data: Wednesday 8 am has 1,352 usable segments, weekday night (00 to 04) 846, Saturday evening (20 to 23) 298.

The schedules are the MTA's own: its GTFS calendars have exactly three service ids (Weekday, Saturday, Sunday). Night is a time band inside each, not a fourth schedule.

## Change and scope

- `server/features/transit/segments.ts`: `scheduleDows`, `bandHours`, and pooled SQL for `/api/transit-segments` and `/all`. Counts add; medians and 80th percentiles are averaged weighted by count (an approximation).
- `server/features/transit/busSegments.ts`: bus samples are pooled the same way, and the median is taken over every pooled sample.
- `server/features/transit/segments.test.ts`: the schedules, every hour in exactly one band, and the pooled query's parameters.

## What stays unchanged

The response shape (the client needs no change), what the recorder stores, and holiday schedules (the MTA runs Sunday service on holidays; not handled here).
