**Problem.** Following a train on a small iPhone (320×568) hid the train. A "Following your 2" card sat at the top left, and a tall card with the next stop, the stops list, notices and buttons sat in the middle of the screen, right where the train is.

**What changes**
- **Top right holds only quick buttons:** Change view, zoom (+ and − on a phone; the slider stays on wide screens) and north (the compass, now always shown). Center on my location moves just above the chat.
- **Following shows one line above the chat:** the route, "Next: <stop>", the minutes, and the X to stop following. It opens in two steps:
  | Gesture | Tap that does the same | Result |
  |---|---|---|
  | Swipe up | Tap the line or the grip | Opens: tabs, Exit help / Other ways, the next stops |
  | Swipe up again | Tap the grip | Tallest: every listed stop; still stops short of the train |
  | Swipe left or right | Tap a tab | Train → Notices → Settings (language, QR code), wrapping round |
  | Swipe down | Tap the grip at the tallest step | One step back |
- **The train stays in view:** the sheet counts as a panel covering the map, so the camera keeps the train in the clear part above it.
- **The map's load event no longer has to fire:** the live trains keep one of the map's data sources loading, so on a slow device the map's "load" event may never fire. When that happened, the quick buttons never got the map and panels never moved the camera. Now the first basemap tiles run the same one-time setup.

**How to verify**
- `pnpm exec vitest run src/features/transit/sheetSwipe.test.ts` checks the swipe rules (direction, steps, wrapping).
- Run `e2e/menu-row.spec.ts`. The phone test checks for the + and − buttons and that locate sits just above the chat.
- By hand on a phone: My train → a line → Follow a train → swipe the strip up, sideways, down.

- The classic (non-agent) follow card and its tests.
- Wide-screen layouts, apart from the always-visible compass and the sheet replacing the two follow cards (docked left at the same width).
- On a phone, language and QR stay in the rail when nothing is followed. They will move into a sheet once the other panels come up from behind the chat too.
