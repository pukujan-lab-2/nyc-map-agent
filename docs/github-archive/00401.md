In Brave, tapping the mic or the hands-free button showed "Speech recognition failed (network)". Brave switches off the speech service that Chrome's built-in recognizer depends on, so voice never worked there. Firefox has no recognizer at all.

## What changes

- **A server endpoint, `/api/stt`** (`server/features/agent/stt.ts`). It takes one short recording (at most 2 MB) and returns the words.
  - It works with any OpenAI-compatible transcription API: Groq Whisper by default, OpenAI also works.
  - The clip isn't stored or logged.
  - It allows 12 turns a minute and 120 an hour per visitor.
- **A browser fallback** (`src/lib/voice/serverStt.ts`, wired into `useSpeech`):
  - Brave, or any browser whose recognizer fails with "network", records the turn instead. The turn ends after the same 1.3-second pause, or after 6 seconds if nobody spoke, or at 20 seconds.
  - Chrome and Safari keep their built-in recognizer.
- **No key, no confusing error.** Without a key the endpoint answers 503, and the app says "Voice isn't set up on this server yet. Type your question instead."
- **Docs:** `STT_API_KEY`, `STT_BASE_URL` and `STT_MODEL` are listed in `.env.example`. `GROQ_API_KEY` also works.

## How to verify

- **Local checks:** type check, lint, dependency rules, knip, OpenAPI (62 paths), size limits.
- **Unit tests:** 975 passed, including the endpoint (no key, empty or too-long clips, language mapping) and the recorder's end-of-turn rules.
- **Browser tests:** 21 of 22 passed in the full parallel run. The smoke test timed out waiting for map data under load, then passed on its own.
- **The mounted route,** with no key set, answers `{"error":"stt_unavailable"}` (503) on both `/api/stt` and `/api/v1/stt`.
- **Not checked end to end with a real provider:** neither the Groq nor the OpenAI keys on the developer's Mac are valid. After `STT_API_KEY` is set on the server, try the mic in Brave.

## What stays the same

Chrome and Safari still use their own recognizer, and no data is stored. There's no migration.
