# Issue #254: Live trains/buses missing in street view, 3D and when boarded; labels blink

- State: closed (2026-10-22)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-22
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/254

Reported by Alex on nyc.design-bakery.com:
- No live trains or buses at street level (above or underground).
- Boarding (Follow) a train shows no train.
- Train bullets disappear in the tilted 3D view.
- Some street labels blink.

Causes found: street level culls vehicles to the viewport bounds (a few metres wide at walking zoom/pitch); underground trains are dropped at street level; tilted view hides all 2D bullets; ghost instance colours are never applied (buffer created after shader compile) so underground cars draw white; full bus badges take part in label placement.
