# Issue #129: Street-name legibility at street level

- State: closed (2026-10-21)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-21
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/129

At eye level the corner blade text is a few pixels tall and there is no quick way to tell which intersection you are at.

- Bigger sign blades (decal blades ~1.6x real size)
- Street names painted on the road surface before each crossing
- A small location card while walking, e.g. **W 44 St at 8 Av** (nearest baked corner sign)
- Signs and road names fade out with distance from the eye
