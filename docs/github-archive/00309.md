# Issue #309: The map cannot hide its roads, and bus badges are too small

- State: closed (2026-10-23)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-23
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/309

There is no way to take the roads off the map, and the bus badges are too small to read once a borough filter is on.

The street grid stays up even when the point of the map is the subway and the buses. Simple streets only pales that grid. A Roads switch should remove the road paint and the road names, and leave the water, the parks, and the rail.

A bus badge at city zoom is about half the size of a subway bullet. With one borough on, the badges can be that same size.
