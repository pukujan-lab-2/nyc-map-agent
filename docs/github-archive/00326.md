# PR #326: feat(map): show city landmarks with a picture

Zoomed out, the map has no named buildings to say which part of the city you are in, so a familiar tower or stadium is hard to pick out.

This adds 47 recognizable places across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, and Staten Island as ivory dots and names (dots from zoom 10, names from zoom 12). Landmarks in the quick layers menu, and Hide landmarks under Map detail, turn them off. They stay on until that choice is saved. Tapping a dot opens a sheet with the Wikipedia picture, a short description, and a link to the article. Tapping a hero model (Empire State, Chrysler, One World Trade Center, Statue of Liberty) opens the same sheet. The picture is fetched when the sheet opens and is not stored in the repo. The map credits Wikipedia (CC BY-SA).

How to verify: zoom to about 12 over any borough, tap a named dot, and confirm the picture and the borough. Turn Landmarks off and the dots and names leave. Turn it back on and they return.

Unchanged: the four 3D hero models, transit taps, bus stops, roads, and the basemap sights categories.
