# Issue #648: Learned train and bus times should pool by MTA schedule and time of day

### Summary

The learned train and bus times are keyed by day of the week plus hour (168 buckets), but the MTA runs three schedules: Weekday, Saturday and Sunday. Monday to Friday share one schedule and are split into five buckets, so each bucket collects a fifth of the samples and the app's "at least 3 samples" rule rarely passes. Grouping by schedule and time of day fills each bucket several times faster.

### Identity and Lineage

Repository Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent. Code: `server/features/transit/segments.ts` (reads `marts.transit_segment_hourly` by `dow` and `hour`), `src/features/transit/history.ts` (`nyHourOfWeek`, `liveWeight`). Related: #594 (estimates are never scored against outcomes).

### Acceptance Criteria

1. `/api/transit-segments` and `/all` pool the stored rows over the schedule (Weekday: Mon to Fri; Saturday; Sunday) and the time band of the requested hour, weighting by sample count.
2. The bands are night 00-04, early 05-06, morning 07-09, midday 10-15, evening peak 16-19, evening 20-23.
3. A unit test covers the schedule and band of every hour of the week, and the pooled query.
4. The response keeps its shape so the client needs no change.
5. `docs/` names the three schedules and the bands.

### Boundaries and Next Action

In scope: the read side (the API's SQL). Out of scope: changing what the recorder stores, holiday schedules (the MTA runs Sunday service on holidays; noted, not built), and scoring (#594). Next action: pool in SQL by schedule and band.

Owner note, 2026-11-01 (in chat): trains and buses have weekday, weekday night and weekend schedules. Checked against the MTA's files the same day: three calendars (Weekday, Saturday, Sunday), with night as a time band inside each.

### Symptoms

1. **Sparse buckets:** the recorder covers about 1 to 1.5 hours a day, so most of the 168 hour-of-week buckets have under 3 samples.
2. **Same schedule, split data:** Monday to Friday use one MTA schedule but five buckets.
3. **Fallback to live only:** with no history, the map's motion uses the feed and default dwell times alone.

### Hypotheses (unverified)

H1. Pooling by schedule and band raises the share of segments that have history. **Status:** unverified. **Would confirm:** after the change, more rows pass the `n >= 3` rule for the same data. **Would refute:** the share is unchanged. **Experiment:** count rows with n >= 3 before and after on the stored data.

### Counter-signal

Fridays may run slightly differently from Monday to Thursday, and weekday travel times vary by season; pooling hides that. The hour of the week was a finer grain on purpose.

### Honest Caveat

Medians cannot be pooled exactly; the weighted average of medians is an approximation, and the p80 is a weighted average too, not a true percentile.

### Problems vs Gaps

Problem: data split finer than the schedule. Gap: no error score to say whether pooling helps (#594).
