**Problem.** Answers, map cards and the ask box each sat in their own box: on a wide screen the answer docked bottom-left in a short box while a tapped place's card floated top-left over the same side, and on a phone the answer box was a fixed 30% of the screen. The owner asked for one sheet, the way Google and Apple Maps do it (step 1 of 3).

**What changes**
- Wide screens (1024 px and up): an open answer or a map card makes one left column. The card prints at its top, the answer under it (it can now use the column's full height), and the ask box sits at the foot. The middle of the map stays clear, and `--sheet-left` moves the view buttons beside the column.
- Phones: the answer box has a grip. Tap or swipe up for more answer, down for more map. Each new question starts at the usual size.
- The ask box stays the same element in both shapes, so it keeps the cursor when the column opens.

**How to verify**
- `e2e/one-sheet.spec.ts`: at 1280×800 a long answer fills the left column above the ask box; at 390×844 the grip grows and shrinks the answer. Both run `expectLayout`.
- Live: tap a restroom pin on a wide screen; its card sits at the top of the left column.

**What stays unchanged**
- The legacy layout (`?flags=-agentHome`), the phone card drawer, and the line, station, trip and alert cards. Those move in step 2.
