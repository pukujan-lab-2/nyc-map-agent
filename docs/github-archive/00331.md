# Issue #331: Underground dashes are too thin to follow

- State: closed (2026-10-24)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-23
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/331

## Summary

On the New York City map, an underground subway line is hard to see when X-ray is off. The line is a faint dash. The person who uses the map asked for a thicker dash, and said the dash itself is the right idea.

## Symptoms

1. **Hard to find**: With X-ray off, underground lines are difficult to follow by eye.
2. **Dash is wanted**: The same note says a dashed line is a good way to show a tunnel.

## Evidence

The queued note, in the person's words: "[Image #1] i wish it wasnt so hard to see underground lines, yes dashed lines is good concept but they are very hard to find right now needs to be thicker lines i think"

On `main`, the underground layer `transit-line-under` draws two ways. With X-ray off it is a dash: line width 1 at zoom 10 growing to 2.5 at zoom 16, opacity 0.45, dash pattern 2, 2. With X-ray on it is a solid glowing run, width 2 to 6, opacity 0.95. The at-grade and elevated layer `transit-line` is wider, from about 1.5 at zoom 10 to 4 at zoom 16. That paint is in `src/features/transit/TransitLayer.tsx` on `origin/main` (the underground layer is the block whose comment calls the X-ray-off line a faint dashed hint). This supports the "too thin" reading for the X-ray-off dash. It does not by itself prove that width is the only reason the line is hard to see.

## Hypotheses

**Thin stroke.** Status: unverified. Would confirm: at the same zoom, with X-ray off, a wider dash is easy to follow along a known tunnel and the current dash is not. Would refute: a wider dash is still hard to see, and the color, the dash gap, or the basemap is what hides it. Experiment: compare the current dash with a thicker dash on one underground stretch, leaving opacity at 0.45.

## Counter-signal

X-ray already draws the same underground geometry as a thicker solid line. The complaint is the faint dash used when X-ray is off. A person who turns X-ray on is seeing that solid run.

## Honest caveat

This filing did not open the live site and re-measure the line. The long A train tunnel through East New York is a real tunnel and stays a dash. No thickness number is chosen here.

## Problems vs gaps

Problem: the X-ray-off underground dash is hard to see.

Gap: the thickness that would make it readable is not chosen. *(proposal)* A thicker underground dash, still at 45% opacity and still dashed, does not exist as a change. Nothing in this log has been built.
