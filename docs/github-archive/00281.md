## Problem

Tapping the map beside the Layers and filters sheet closes it. The sheet is where you turn on Directions, Live trains, and the other layers, so a miss on the pavement dismisses the work.

## What changes

An empty map tap still closes a details sheet. It leaves the layers drawer open. A train or bus tap still closes the drawer and follows that vehicle. Done, Show map, and Esc are unchanged.

## How to verify

Open Layers and filters, tap the map where there is no train or bus, and the sheet stays. Tap a bus and the sheet closes into the follow card. Open a neighborhood card and tap empty map: that card still closes.
