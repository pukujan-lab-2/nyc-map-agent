Since #395 went live, the map has opened as OpenStreetMap's image tiles: yellow and orange roads, every shop, parking and bus-stop icon, and dozens of "WC" discs. The subway lines are hard to find under all of it. The cause was one filter. The new subway-line panel's "match nothing" filter used a form MapLibre rejects, and that error, raised while the style loaded, made the app give up on the vector map and fall back to image tiles. Image tiles can't be restyled, so the quiet-roads look never applied.

## What changes

1. **The clean map is back.** The filter now uses a valid "match nothing" expression, so the vector map loads. Streets are plain white with no casings or shields, and the subway and bus lines stand out on top (`src/features/transit/TransitLayer.tsx`).
2. **Restrooms are off when the map opens.** They show when you tap Restroom, ask for one, or switch them on in Layers. The markers now use the two-person restroom pictogram instead of "WC", including on the 3D street kiosks.
3. **The menu row is readable on light maps.** Faded icons sit on small dark discs with no outline, and the fade is gentler (1, 0.9, 0.7, 0.5, 0.32, 0.18).
4. **The task buttons are smaller on desktop:** one row of 40px pills instead of 72px tiles. Phones keep the larger tiles.
5. **An alert's "Show on map" zooms to the stations the alert names.** "No 7 at Flushing-Main St" frames Flushing-Main, and "skips 52 St and 69 St … Woodside-61 St" frames those three stops. It shows the whole line only when no station is named.
6. **The Exact location switch actually shares your location.** It used to save only a preference. It now uses the app's one shared location, the same one the locate button and the agent use: on asks the browser to share, off stops sharing.

## How to verify
