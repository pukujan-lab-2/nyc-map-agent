# PR #471: fix(panel): the last lines in the Lines sheet scroll into view on phones

On a phone, tapping My train opens the Lines sheet at half height, but the last rows in it (the J, the shuttles, the Staten Island Railway) could never be reached. Scrolling stopped with them hidden behind the chat bar or below the bottom of the screen, so anyone riding one of those lines couldn't open its notices from the list.

The cause: the phone sheet is 97% of the screen tall and slides down to its snap point. At half height its lower half sits off screen, but the list inside it still believed it had the full height, so its scroll end was out of reach. On top of that, the chat bar covers the sheet's bottom edge.

## What changes

The sheet's content area now gets only the height you can actually see: the visible part of the sheet, less the grab handle and the chat bar (`--chat-bar-h`). That applies at half and full height. The peek height is unchanged. Every panel that opens in this sheet benefits, not only Lines.

## How to check

1. Open the app at 390×844 (or any phone size) and tap My train.
2. Scroll the Lines list to the end. The Staten Island Railway row should sit fully above the chat bar, and tapping it should open the line.
3. Drag the sheet to full height and scroll again: the last row still clears the chat bar.

I checked step 2 at 390×844 against a local build with the API proxied to the live site.

## What stays the same

The snap points, the peek row and the tablet/desktop side panel are untouched.

`pnpm test`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm knip`, `pnpm build:size && pnpm size` and the Playwright suite pass.

Found while walking the live site at phone sizes after the owner reported "lots of issues in phone".
