# PR #515: Map · 3D · Street switch; mini map opens Map centred on you, with Back to street view

**Problem.** In street view, a tap on the small corner map (the mini map) dropped you out to a zoomed-out map. You couldn't tell where you had been standing, and there was no quick way back. Restrooms disappeared at the same moment. The owner asked for one Map · 3D · Street switch instead of scattered view controls.

**What changes**
- **One switch.** Map · 3D · Street now sits at the top centre on tablets and desktops. On a phone, the cube button opens the same three choices. Each choice keeps the spot you are looking at and the way you face.
- **The mini map opens Map, centred on you.** A tap on the mini map (anywhere except a restroom) shows the flat map at street-block zoom, centred where you stood. A blue "you" marker with a heading cone shows which way you faced.
- **Back to street view.** A button under the switch (at the top centre on phones) puts you back on the exact spot, facing the same way. The × next to it dismisses it.
- **One reusable function.** `showView(view, place, heading)` in `src/lib/map/viewChange.ts` carries out every view change, so a later transit tour can call it too. The tour itself is not built here.
- **Restrooms stay on after street view.** Shops stay off, as before, because they were only on while you walked (no unasked shop suggestions).
- **Fix found along the way.** Leaving street view used to end at the walk's zoom (about 20–22), not the intended zoom. A "moveend" fired mid-exit flipped the tilt flag, and the tilt effect then flattened the map at the walk's zoom over the top of the exit move. The exit now lands where intended.

**How to verify**
1. Open the app on a desktop, go to Union Square and pick **Street** in the top switch.
2. Tap the mini map. Expected: a flat map at zoom 16.5 centred on you, the blue marker pointing the way you faced, restrooms showing, and "Back to street view" at the top.
3. Tap **Back to street view**. Expected: the same spot and the same heading at eye level.
4. Repeat at 375 px wide using the cube button.

I checked these steps on the dev build in a headless browser at 1280×800 and 375×667. The camera ended at zoom 16.5, pitch 0, centred on the standing spot, with `restrooms=1` in the address. "Back to street view" restored the 30° heading and eye-level pitch. All 1,335 unit tests pass, including two new ones for `showView`. End-to-end tests run in CI only: the Mac is short on memory.

**What stays the same**
- Follow cameras (Chase, Side, Overhead, Street) are unchanged and keep their own menu. The top switch is hidden while you follow a vehicle. Mini maps for follow cameras come in a separate change.
- "Leave street view" behaves as before, except that it now also centres on you.
- The classic view switcher (with the agent home turned off) is untouched.
