## Problem

The owner marked up the design canvas with these problems:
- Open panels and menus hid the followed train and your own position.
- The zoomed-out map was full of dots: a dot for every live train, plus station dots and landmark icons. The train lines got lost among them.
- The line panel's next-train times took a whole row.
- The notices list was too tall.

## What changes

- **Line panel**
  - The next trains are stacked at the right of "Next R at …".
  - When no notice is open, the notices list shows about one and a half notices, with a thin scroll bar for the rest.
  - "Other ways" and "Follow a train" are smaller.
- **Map stays clear of panels** (new `src/lib/map/mapCover.ts`).
  - Open panels and menus now set the map's padding, so its centre moves into the part you can still see:
    - the line panel, the menu dropdowns (My day and the others), the Change view menu, and the task panels;
    - phones: the top or bottom edge;
    - tablets and desktops: the side the panel is docked to.
  - The padding animates in. If the camera is already moving, it waits for that move to finish. When the panel closes, the padding goes back to zero.
  - The existing hand-tuned `fitBounds` paddings now add only what the map's padding doesn't already clear, so nothing is counted twice.
- **Quieter map, train lines first**
  - Zoomed out, there are no train dots, except for a picked line, an alert's lines or the trip's lines.
  - Station dots and elevator-outage icons appear from zoom 14.
  - Landmarks appear from zoom 14, 15 or 16, depending on how prominent they are.
  - Train lines are thicker, and the underground dashes are stronger.
