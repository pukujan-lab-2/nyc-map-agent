## What changes
- The quick Layers dropdown (`QuickLayers`) moves into the top bar's button row, next to Layers & filters and Transit. The top of the screen is one controls bar. `MapView` no longer mounts it separately.
- Its menu opens under that row, scrolls inside 60% of the screen height, and closes on a tap outside it or a swipe right.
- While a train or bus is followed, the menu opens below the follow card (`margin-top` includes `--follow-card-h`), so it never covers the card.
