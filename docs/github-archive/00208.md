# Issue #208: Live subway trains jump backward on the map

- State: closed (2026-10-22)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-21
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/208

Trains reach a station early and then snap back. The position is recomputed from scratch every second from the latest predicted arrivals. When the next feed update pushes the next stop's ETA later, or drops a passed stop so we extrapolate back from the next one, the fresh estimate lands behind the train we already drew, so it jumps backward. Nothing stops the train from passing a stop the feed still lists either.

Fix: keep along-route progress monotonic per trip (reset on a new trip, route or reroute), hold at the next listed stop until the feed drops it, and slow down to meet a later ETA instead of jumping back. Dwell behavior from #195 stays.
