Smart mode: the assistant becomes the main way to use the map. It's a hands-free voice + text agent on the rail's **Ask** button that uses your location (with permission). Refs #84 (I'll keep it open until a live InferHub call is verified; the key on the build box is revoked).

## What it does
- "How will my trip be tomorrow from here to Grand Central at 9am?" gets a spoken and written answer. The map draws the route, the trip panel opens (weather chip for that hour, lines, ETA, planned work, elevator/escalator outages, recommended exit), and a fast-forward preview plays.
- **Preview (transit):** walk at street level with turn callouts → descent at the chosen entrance → ride along the line (buildings go x-ray underground, normal 3D city when elevated) with stop callouts and a transfer walk → **recommended exit**, picked as the MTA Subway Entrances and Exits row nearest the destination and said plainly ("Use the NW exit on E 41 St at Grand Central-42 St"), with elevator-only/outage notes → walk out.
- **Preview (drive, flag `agentDrive`):** drive-through at car eye height (pitch 80°, z19; MapLibre has no altitude) with OSRM turn + lane hints. The answer covers ETA, DOT live slow segments, construction/event closures on the route, MTA B&T tolls (static table, rates effective 2026-01-04), and crash hotspots from `marts.block_month`.
- One slim playback bar: play/pause, 2x/10x/30x, skip leg, close. There's also a Walk/Transit/Drive mode chip. No tooltips: settings and explanations live in dropdowns.
- Voice: Web Speech push-to-talk, an optional "Hey map" wake phrase (off by default, in the ⋮ menu), and spoken answers. Browsers without STT (Firefox) fall back to text.
- No "board the front car" advice. Car position isn't in MTA open data.

## Architecture
- `src/features/agent` (lazy `AgentOverlay` chunk, ~7.6 kB brotli, its own size-limit line) + `server/features/agent` (+ `drive/`). Flags: `agent` and `agentDrive`, both basic tier.
- `POST /api/agent` (Hono, in the @​hono/zod-openapi table; openapi.json regenerated) streams NDJSON events. Map actions are **zod-validated commands** (`flyTo, showRoute, openPanel, enterStreetView, playFlythrough, setTime`), and `executor.ts` runs them.
- LLM: InferHub via `ai` 7.0.143 + `@ai-sdk/openai-compatible` 3.0.71, used only as libraries (Apache-2.0, pinned). This is not Vercel hosting. `zod` moves to 3.25.76 (their peer range). `INFERHUB_API_KEY` is server-only, and provider errors are logged by request id only. Model: `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash` (override with `INFERHUB_MODEL`).
- Core touches: `FeatureModule.overlay` + `FeatureOverlays` (lazy mount via the registry; `replacesChat` hands over the Ask button), `src/lib/map/mapInstance.ts`, the POST route, and the dev API in `vite.config.ts` now forwards bodies and streams.
- Swappable interfaces: `TransitPlanner` / `StationEntrances` (`server/features/agent/transit.ts`) for #56, and `DriveRouter` (public OSRM car demo, light use only; `DRIVE_ROUTER_URL` for self-hosted OSRM, or implement it for Valhalla; see docs/MODULES.md).

## Stubbed / not wired
- Transit: MTA open-data stations + entrances. Rides are straight lines (`geometrySource: "straight-line"`) with heuristic times and a 4-minute average wait, until #56's GTFS shapes and realtime land.
- `setTime` only sets the trip card's time. There's no global time/day-night yet.
- The train is a coloured marker, not a three.js model.
- 511NY needs `NY511_API_KEY` (free). Camera locations (no open dataset), the DOT weekly advisory (PDF only), and parking difficulty are reported as missing, not invented.
