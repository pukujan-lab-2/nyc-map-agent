**Problem:** "Directions from here" on a place card always drew a walking line, however far away the place was. From Rockefeller Center it planned a walk across the river to Elmhurst. On the assistant home screen the line had no card, so closing the place card left it on the map. There was also no way to ask for the subway only, the bus only, or the least walking.

**What changes**
- **Far places go to the trip planner.** A place more than 800 m away asks the planner in chat ("How do I get to Rockefeller Center (lat, lon)?"). The exact spot travels with the question, so the planner can't swap in a different building with the same name.
- **Short walks get the walk card.** A place under 800 m away still draws the walk straight away, but now opens the walk card, which has its own close.
- **The trip card gets four choices:** Subway, Bus, Walk and Least walking, in all 8 app languages. Each asks again for the same two ends.
- **The planner takes a preference.** The trip search tool (`transit_plan`) now takes `prefer`: `fastest`, `subway`, `bus` or `less-walking`. Subway asks the trip planner (OpenTripPlanner) for rail only, bus asks for buses only, and least walking ranks the shortest walk first.

**How to verify**
1. Open a landmark across town, press Directions from here, and expect a subway or bus trip in chat.
2. Tap Bus, then Least walking, and expect the plan to change.
3. Press Directions from here on a restroom a block away, and expect the walk card.

**What stays unchanged:** the fastest-first ranking, Walk me there, and the fallback router when the planner is down (it ignores the preference).
