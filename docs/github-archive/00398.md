On desktop the "Following your 7" panel sat in the middle of the map, right over the train you were following, and its notices ran to three lines each. "Walk me there" from a restroom answer sent people to the wrong place: it asked the agent again, and the agent's walk router looked the name up and picked a building half an hour away. And the Street / Top switch had disappeared while following a train.

## What changes

1. **Nothing covers the middle of the map.** On tablet and desktop the follow, trip and exit panels sit at the left above the chat bar, at most 380 px wide. On phones they're capped at 42% of the screen height (`src/components/task-screens.css`).
2. **Notices are one line each,** with an icon for what's happening: elevator or escalator out, repair (signal, track, switch), skipped stops, no service, delays, detour, planned work, or a general notice. Tap a notice for the full text and its spot on the map. The icon is chosen from the notice's own wording (`src/features/transit/noticeKind.tsx`, with tests).
3. **"Walk me there" goes straight to the restroom.** It draws the walking route from your shared location to the restroom's coordinates and starts guidance, without asking the agent again. A restroom answer also frames you and the restroom on the map right away.
4. **The Chase / Side / Overhead / Cab row is back while following,** placed beside the side panel, not under it.
