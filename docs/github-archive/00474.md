On a small iPhone (320×568, the size of an iPhone SE first generation), starting a walk filled the screen with two cards. "Walk toward Bank of America Tower" wrapped to five lines, the landmark card sat right under it, and no map was left between them, so you couldn't see yourself or the route. On every phone size, picking a line that wasn't on screen framed it so its far end (Rector St and South Ferry on the 1) ended up under the chat bar.

## What changes

- **Walking cards** (`src/features/liveview/RouteGuide.tsx`). "Walk toward" becomes a small label above the landmark name. The name gets up to three lines below 380px wide and two lines above that, one text size smaller on narrow phones. The street and time line stays on one line. The landmark card's question is limited to two lines, its buttons stay 44px tall (the usual tap size) with slightly smaller text on narrow phones, and the photo credit keeps to one line. At 320×568 the map now shows between the two cards.
- **Framing a picked line** (`src/features/transit/TransitLayer.tsx`). The camera waits for the line panel to open, then fits the line between the panel and the chat bar. Before, it fitted first, then the panel's padding slid in and pushed the line down by about half the panel's height.

## How to check

1. At 320×568, ask "Times Square from Grand Central" and tap Use this route. The step card and the landmark card leave map visible between them, and the full landmark name is readable.
2. At 390×844, from Brooklyn, tap My train and then the 1. Every station down to South Ferry is above the chat bar.

## What stays the same

Tablet and desktop layouts, the exit help card, the order of the buttons, and the text on them are unchanged.
