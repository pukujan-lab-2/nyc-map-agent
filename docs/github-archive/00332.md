# Issue #332: On a phone the follow card sits under Layers and Lines sits on the train

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-23
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/332

## Summary

On a phone, while following the L train in the Cab view, the follow card is painted underneath the Layers controls, and the Lines button sits on its own row on top of the train.

## Symptoms

1. **Card under the pills**: The follow card shares a band with "Layers & filters" and "Transit on". Those pills cover it. The fragments "se" and "ys repo" show through, and the word Underground shows at the right edge.
2. **Layers button covers the card**: The Layers button overlaps the card's right side. The open menu lists Businesses, Street art, Transit Focus, X-ray, Restrooms, Events, Parking, Health, Parks & places, and Citi Bike.
3. **Lines on the train**: Chase, Side, Overhead, Cab, X-ray, and Stop following share one row. Lines is centered alone on the next row, over the train.

## Evidence

A portrait screenshot of the live map shows the L at Halsey Street, in Cab, with the grade word Underground. Station labels Halsey St, Wyckoff Avenue & Weirfield Street, and Covert Street & Wyckoff Avenue are visible. The person's words with that screenshot: "the ui and buttons plapced weird and overlapping in mobile view"

The screenshot was sent in this session before the request to stop and file logs. It is included here because issue #314 describes a different overlap (the view row, the search bar, and the map credits) and does not describe this follow card.

## Hypotheses

**Shared screen band.** Status: unverified as a layout cause. The screenshot shows the boxes overlapping. Would confirm: on a phone-width view, the follow card's box intersects the Layers pills and the Layers button, and Lines is on a second row. Would refute: a later visit to the same live view shows the card fully readable and Lines on the action row. Experiment: open the live map at a phone width, follow a train, open Layers, and compare the boxes. This filing did not run that check.

## Counter-signal

In the same screenshot the bottom row from Chase through Stop following is readable and on one line. The break is the follow card at the top, and Lines on the following row.

## Honest caveat

The screenshot's Layers menu does not list Roads or Bus stops. This filing did not check whether the live site is behind main. The card in the screenshot is the short live follow card. A taller card that lists stops and delays is a separate open change (pull request #330, for issue #316) and is not what this screenshot shows.

## Problems vs gaps

Problem: on a phone the follow card and the Lines button are placed on top of other things.

Gap: a layout that keeps them clear is not what the screenshot shows. *(proposal)* That layout does not exist as a reviewed change. A local edit was started on branch `fix/follow-card`. It is not committed, it is not in pull request #330, and it was not checked in a browser. That edit is not this fix.
