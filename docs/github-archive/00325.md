# Issue #325: Show recognizable buildings across the city, with a picture

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-23
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/325

Zoomed out, the map has no named buildings to say which part of the city you are in. The four hero models cover a few Manhattan towers, and tapping one does not show a picture.

Show a short list of recognizable places in all five boroughs as dots and names. A Landmarks switch and a Hide landmarks control turn them off. Tapping a dot, or a hero model, opens a sheet with that place's Wikipedia picture and a short description, so someone can tell where they are.
