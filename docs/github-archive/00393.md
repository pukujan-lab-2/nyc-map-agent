People need landmarks to know where they are, and the simple default map keeps only four.

## What changes
- `scripts/build-landmark-guide.ts` pulls from live Wikidata (CC0) and writes 151 visible-from-the-street landmarks: Manhattan 88, Brooklyn 20, Queens 19, Bronx 12, Staten Island 13 (including Hell Gate Bridge, the RFK Bridge and the St. George Ferry Terminal). Each has a Wikimedia Commons picture stored only with its licence, author and file page.
- The list loads on first use as its own file: the main chunk shrank by 11,889 B gzipped.
- Density by zoom: about 25 citywide, 70 from zoom 12, all from zoom 14; labelled icons by kind, above basemap labels. Layer ids start with `landmark-`. At street level only landmarks within 1.5 km show (37 at Times Square, no JFK), and street level now keeps them where it used to hide all labels.
- `pickGuideLandmarks(point, heading)` gives "Empire State Building ahead, slightly left" (compass words without a heading). The street-level header adds the top pick and now wraps to two lines on phones instead of cutting off.

## How to verify
26 landmark tests, typecheck and eslint pass. In the browser at Times Square facing north the header read "West 45th Street & 7th Avenue · Times Square to your left".

## Not checked
