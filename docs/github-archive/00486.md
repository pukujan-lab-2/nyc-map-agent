**Problem.** On a phone's home screen, language and the QR code still sat in the top-right column, under view, zoom and north. The agreed layout keeps only view, zoom and north up there and pulls everything else from behind the chat. Those two buttons crowded the map on small iPhones.

**What changes**
- A **Settings** chip now ends the quick-task row (Restroom, My train, Plan trip, Find exit). It shows on phones only.
- Tapping Settings opens the same pull sheet as following a train or picking a line. The sheet holds language and "Show the app's QR code". Swipe down or tap ✕ to close it. Asking anything also closes it.
- The follow sheet's Settings tab now uses the same shared body (`SettingsBody`), so the two stay identical.
- Wider screens keep language and the QR code in the top-right column, unchanged.
- The sheet loads lazily, so the boot chunk and the agent overlay stay within their size budgets.

**How to verify**
- Use a 320×568 or 375×667 viewport. The top right shows only view, zoom and north.
- Scroll the chips, tap Settings, and switch the language. Tap ✕ to close.
- `e2e/menu-row.spec.ts` and `e2e/app-language.spec.ts` now cover this.

**Unchanged:** desktop chrome, the follow sheet, the line sheet, and the language behaviour itself.
