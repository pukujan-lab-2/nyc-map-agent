When someone steps through street photos (Drive, or Next and Previous), the photo moves down the block but the map behind it stays where the walk began. After a few steps, the map no longer shows where the photo is. Now the map glides along with each step, low and tilted at street level and facing the way the photo faces. That's the block-by-block glide #10 asks for.

**What changes**
- `CameraTarget` gains an optional `bearing` and `glide`. With `glide`, `MapView` eases straight to the target in 1.4 s instead of flying in an arc. Existing callers send neither, so they behave exactly as before.
- `src/lib/map/photoGlide.ts` builds the street-level camera for a photo: zoom 18.4 and pitch 72, as #10 specifies, facing the photo's bearing.
- `StreetViewSheet` sends that camera on every photo after the first. The first photo is the block the map already shows.
- **Coverage lines:** while the photo sheet is open, the map draws Mapillary's public coverage tiles as thin green lines under the labels. You can see which streets have photos before you step (`src/lib/map/mapillaryCoverage.ts`). They're removed when the sheet closes, and drawn only when a token is set.

**How to verify**
- `pnpm vitest run src/lib/map/photoGlide.test.ts src/lib/map/mapillaryCoverage.test.ts`: the camera stands at the photo, tilted, facing its bearing, with the heading kept within one turn. The coverage layer goes under the labels, isn't added twice, removes itself, and escapes the token in the tile URL.
- Typecheck, lint, module boundaries and the size limits are clean.
- With a Mapillary token: open street photos on a block, press Drive, and watch the map follow each photo.

**Caveat:** there's no Mapillary token on the machine this was written on, so I couldn't watch the viewer and map together. The viewer calls used (`getPosition`, `getBearing`) are typed by mapillary-js, and typecheck passes. For the same reason, I haven't seen the coverage lines drawn against real tiles; the layer reads the documented `sequence` layer of `mly1_public`. There's no separate thumbnail prefetch, because mapillary-js already caches the photos next to the current one in a sequence.

**What stays the same:** every other camera move, reduced-motion users (they still jump instead of easing), and the photo sheet's own controls.

Closes #10
