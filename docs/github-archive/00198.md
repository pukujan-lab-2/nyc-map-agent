Trains on the map ignored service changes: they drove toward stops they would skip, kept moving while held, stayed visible after cancellation, and alerts were a raw MTA header with no location. This makes the live map follow the real feed data and say what is happening in plain English.

Refs #197. Stacked on #178 (base `feat/calm-map`), reusing its train sheet, station sheet and `estimateTrain`. No train-dwell PR was open, so there was nothing to stack on for that.

## What changes
1. **Skipped stops and reroutes.** The server keeps the feed's stop order and moves `schedule_relationship` SKIPPED stops into `skipped`; the train sheet lists them crossed out. Each hop is placed on the track of a line that serves both stops, so an F calling at A-line platforms rides the A's shape; the selected train's real path is drawn.
2. **Held trains.** If the next-stop ETA slips later on two refreshes in a row while the vehicle reports the same stop and status, or it reports STOPPED_AT / IN_TRANSIT_TO the same stop for 3+ minutes after that stop's time, the train freezes where it was, gets a **Holding** (stopped) or **Delayed** tag under the bullet, and the sheet says "Held near <station>". A train never moves past the stop the vehicle still reports.
3. **Cancelled trips** (`CANCELED`) are dropped.
4. **Station notices.** Active alerts keep header, description, active period and Mercury `human_readable_active_period`, plus stop-level entities with direction. Stations named by an alert get a small '!' dot; the station sheet and train sheet show each alert.
5. **Where the problem is.** 2+ affected stops on one route: dashed segment (amber, red for delays) between the first and last, labelled. Route-only: a "between A and B" phrase is used only when both names match our stations. Otherwise nothing is drawn.
6. **Plain-English summary first**, from fixed rules: skipped stops (with neighbouring stations as alternatives, never one the header says is affected), reroute onto another line, no service between two stations (with lines serving both station complexes), express-to-local, delays (with the station only when it resolves). Station notices and anything unresolved show the MTA header. "Full MTA notice" expands underneath. No model calls.
7. CI file matches `main` so this runs on the self-hosted gravebuster runner.

## Live sample (2026-10-21, about 5:03 PM EDT)
- Trip feeds (ACE, BDFM, 1-6): 674 trip updates, 539 vehicles. Present: `current_status` (STOPPED_AT 289, IN_TRANSIT_TO 216, INCOMING_AT 34), `stop_id`, `current_stop_sequence`, `timestamp`. No SKIPPED stops and no CANCELED trips in this sample, so those tests flip fields on real captured trips.
- Alerts: 10 active subway alerts. 7 had stop-level entities, 3 were route-only. Text parsing was needed for 0 (no live "between" wording), so it is tested with feed-style headers. Mercury fields seen: `alert_type`, `human_readable_active_period`, `affected_stations`, `station_alternative` (empty), `created_at`, `updated_at`, `display_before_active`, `clone_id`.
- Summaries for the live alerts: 8 of 10 got one, for example "Woodlawn-bound 4 skips Burnside Av. Use 176 St or 183 St." and "Flushing-bound 7 skips 52 St and 69 St. Use 74 St-Broadway or 46 St-Bliss St." The 2 station notices show the MTA header.
