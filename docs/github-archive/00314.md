# Issue #314: Phone controls overlap, and a line opens as a short peek

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-23
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/314

## Problem

On a phone the map controls sit on top of each other, and tapping a line opens a short peek instead of a drawer you can read.

## Symptoms

1. **Overlapping buttons**: the extra Layers button, the view row (Street, Mid, Up, Top, X-ray), and the compass share the same corners as the search bar and the map credits.
2. **Short peek**: the Lines list opens at the peek height, so it reads as a small pop instead of a drawer.
3. **One pop blocks another**: Transparent information boxes are acceptable. An open dropdown or pop still covers another one, because those panels do not keep their own state.
4. **Bus stop dots**: a selected bus route draws a dot at every stop, and Layers has no switch to hide those dots without also hiding the buses.
5. **Schools**: the Schools control does not show the school points that are already in the city tiles.

## Ask

Keep the phone controls in separate bands. Open Lines as a half-height drawer. Transparent information boxes can stay. Each dropdown and pop keeps its own state so one open panel does not block another. Add a Bus stops switch on the existing Layers control that hides stop dots only. Wire Schools to the existing school points.

Refs #9.
