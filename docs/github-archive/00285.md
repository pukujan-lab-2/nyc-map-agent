Stop following leaves the camera nose-down on the tracks.

Tapping a train or bus drops the view into the chase camera, low and tilted. Stop following only let go of the vehicle, so the map stayed there. The button now eases back to the view from just before that follow started. Dragging the map, or Esc, still lets go wherever the camera is.
