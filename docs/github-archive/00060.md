# Issue #60: Street-level NYC street-name sign poles at every intersection

- State: closed (2026-10-21)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-21
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/60

At street level (eye-level 3D mode), show a green NYC-style street-name sign at every intersection citywide: grey pole ~3.5 m with two perpendicular green blades naming the cross streets.

- Data: intersections + street-name pairs + bearings derived from DCP LION with DuckDB spatial (`scripts/street-signs.sql`), baked as a `signs` point layer into the city PMTiles (`pnpm build:sign-tiles`, then `pnpm publish:city-tiles`).
- Render: CC0 "Street Name Sign" GLB (Grab3D) in a MapLibre custom layer via three.js (same canvas and depth buffer), blades overlaid with canvas-text decals.
- Performance: only at zoom >= 17 and within ~300 m of the camera, capped.
- Verify: `pnpm build` + headless Playwright eye-level screenshots.
