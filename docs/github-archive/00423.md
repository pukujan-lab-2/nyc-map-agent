While following a train, a trip or your own walk, the camera chased the dot from behind. It tilted, turned and re-centred on every update, so the map never sat still long enough to read. The demo video shows a steady top-down map instead.

## What changes

- Following keeps the map flat and north-up.
- The map glides only when the dot gets near the edge of the screen, not on every update.
- The trip preview and the walking guide use the same rule.
- The chase, side and overhead cameras are still in the view switcher for anyone who wants them.

## How to check

Follow a train and watch the map stay still until the train nears the edge. `src/lib/map/keepInView.test.ts` covers the edge rule. `pnpm test` passes (1,167 tests), along with typecheck and lint.

## What stays the same

Street view, the 3D views and the train motion itself.
