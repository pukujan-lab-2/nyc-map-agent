The map is too noisy: on a subway you don't need other lines, buses, businesses or roads, only your line and landmarks. Until now this was only manual switches.

## What changes (behind the new flag `contextClean`, off by default)
- A context store (`src/lib/mapContext/context.ts`): following, trip, walking, place, agent, else browsing; fed by the follow store, walking, chat and the open panel.
- A rule table: following a subway train keeps that line, its stations and the train, hides other lines, buses, businesses, events, parking, data and ferry layers, and dims roads; walking keeps stations, restrooms, streets and landmarks. Layers prefixed `landmark-` are never touched.
- Overrides remember what they replaced and restore it on exit; if the app changes a property during a context, the app's latest value is restored. Saved toggles are never written.
- A "Show everything" / "Clean map" chip.

## How to verify

In the browser, drawn layers: browsing 114; following a D train 99 (only the D line and train left); "Show everything" 115; after Stop following 112; street level cleaned 76, "Show everything" 88. After exiting, the app's own hide-and-show changes stayed as the app set them.
