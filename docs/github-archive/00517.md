**Problem.** In street view, tapping a notice in a line's panel (for example "Elevator out at Times Sq-42 St") moved the camera while you were still at eye level. You got a sideways glimpse at zoom 15 instead of the map, and no way back to where you were walking. The owner asked to see the notice on the map, with an option to resume the walk.

**What changes**
- **A notice tapped in street view opens Map, framed on it.** Elevator and escalator outages, and alerts with an affected stretch of line, both go through the shared `showView("map", …, { frame })` from #515. Leaving street view therefore fits the notice and remembers your spot.
- **"Back to street view" resumes the walk:** same spot, same heading, eye level.
- **No collisions at the top.** When a line panel is docked at the top, the Map · 3D · Street switch and the back pill move below its pill and centre in the map beside it. That is the right-hand panel on wider screens, or the folded line bar on a phone. They also stay clear of the menu row.
- **Fix on phones.** With a line open, the cube "Change view" menu used to close the moment it opened: a rule meant for "a new line was picked" fired for any open line. It now fires only when the line changes.

**How to verify**
1. Pick the 2 line (My train → 7 Avenue Express) and stand in street view near Times Square.
2. Tap "Elevator out at Times Sq-42 St". Expected: a flat map at zoom 15 framed on the station, with the ring and "No elevator", and "Back to street view" at the top, clear of the panel.
3. Tap **Back to street view**. Expected: the same spot and heading at eye level.

I checked these steps on the dev build in a headless browser with the elevator-outage fixture from `e2e/line-notices.spec.ts`:

- 375×667: the back pill sat at the top (y 16–62) and the line sheet below it (y 214+). Resuming restored the heading.
