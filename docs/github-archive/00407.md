**Problem:** tapping "Use this route" on a subway trip (for example the R to Aldi in Rego Park) opened the line's notices panel, or followed whichever train happened to be nearest, but never said when the next train reaches your stop.

**What changes**
- "Use this route" on a subway ride now opens the line panel with a new row at the top: the next three trains at your boarding station, only in the direction of the stop you get off at, and where those trains end (for example "Next R at 63 Dr-Rego Park, toward Bay Ridge-95 St: 10 min, 22 min, 33 min"). The minutes count down every second.
- Direction comes from each live train's neighbouring stop along the line, so it works even when the feed does not list your exit stop yet.
- Bus rides keep following the nearest bus as before.

**How to verify**
- Unit tests for the new `boardingArrivals` in `src/features/transit/model.test.ts` (right direction kept, wrong direction and departed trains dropped, same-station trips ignored).
- Checked in a local preview against live data: the R at 63 Dr-Rego Park showed three times toward Bay Ridge-95 St.

**Unchanged:** the notices list, "Other ways" and "Follow a train" buttons; picking a line from More layers shows no boarding row.
