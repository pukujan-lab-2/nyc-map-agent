At the demo, someone on an older, small iPhone spoke to the app in her own language and it heard nonsense. On phones the app used the browser's speech recognizer, which only listens in one language: the app's. Our language detection ran on the text afterwards, too late.

## What changes
- **Phones send voice to our server.** That's a touch screen whose small side is under 600 px. Each turn is recorded and transcribed by Whisper on the server, the path Brave already used. Older iPhone recognizers are weak, so this helps even in English.
- **Language detection when none is picked.** The app sends `lang=auto`, and Whisper reports the language it heard.
- **The app answers in that language.** The question goes to the agent with that language, the app's text switches to it, and the spoken answer uses a voice for it.
- **A language picked in Settings still wins.** It is sent as the hint, as before.
- **Desktop is unchanged.**

## How to verify
- Unit tests:
  - `server/features/agent/stt.test.ts` covers auto mode and the language names.
  - Voice and agent tests pass (115 in total).
- Type check and lint are clean.
- After deploy: post a short Spanish clip to `/api/stt` with `lang=auto`. It should return `"lang": "es"`.
- On a phone with no language picked, tap the mic and speak Spanish. The reply should come back in Spanish, written and spoken.

## What stays the same
- Audio is still sent once and never stored.
- Rate limits are the same.
- Dictation into the text box works as before.
