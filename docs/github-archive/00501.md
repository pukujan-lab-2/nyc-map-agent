**Problem:** a picked line drew every path it has ever been scheduled on. The F showed both the 53 St and the 63 St tunnels at once, so a rider could not tell which way trains run today. The same is true for any line with branches or reroutes (the R, the Q).

**What changes**
- A new pure helper, `src/features/transit/liveBranches.ts`, reads the live trips: which station follows which.
- Track between two stations a train runs between is drawn bold. The rest of the line is faded.
- A run that is not on the line's own track (the R via 6 Av and 53 St today) is drawn dashed, with a white casing, along the track it is really on.
- Express runs keep the track past the stations they skip. Timing points such as R60 are not counted as stations. Short tail track past a terminal stays with its neighbour; a long one (the R's 2nd Ave run) fades.
- With no trains on the line (late night, a feed gap), the line is drawn as before.

**How to verify**
- Pick the F: the 53 St tunnel is bold, while Lexington Av/63 St, Roosevelt Island and 21 St-Queensbridge are faded.
- Pick the R: 2nd Ave is faded and the 6 Av/53 St detour is dashed.
- Unit tests in `liveBranches.test.ts` cover a branch in use versus faded, a branch past the last stop, an express run, a timing point, a detour onto another line's track, and no trains.

**What stays unchanged:** the map with no line picked, the line panel and its notices, and train bullets.

**Size:** transit layers budget 23.8 → 24.7 kB and all JS 1075 → 1076 kB, in their own commits.

Known limit: where a line's static shapes split one corridor into two tracks (the F between 36 St and Northern Blvd), both stay bold, since both are the trains' corridor.
