# PR #336: fix(transit): thicken the underground dash when X-ray is off

- State: closed (2026-10-25)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-23
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/pull/336
- Branch `fix/underground-dash-width` into `main`, +2 −2 in 1 files

With X-ray off, an underground subway line is a faint dash. A tunnel is easy to lose on the map, even though a dashed line is the right way to show that the track is not on the street.

The X-ray-off underground dash is now about as wide as the solid street-level stroke: 1.5 at map zoom 10, growing to 4 at zoom 16. Opacity stays 0.45, and the dash pattern stays 2, 2, so a tunnel still does not look like elevated track. Those widths are paint on the underground line layer, not a shared constant, so this change does not add a unit test.

## How to verify

- Leave X-ray off and follow a known tunnel, such as the A train near Newtown Creek or the Fulton Street tunnel in East New York. The dash should be about as wide as the solid line beside it, and still dashed.
- Turn X-ray on. The same tunnel should still be a solid glowing run (width 2 at zoom 10 to 6 at zoom 16, opacity 0.95, with the existing blur).
- Street-level and elevated lines stay solid.
- The amber planned-work line stays its own overlay.

## What stays unchanged

- The X-ray-on underground run stays a solid glow.
- Street-level and elevated line paint stays solid.
- The amber work overlay is unchanged.
- The 945 kB script bundle cap is unchanged.

Next: after this is on the live map, look at the A and G dashes near Newtown Creek with X-ray off and confirm they can be followed by eye.

Closes #331
