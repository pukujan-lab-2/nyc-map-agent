A compact 'Height' range in the settings drawer's View section, from 1.7 m (the street-level walk) up to 3 km overhead, log scale, with the current height shown next to it (aria-valuetext too, no tooltip).
- Below 2.5 m it enters street level; above, it leaves the walk and places the camera that high behind the current centre, looking at it with `calculateCameraOptionsFromTo` (the same MapLibre 5 API the eye camera uses). Tilt eases from 80° near the street to straight down at the top.
- The map reports its real camera altitude on moveend, so the slider follows zoom/tilt changes made elsewhere.
- Pure mapping in src/lib/map/cameraHeight.ts with tests.

Headless 1440: slider at 35 → camera 23.3 m, pitch 52°; at 0 → street level, 1.7 m, pitch 86°. Screenshot /workspace/nyc-map-qa/fix4-height-1440-35.png.
