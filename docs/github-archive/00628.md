Asked about the A train, the live chat told a rider to change trains at stops the A skips, even though its line status tool returned correct data.

## Human outcome

A rider who asks "Is the A running near Times Square?" hears what is wrong and where, and is only sent to stations where they can change trains.

## Before and after

**Before:** the tool said the Inwood-bound A skips High St, Fulton St, Chambers St, Canal St and Spring St, and listed the lines that still stop at each under `otherLinesAtSkippedStations`. The chat answered "switch at High St or Fulton St/Chambers St/Canal St". It also said the delays were "in both directions at Times Square" (the MTA text says downtown trains, throughout Manhattan) and ended with "Want to start?" with no trip planned.

**After:** that key is `otherLinesThatStillStopAtSkippedStations`, and the prompt tells the model four things: keep a delay's direction and place as the text gives them, never send anyone to change trains or board at a skipped stop, say whether the trouble touches a station the rider named, and not end a status answer with "Want to start?" unless a trip was planned.
