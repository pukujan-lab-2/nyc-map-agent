# PR #446: fix(ui): map credit and long task labels stop overlapping on phones

On a 390 px phone, two controls still collided (#9). The map credit's (i) button overlapped the top-right corner of the ask bar, and in Spanish "Planear viaje" and "Buscar salida" ran to the edges of their buttons.

## What changes
- **Map credit:** on narrow screens it sits 8 px above the home dock. The position uses the height the home already publishes (`--chat-bar-h`). Before, the offset was a fixed 64 px, which stopped clearing the dock once the task buttons were added. When the dock is hidden, the credit drops to the bottom of the screen.
- **Task buttons:** labels can wrap to two lines and get 4 px of side padding, so long translations stay inside the button.

## How to verify
- Open the site at 390 × 844 in Spanish. The (i) sits above the four task buttons, and every label fits inside its button.
- I ran an overlap check on the live site at 390 px. The home screen has no other overlapping buttons.

## Unchanged
- Desktop layout: both rules apply only below 768 px, or below the `lg` breakpoint for the buttons.
