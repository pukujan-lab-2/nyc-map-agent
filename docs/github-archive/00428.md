Walking answers gave street turns only, so a newcomer heard "turn left onto W 42nd St" with nothing to look for. The demo video says "along 42nd St, on your left, next to the library".

**What changes**
- Every walk the agent receives (`route_walk`, the restroom walk, the walk from a station exit) now carries `passes`: up to three guide landmarks within 60 m of the line, in the order you reach them, each with `left` or `right`.
- A turn within 50 m of a landmark reads "Turn left onto W 42nd St (by Bryant Park)".
- The prompt tells the agent to use `passes` for "on your left, next to …", and still never to invent a landmark.

**How to verify**
`pnpm vitest run server/features/agent/landmarksOnWay.test.ts`: a walk west on 42nd St puts the library on the left and the Grace Building on the right, and skips the Empire State Building, which is too far.

**What stays the same**
The map, the trip card and the route itself. Only the text the model reads changes.
