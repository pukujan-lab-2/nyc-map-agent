# Issue #224: Panel slot: one owner for the drawer and details sheets

- State: closed (2026-10-22)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-22
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/nyc-map-agent/issues/224

Build step 2 of the approved UI plan: one primary panel slot, so the Layers & filters drawer and the details sheets stop fighting over the screen.

Today `layersOpen` lives in MapPanel.tsx and nothing else knows about it. On phone the details sheet (z40) and the drawer (z50) are two bottom sheets that can be open together. An empty-map tap never closes the drawer, and Esc and Back don't close panels.

What this changes:
- Drawer open state moves into `PanelContext`, which becomes the one owner of the slot.
- Opening any details sheet (trains, buses, stations, entrances from TransitLayer; restrooms, places, schools, neighborhoods from MapView) closes the drawer.
- A minimized sheet becomes a labelled peek chip that restores on tap.
- The phone drawer is non-modal, so the map behind it stays usable.
- An empty-map tap closes the drawer and the sheet.
- Esc and browser Back close the top panel first, then stop the active mode (Follow now; Navigate registers the same way when #189 lands).
- With Follow or Navigate active, the phone drawer stops short of the top HUD and the sheet drops to peek.

Done when unit and Playwright tests cover: no overlap at 390px, a vehicle tap closes the drawer, an empty tap closes everything, and the Esc/Back order.
