In Live View, someone walking with the camera up could see which way to turn, but nothing showed where they were going. Now a pin stands at the end of the walking route, drawn over the camera picture. This is the last open piece of #192.

**What changes**
- `layers.ts` adds a destination source and a symbol layer that uses a teardrop pin in the route colour. The pin is anchored at its tip, stands upright toward the viewer, and grows as the map zooms in.
- `updateNavLayers` places the pin at the route's last point when the frame asks for it (`destPin: true`). Otherwise it clears the pin, so the 2D walking map is unchanged.
- `ArOverlay` asks for the pin in both of its update calls.

**How to verify**
- `pnpm vitest run src/features/liveview` passes all 21 tests, including the 2 new ones. They check that the pin sits on the route's last coordinate and is cleared when the frame doesn't ask for it.
- Typecheck and lint are clean.
- On a phone: walk to a restroom, open Live View (Beta), and turn toward the destination. The pin appears where the route ends.

**Caveat:** in a headless browser the overlay map doesn't draw over Chrome's fake camera at all. The existing route line is missing too, so screenshots couldn't show the pin. The proof here is the unit tests, and the real check is on a phone.

**What stays the same:** the route line, the turn arrow, the camera permission flow and the home walking card.
