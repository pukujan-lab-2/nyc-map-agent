### Summary

Apple Maps shows real street photos (Look Around), including the entrance to Grand Av–Newtown. Our street view is a 3D model, so entrances show as labels only. The owner's call: keep photos to destinations and entrances, not every street. Restrooms and landmarks already have photos; whether the Mapillary photos show on the live site is unchecked.

### Identity and Lineage

- Leaf issue: this one
- Parent: none
- Task/branch: none yet

### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] It is confirmed whether the Mapillary key is set on the live site (proposed)
- [ ] Entrance and exit cards show the nearest photo that faces the entrance, credited and dated (proposed)
- [ ] A trip's or answer's destination shows a photo the same way (proposed)

### Boundaries and Next Action

Out of scope: photos for every street (Look Around-style browsing); business suggestions the rider didn't ask for.
Next action: check the live site for the Mapillary key, then sample entrance coverage (H1).

### Symptoms

1. **Entrances have no real photo.** Street view is a 3D model; an entrance is a label and an exit name.
2. **Photos exist elsewhere.** Restroom cards show the nearest Mapillary street photo (`src/components/RestroomPhoto.tsx`); landmarks carry Wikimedia Commons pictures (`src/lib/guide/landmarks.json`); the Street View sheet finds a Mapillary photo for a block.
3. **Live status unchecked.** Mapillary photos only appear when its access key is set (`hasMapillaryToken`).

### Hypotheses (unverified)

- (hypothesis) H1: Mapillary has a photo facing most NYC subway entrances.
  Experiment to confirm: query the nearest image for 50 entrances and check by eye how many show the entrance. Refuted if most have no nearby photo or face away.

### Counter-signal

Mapillary coverage is crowd-sourced and uneven; photos may be old.

### Honest Caveat

Coverage and the live key are both unknown. A photo of the wrong door is worse than none.

### Problems vs Gaps

- Problem (evidenced): entrances and destinations have no real photo.
- Gap (untested): coverage (H1) and whether the key is live.
