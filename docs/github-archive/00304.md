Stop following a subway leaves the street x-ray veil up, and the M sits on the pavement at Middle Village–Metropolitan Avenue.

Riding an underground train turns the veil on so the train stays visible. Pressing Stop following, dragging the map, or pressing Esc left that veil up. The open-air Myrtle Avenue viaduct from the terminal toward Fresh Pond Road is tagged at street level for about the first 650 m, so trains there sit on the street.

X-ray now matches the track under the train you boarded. Underground turns the veil on, open track turns it off, and stepping off turns it off. A manual X-ray choice stays when nobody is being followed. The Middle Village stretch is drawn as an 8 m viaduct, the same height as the rest of the elevated M.

To verify: follow an underground train and press Stop following. The X-ray button turns off. Follow an M through Middle Village and it rides the viaduct with the veil down. The rest of the Myrtle elevated line was already elevated. The Queens Boulevard tunnel stays underground. Station interiors stay as they are.
