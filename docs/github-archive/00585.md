**Problem.** In street view, a restroom answer with two or more spots still pulled the camera off eye level. On the live site at 800×1000 the zoom dropped from 23 to 16. [PR #584](https://github.com/Pukujan/nyc-map-agent/pull/584) stopped the answer's own framing, but `showPoints` in `tripLayer.ts` moves the camera by itself: it flies to a single pin and fits several from above.

**What changes.** `showPoints` still draws the pins in street view but leaves the camera alone. This covers every answer that drops pins: restrooms, stops, crashes and trouble spots. The mini map still shows where they are.

**How to verify.** The tablet street-view test now uses two restrooms and checks that the zoom stays within 0.5 of eye level, in addition to the pitch, the pad and mini map placement, and the layout check.

**Unchanged.** The overhead map and 3D views frame pins as before.
