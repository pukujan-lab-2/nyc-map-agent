Live buses only moved when a new GPS fix arrived (about every 30 s), so they sat still and then jumped. This snaps each bus to its route shape and estimates motion between reports.

**What changes**
- New `src/features/transit/busEstimate.ts` (pure, unit-tested): tracks distance along the route shape (Bus Time polylines from `/api/transit/bus-route`, both orientations; bearing picks direction).
- Between reports: dead-reckons along the shape at a cruise speed from the last two reports (clamped 1.5–15 m/s, blended 60/40 with a route default: local 5.2, SBS 6.5, express 9 m/s), braking into each upcoming stop on the shape and dwelling 16 s.
- New report: no teleport. Display speed = estimated speed + error/4 s, never negative, so it catches up or holds; more than 150 m ahead (or 600 m behind) fades out, jumps, fades in. Shape switch also fades.
- Report older than 2 min: freezes and fades to 25 %.
- `TransitLayer`: bus shapes are fetched for routes near the view (shared react-query cache with the selected-route overlay), bus frames come from the estimator; bus badge layers multiply opacity by a new `alpha` property. Buses with no shape yet fall back to the old tween. Train code paths are untouched.

**Verify**: `pnpm test src/features/transit/busEstimate.test.ts` (12 tests); on the live map, buses glide continuously.

**Unchanged**: trains, server, bundle deps (no new packages).

Closes #256. Learned speeds follow in a second PR (Refs #257).

**Lane and heading (from a 3D report of a bus sitting across the sidewalk)**
- `/api/transit/bus-route` now snaps each shape vertex to the nearest parallel NYC street centerline (DCP CSCL, Open Data inkn-q76z, within 18 m and 35°) and returns a per-vertex right-hand lane offset (two-way: a quarter of street width; one-way: curb lane). M15: 617 of 637 vertices snapped, ~3.4 s once per 6 h cache.
- The estimator draws the bus that far right of the centerline, and the heading is the shape tangent in the direction of travel (chord over ±10 m), slerped through turns (τ 0.5 s). GPS bearing is used only to pick the shape orientation.
- Model forward axis: `vehicleParts` already rotates the glTF so its long axis is +Y (north at bearing 0) and the instance is rotated by -bearing, so no extra 90° offset is needed; checked live in 3D after deploy.
