**Problem.** After switching on Live buses (or entering street view) near 5 Av / 53 St, the app chose the routes near you (M1, M2, M3, M4, SIM10, SIM1C) and then drew every bus on those routes anywhere on screen. Zoomed out, M and SIM buses covered Manhattan and Queens. The per-line cap was only applied to the position estimator, not to what got drawn. Separately, the only way to choose a route that isn't nearby was to type it.

**What changes.**
1. Buses are now either **in focus** or **out of focus**:
   - In focus: the bus you follow, and routes you picked (the picker, a bus stop's "watch", a trip, a replacement shuttle). These show up to the buses-per-line cap, nearest first.
   - Out of focus: routes that only came from a "buses near you" lookup (the Live buses switch on the home screen, street view). The URL marks them with `?busNear=1`, and only their buses within 1.2 km of you show. "You" means your shared location, then where you stand in street view, then the map centre.
   - While you follow a bus, every other bus is out of focus and shows only near the bus you follow. When you stop following, the earlier rule applies again.
   - Following never added routes (checked: nothing calls `setBusLines` on follow). The citywide spread came from the missing draw filter above.
2. Route picker under Live buses: an **All routes** row of groups (M, Bx, B, Q, S, then express QM, BM, BxM, X, SIM). Tap a group to see its routes as chips in number order (Q1 … Q113), and tap any number of them on or off. You can still type a route. The 20-route limit is explained when you reach it.

**How to verify.** Open https://nyc.design-bakery.com at 5 Av / 53 St, switch on Live buses and zoom out: only buses near the centre stay. In Layers → Live buses → Change, tap Q and then several Q routes: each one shows, capped per line.

**Unchanged.** Trains, trip and shuttle precedence, the buses-per-line setting, and the URL for picked lines. Old links that have `busLines` but no `busNear` count as picked, so they show up to 3 buses per line.
