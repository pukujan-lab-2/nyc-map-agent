## In flight

**A subway notice now guides the rider instead of repeating the MTA paragraph (#55, pull request #61, branch `feat/55-alert-diagram`).** Tapping a notice in the line panel draws the stretch of line it touches and reads short steps aloud while the map frames each step's stations: what is happening, where to leave the train, the street corner the shuttle bus stops at (from the feed's per-station notes), where to get back on, bus alternatives from the travel tips, and when it applies. With the rider's location shared it starts from the nearest closed station. Steps are translated into the app language by the agent's model through `POST /api/agent/translate`, with station names kept as on the map. Other ways is gone. Not done: nobody has listened to it on a phone.

**Street view stopped at the pavement: you could not walk into a station (#62, merged as pull request #63).** Each station now gets a walkable model on first approach (`src/lib/map/stationWalk.ts`), built from the x-ray data and a new `public/transit/elevated.json` (`pnpm build:elevated`, from OpenStreetMap). Walking onto a street stair takes you down to the platforms, drawn on their own canvas because MapLibre won't put the camera under the ground; at an elevated station the stairs take you up and the map camera rises to the platform, 10 m over the city. Walls and platform edges hold you in. Since #69 (task NMA-0030), a step onto a stair within about 70 degrees of its line runs down the flight, so walking up to an entrance takes you in instead of across it: most entrances now let you in from 5 or 6 of 16 headings, where before most allowed one or two. Still closed: 148 of 1062 street-stair entrances, 78 of them with no mapped stair within 30 m. Not done: 13 of 274 underground and 7 of 155 elevated stations still have a platform you cannot reach, OpenStreetMap maps street stairs at only 21 elevated stations (the rest get one added at the entrance), and nobody has walked it on a phone.

**A phone shows one bottom sheet at a time (#53, branch `fix/53-one-phone-sheet`).** Opening a station and tapping one of its line chips used to stack the line sheet on the station sheet, each with its own handle. Now every phone sheet built on `PullSheet` joins one stack (`src/lib/store/pullSheet.ts`): only the newest draws, a Back arrow returns to the one under it, and Close line empties the sheet. The station sheet moved off the old vaul drawer onto `PullSheet`, so it sits above the chat bar. Not done: nobody has dragged it on a real phone yet.

**The model switch and the spoken answers merged on 2026-11-02 and 2026-11-03 (#35, pull request #36; #37, pull request #38).** Settings has a Model choice (Auto, Smart or Dev), stored on the phone and sent with each question. Smart tries DeepSeek on InferHub, Qwen, WorkBuddy and a free OpenRouter model through the Octo vault relay, and a watcher probes each one every five minutes with a real tool call and puts failing ones last; Dev is the LFM3 path. The four `SMART_*_KEY` working keys are in the VPS env (`~/apps/nyc-map-agent.env`), so Auto means Smart in production once that build deploys; the watcher renews them daily. Answers and speech now spell out station shorthand ("Times Square-42 Street"), and `POST /api/tts` reads English, Spanish, French and Chinese answers with Kokoro from the copilot speech server on the MacBook Pro; other languages and any failure use the browser voice. Not done: if all four smart providers fail the rider gets the error (Dev is not a last step), a cold Kokoro can leave up to 20 s of silence before the browser voice speaks, the trip suite (`docs/eval/trips/`, 41.2% on LFM3, #23) has not been run on both profiles, and nobody has listened to the voice on a phone.

**The trip preview showed the walk into a station in x-ray, with no way to the train and no train (#65, task NMA-0028, branch `feat/trip-station-guide`).** Where a station has a walkable model (the one street view uses since #63), the preview now walks from the entrance to the platform at eye level with the walls drawn and no x-ray. The route comes from `src/lib/map/stationRoute.ts`, a search over the model's floor cells that keeps off the walls. It finds a way at 236 of 246 underground stations. On a phone the preview bar now gives the step its own line, and the compass, view chips and "I'm in the station" sit above the bar.
