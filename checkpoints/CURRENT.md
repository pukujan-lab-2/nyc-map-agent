## In flight

**Trips sent riders through entrances the MTA had closed, and someone who can only use elevators had no way to say so (#133, branch `feat/133-step-free-closures`).** On 2026-11-12 the MTA said the Madison Av entrance to 5 Av/53 St takes no one in, and the elevator from the Fulton Center concourse to the downtown 4/5 platform was out. The trip planner now reads the subway alerts feed's station notices, places each one on an entrance by street name (the city's street centreline), corner or end of the station, and picks another entrance or exit with a line saying why. Settings has an Elevators only switch, kept on the phone: with it on, trips ask OpenTripPlanner for wheelchair routing, use elevator entrances, rank trips through stations with no working elevator last and say which station blocks them, and the preview rides the elevator instead of walking the stairs. Not covered: closed passages inside a station model, since no feed places a closure inside one.

**Someone lost in a station with no signal got three confusing buttons, so they are now one where-am-I helper (#131, branch `feat/131-lost-helper`, task NMA-0056).** Find exit on the home, Find exit on a followed train's card (it said Exit help) and Where am I on a walk (it said I'm lost) all open it. Typing goes through the chat bar, so text comes first; the thread shows in the helper, and questions go to the agent with the guess attached as a guess.
